# Backlash Prep — Be Ready for the Worst

Faith-based products draw fire from multiple sides. This doc helps you respond calmly and point to existing policies.

---

## Anticipated Critiques & Responses

### "Too Christian" / "Propaganda"

**Who:** Atheists, agnostics, ex-Christians (Reddit, etc.)

**Typical claims:** "KJV is outdated/patriarchal." "Why push religion?" "This is indoctrination."

**Response (short):** "We're a Bible companion for people who already want Scripture. No one's forced here. If it's not for you, that's fine."

**Response (longer):** "Today's Daily Battle is built for people who want a daily verse habit—anxiety, hope, grief, strength. We use KJV because it's public domain and widely recognized. We're not evangelizing; we're serving people who are already looking. If that's not you, no hard feelings."

**Point to:** [About](https://todaysdailybattle.com/about.html) — "No preaching. Just survival."

---

### "Not Christian Enough" / "Watered Down"

**Who:** Hardline evangelicals, KJV-only purists

**Typical claims:** "No altar call?" "No repentance/hell?" "Adding context is commentary—stick to text!"

**Response (short):** "We're a daily verse and prayer companion, not a church. We meet people where they are—anxiety, hope, grief—and point to Scripture. Theology and church practice are for pastors and congregations."

**Response (longer):** "We're built for daily habit and encouragement—one verse, one moment. We're not replacing church, confession, or discipleship. We're a tool. Plain-meaning breakdowns help people understand; we're not adding doctrine, just clarity. If you want deeper theology, we have God Tier mode and pastor tools."

**Point to:** [FAQ — Which Bible version?](https://todaysdailybattle.com/faq.html) — KJV. [Terms — Disclaimer](https://todaysdailybattle.com/terms.html) — "Scripture and tools are for encouragement and study only; they are not... professional spiritual or mental health advice."

---

### "Progressive / Exclusive" (LGBTQ+, Social Justice)

**Who:** Progressive Christians, OpenChristian

**Typical claims:** "No queer-friendly verses?" "Where's social justice?" "Conservative/exclusive."

**Response (short):** "We search by topic—hope, fear, anxiety, grief, strength, love. We don't curate by politics or identity. Anyone can search and find what speaks to them."

**Response (longer):** "Our topics are emotion- and life-situation based: anxiety, hope, grief, love, forgiveness, strength. We don't filter by theology or identity. If a verse speaks to you, it's there. We're a tool, not a statement."

**Point to:** [Privacy](https://todaysdailybattle.com/privacy.html) — "User safety is the key; this is a safe place."

---

### Tech / Privacy Nerds

**Who:** Security researchers, privacy advocates

**Typical claims:** "LocalStorage for prayers? Hackers could read it." "PWA = spyware?"

**Response (short):** "Prayers save locally by default—we never see them. Sync is opt-in. We use HSTS, CSP, SRI, and no secrets in the client. See SECURITY.md and privacy.html."

**Response (longer):** "Prayers and notes stay on your device unless you sign in and sync. We use industry-standard security (HSTS, CSP, SRI). The anon key is public by design; RLS enforces access. No service role or secrets in the client. PWA = app-like experience, no tracking beyond anonymous analytics. Full details in our security docs."

**Point to:** [Privacy](https://todaysdailybattle.com/privacy.html), [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md), [PRIVACY-ANALYTICS.md](PRIVACY-ANALYTICS.md)

---

### Monetization Haters

**Who:** "You're selling God" crowd

**Typical claims:** "Battle Pro? You're monetizing faith." "Paywall on Scripture?"

**Response (short):** "Core features are free—search, daily verse, streaks, prayer list, message board. Battle Pro adds offline PDFs, Wins Report, premium devotionals. Same model as study Bibles and devotional apps."

**Response (longer):** "Search, Verse of the Day, 7-day plan, chapter reader, notes, message board, kids activities, and Battle of the day are free. Always. Battle Pro ($9.99/mo) adds offline downloads, Wins Report, and premium devotionals. We're not selling Scripture—we're selling tools and convenience. Devotionals and study Bibles have cost money for centuries."

**Point to:** [FAQ — Is this free?](https://todaysdailybattle.com/faq.html), [Pricing](https://todaysdailybattle.com/pricing.html)

## If It Goes Public (Reddit, Twitter, etc.)

1. **Don't argue.** Acknowledge, point to docs, move on.
2. **One reply max.** "Thanks for the feedback. Here's how we handle that: [link]. If you have specific concerns, support@todaysdailybattle.com."
3. **Don't defend theology.** "We're a tool. Theology is for pastors and churches."
4. **Don't apologize for existing.** You built something useful. Stand by it.

## Contact for Escalation

- **Legal:** Have a lawyer on retainer or know who to call if you get a demand letter.
- **Hosting:** Cloudflare + Supabase — both have abuse/legal processes. Know how to report and respond.
- **Stripe:** Chargebacks happen. Refund policy is in Terms; stick to it.
