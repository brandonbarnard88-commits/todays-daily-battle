# Product roadmap ideas (2026)

Drawn from user needs and trends in daily Bible/devotional apps. Use this to prioritize what to build next.

---

## 1. Deeper personalization

- **Custom daily battle plans** — Themed sequences (e.g. "Battle Anxiety in 30 Days") that auto-deliver verses; mood/trigger prompts ("Struggling with sleep? Start here").
- **Verse & prayer matching** — Suggest verses/prayers from Prayer List items or past topics (simple rules or future AI).
- **Profile preferences** (post-sync) — Preferred translation (beyond KJV), audio speed/voice, email reminder time, default theme (e.g. Gratitude mornings).

---

## 2. Stronger accountability & motivation

- **Expanded gamification** — More badges (e.g. "Wisdom Warrior" for James 1:5), streak freezes (Pro: more than 1/month), anonymous leaderboards (e.g. longest family streak).
- **Progress reports & Wins** — Tease 2026 Wins Report with mockups: "You prayed through 180 battles," "Verses on Hope helped most," shareable graphics.
- **Gentle nudges** — "Missed yesterday? Here's a quick reset verse" / "Your streak is on fire—keep it going!"

---

## 3. Multimedia & variety

- **Short audio/video devotionals** — 1–3 min narrated reflections or worship tied to the verse (post–March walkthrough).
- **Visual enhancements** — More verse image templates (serene backgrounds, lock-screen widgets, wallpapers).
- **Memory verse mode** — Spaced repetition, fill-in-the-blank, audio playback for memorization.

---

## 4. Community & connection

- **Prayer Wall** — "Top Prayed" daily (anonymous), private groups (family/small group lists for Church tier), "Pray Together" live counters.
- **Stories of Hope feed** — Curated, moderated feed of opt-in testimonies.
- **Battle Buddies** — Opt-in streak/prayer accountability partners (moderated).

---

## 5. Offline & convenience

- **Full offline (Battle Pro)** — Auto-download daily verse + buffer, offline notes, Prayer List, custom plans.
- **Multi-device sync** — Already in progress; prioritize post-accounts.
- **Integrations** — Export to calendar, note apps, church tools.

---

## 6. Tiered value (convert free → Pro)

- Highlight "Free is enough to build the habit" + preview Pro (sample PDF export, custom plan teaser).
- Early perks (first 50, 1 month free) — promote via email and social.
- **"Why Upgrade?"** — User scenarios: "Tired of re-typing prayers on new devices? Sync with Pro."

---

**Suggested order:** Launch sync + Battle Pro with early perks (done/planned) → Wins Report mockups + "Why Upgrade?" → gentle nudges + streak freezes (Pro) → then audio/visual and personalization as capacity allows.
