# Roadmap Playbook — Make It "My Go-To"

**Goal:** Everyone thinks "this is my go-to," not "another Bible thing." Quiet, no ads, real focus on daily habit. Short, actionable.

---

## 1. Nail the First 5 Seconds (Hook 'Em Fast)

- **Push "Add to Home Screen" harder** — make it pop like a real app. Right now it's buried.
- Add a **big floating button on load**: "Feels like an app? Tap here." PWA magic so it lives on their phone, no store download.
- Reference: minimal cards, soft colors, streak right up front (e.g. Lemon8, clean app store examples). PWA add-to-home prompts (e.g. Gomage 2025 guide) — make ours that obvious.

---

## 2. Make It Personal, Not Preachy

- **AI-lite:** "How're you feeling today?" (anxious? angry? grateful?) → one verse + 30-sec prayer tailored. No full chat — just smart suggestions.
- People want the nudge, not lectures. YouVersion vibe (mood-based search + notifications) without the bloat.

---

## 3. Streak + Community Without Drama

- **30-Day Battle is gold** — lean in.
- **Wins Report now** (don’t wait for Pro): e.g. weekly email "You prayed 5 days straight — here's your verse."
- **Prayer Wall:** Let folks "heart" anonymously + add private notes. No comments — keeps it safe.
- **"Pray with me" streak:** Two people link up, see each other's count, no names.

---

## 4. Offline & Audio First

- **Launch Battle Pro early** — offline verses, audio on tap (not new tab). Voice read-aloud, calm tones.
- Commute, gym — make it pocket-ready.

---

## 5. Stand Out: Tiny Wins, Big Feels

- **Widget:** Daily verse on home screen — no open app needed.
- **Battle Buddy:** Random match for accountability — swap one prayer a week.
- **Gamify light:** Earn "armor" badges for streaks (shield for patience, sword for courage).

---

## 6. Marketing Quiet

- No ads. Share card with verse + "Join my streak?" Tag #DailyBattle.
- Partner pastors — give 'em free toolkit.
