# Möbius Loop — 10-Minute Guided Meditation Script

**Total duration:** ~10 minutes  
**Tone:** Quiet friend at dawn. Warm, direct, no hype.  
**Pacing:** Slow. Pauses between phrases. Breathe with the listener.

---

## Intro (0:00–0:45)

*[Soft, calm]*

You're about to walk the loop. One ribbon. No beginning, no end. Fear and faith on the same path—twisted once, redeemed.

Sit still. Close your eyes if you like. Breathe slow.

*[Pause 3 seconds]*

"Enoch walked with God: and he was not; for God took him." Genesis 5:24. One step. Then another. Presence, not striving.

*[Pause 2 seconds]*

Let's begin.

---

## Mantra Pass 1 — Acknowledgment (1:00–3:30)

*[Read each line slowly. Pause 8–10 seconds between lines.]*

You're not ready.

*[Pause]*

But you're not alone.

*[Pause]*

Power. Love. Sound mind.

*[Pause]*

Let go.

*[Pause]*

You're stronger than you know.

*[Pause]*

Keep falling.

*[Pause]*

Deeper.

*[Pause]*

Still here.

---

## Breathwork Bridge (3:30–5:00)

*[Slower, more spacious]*

Inhale: "I walk…"

*[Pause 4 seconds]*

Exhale: "…with God."

*[Pause 4 seconds]*

Again. Inhale: "I walk…" Exhale: "…with God."

*[Pause 5 seconds]*

One ribbon. No separation. Fear turns… faith turns… presence remains.

*[Pause 3 seconds]*

---

## Mantra Pass 2 — Redemption (5:00–7:30)

*[Slightly warmer tone. Pause 8–10 seconds between lines.]*

Fear twists—faith holds.

*[Pause]*

The weight isn't crushing—it's building.

*[Pause]*

You were made for this.

*[Pause]*

Breathe.

*[Pause]*

You made it.

*[Pause]*

The loop continues.

*[Pause]*

---

## Scripture Anchor (7:30–8:30)

*[Clear, reverent]*

"For God hath not given us the spirit of fear; but of power, and of love, and of a sound mind." Second Timothy 1:7.

*[Pause 5 seconds]*

Say it with me, if you want. Power. Love. Sound mind.

*[Pause 3 seconds]*

---

## Closing (8:30–10:00)

*[Gentle, unhurried]*

You've walked the loop. Fear didn't disappear—it turned. It's on the same ribbon with faith now.

*[Pause 3 seconds]*

When you open your eyes, carry this: one journey, endless turns. You're already on the path.

*[Pause 2 seconds]*

Amen.

*[5–10 seconds of silence, then fade]*

---

## Recording Notes

- **Environment:** Quiet. No background noise.
- **Mic:** Close enough for intimacy; not so close it picks up breath pops.
- **Music (optional):** Very soft ambient pad or single sustained note. No melody. Fade in/out gently.
- **Format:** MP3 or WebM. Target ~1–2 MB for 10 min.
- **File:** Save as `mobius-guided-10min.mp3` in project root or `/audio/`.

---

## Alternate track filename (`mobius-guided-human.mp3`)

For the **Spoken narrator (alternate)** radio on Möbius, use the same script and pacing (~10 minutes). A condensed `say` input for regeneration lives at `scripts/mobius-guided-human-say-input.txt`; run **`npm run audio:mobius-guided`** (macOS) to rebuild the default system-voice MP3, or replace that file with a studio recording.
