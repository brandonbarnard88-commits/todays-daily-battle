# Homepage redundancy check

## Found and addressed

1. **Two "Today's Battle" buttons** – `daily-btn` (quick-links) and `daily-plan-btn` (30-Day section) do the same thing (run daily topic search). Removed the duplicate from 30-Day section; kept one in quick-links.

2. **"More topics" duplicated quick-actions** – Hope, Fear, Forgiveness, Grief, Anxiety appeared as both quick-action buttons and in the "More topics" line. Updated "More topics" to only link to topics not in the quick-actions (Strength, Parenting) so the line adds value instead of repeating.

3. **Study Tools in two places** – Hero has "Study Tools" and quick-links have "Build a Lesson" (same destination). Kept both: hero is primary nav, quick-links are contextual shortcuts. Optional future change: drop "Build a Lesson" from quick-links and rely on hero/sidebar.

## Left as-is (intentional)

- **Verse of the Day (hero)** vs **Featured Today's Battle (section)** – Different: verse.html is a page; the section is the on-page daily verse widget. Both stay.
- **Join the Battle Plan** vs **Join Free Newsletter** – Same action (newsletter), different framing. Kept both; consider one label later if desired.
- **How It Helps** vs **How It Works** – Different (benefits vs steps). Both add value; could merge into one section later.
- **Sidebar nav** vs **Quick links** – Sidebar is global nav; quick-links are in-context shortcuts. Both stay.
