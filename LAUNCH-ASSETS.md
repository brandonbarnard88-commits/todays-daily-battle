## 5. Stickiness checklist (things that make people come back)

- [ ] **Daily verse** — One verse, one moment; loads on homepage every day.
- [ ] **Streak** — Local/offline streak; repair via invite (friend signs up → both get 1 day back).
- [ ] **Counter alive** — "Total prayers" from Supabase RPC; "Join N warriors" feels communal.
- [ ] **Email reminders** — SMTP working; daily verse in inbox = habit lock.
- [ ] **Share** — "Copy my link" / tweet with streak or verse; low friction.
- [ ] **Anonymous prayer** — Tap ♥, no login; prayers count toward total and map.
- [ ] **Map** — Gold crosses, anonymous only; "others are praying" without exposing identity.

## 7. Support / FAQ bullets (add to FAQ or help page)

- **How do streaks work?** Your streak is the number of days in a row you've done today's battle (or verse). It saves locally; when you sign in, it can sync across devices. If you miss a day, share your invite link—when a friend signs up with it, you both get one streak repair.
- **Is my prayer public?** Quick-pray (♥) is anonymous. Only the count and anonymous dots on the map are shown—no names, no personal data. We never display who prayed what.
- **How do I cancel?** When subscriptions are live, cancel anytime from your account or by emailing support@todaysdailybattle.com. You keep access until the end of the billing period; no recurring charge after that.
- **How do I get help?** Use the [Contact](contact.html) page or email support@todaysdailybattle.com. We'll get back to you.
- **Why didn’t my confirmation email arrive?** Check spam/junk. If it’s not there, use “Resend” on the signup screen or try “Forgot password?” to reset and get in.
