About Brandon & Today’s Daily Battle

I’m Brandon. Born in Dallas, TX; shaped by a hospital stay in Martin, TN. Home is West Tennessee. That season left me hungry for plain KJV that met me where I was—no lectures, no performance, just open Bible. I built what I still need every day. Read the full Creator & Origin Story when you want the deep version.

I spent twelve days in a hospital last November with spine problems that haven’t fully resolved—I’m facing surgery now, caring for my kids and my mom, and building this between appointments. God put this site so persistently on my heart that I couldn’t not build it. The outward man is cracked. The work kept going anyway, with family and God’s help. “Though our outward man perish, yet the inward man is renewed day by day.” — 2 Corinthians 4:16

This porch continues the quiet work of my father, Evangelist Thomas W. Barnard, through Christian Messenger Service: one verse, one small step, one prayer at a time. No pedestal—I’m not a preacher; just a regular guy who needs the KJV the same way you do.

Reach me at support email.

Why this site exists

This is Today’s Daily Battle in a sentence: real life is the classroom, and rest in Christ is the only graduation that matters. No leaderboards, no streak to protect, no feed. Steady KJV for anxiety, grief, parenting, wonder, and ordinary Tuesdays. Brandon built it from his own need and still uses it. If one verse lands today, the day was enough.

Reading plans are simply called KJV Plans—quiet doors into Scripture for real days. Browse all plans →

This starts with where you are—or with a simple question, or just today’s verse. Then the Word. Then a prayer if you want one. Then one small step. The text is fixed KJV, not a chat. People meet you on Prayer if you want company.

Why we say “God’s University of Life”

It isn’t a rebrand. Today’s Daily Battle is still the name at the door. “God’s University of Life” is a gentle name for what this place has always been: a quiet campus where the textbook is the King James Bible, the Teacher is the Holy Spirit, and weary people learn His ways one verse at a time. No grades, no deadlines, no performance—just open doors for anxiety, parenting, grief, and ordinary days.

The address stays todaysdailybattle.com. Ad-free, private-first, offline-friendly, built solo with real families in mind. Walk the map anytime at five anchor verses.

Accessibility & calm use

Readable type, enough contrast in dark and light themes, visible keyboard focus, and clear labels. Decorative motion steps back when your device requests reduced motion. If anything blocks you—screen reader quirks, contrast, tap targets—please write us. We read every note.
