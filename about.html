Who's Behind This

Hi, I'm Brandon. I built Today's Daily Battle because I wanted a place where anyone could find Scripture by what they're feeling—anxiety, hope, grief, strength—without wading through a huge app. Real battles happen every day; God's Word meets us there. This site is my attempt to keep that simple: search by topic, build a lesson or sermon in minutes, and show up for the fight with something you can actually use. No fluff, no paywall on the core stuff—just tools that tie back to the verses that keep us grounded.

You can reach me at support@todaysdailybattle.com or through the Contact page.

Our Mission

We exist to be the Bible companion you reach for every day—instead of the feed. Less scroll, more soul. We help families, churches, and individuals meet Jesus in real-life battles with Scripture and practical tools.

Whether you need encouragement, a lesson starter, or a sermon outline, Today's Daily Battle keeps the experience simple, fast, and rooted in God’s Word—so hope is always within reach and the habit actually sticks.

Why This Site?

We're built to be the habit that replaces the scroll. Same two minutes you'd spend on social—but here you leave filled up. Search by what you're feeling, build a quick sermon or lesson, track your streak, and show up every day. Scripture becomes the anchor you actually use.
