How We Respond

We respond with clarity, humility, and accountability. We do not fuel outrage loops or argument theater.

Our stance

Today's Daily Battle is a daily Scripture and prayer companion. Core access remains free. Paid tiers add deeper discipline tools. We are a tool for support and formation, not a replacement for a local church or pastoral authority.

Common questions

Why KJV?

KJV is stable, public-domain Scripture that allows broad free access. We add plain-meaning breakdowns for clarity, not doctrinal replacement.

Is data secure?

By default, prayers stay local to your device. Optional sign-in enables sync with role-based controls. See Privacy for details.

Why paid tiers?

Core daily access stays free. Paid tiers fund infrastructure and unlock advanced workflows (offline, exports, team operations, deeper devotionals).

Feedback

We read every message. Contact us at support@todaysdailybattle.com or through Contact. If we miss the mark, we correct and improve.
