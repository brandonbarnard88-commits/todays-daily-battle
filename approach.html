Our stance

We're a daily verse and prayer companion. Search by what you're feeling—anxiety, hope, grief, strength. Core is free; Pro adds offline and Wins Report. We're a tool, not a church. Theology and discipleship are for pastors and congregations.

Common questions

Why KJV?

Public domain, widely recognized. We add plain-meaning breakdowns for clarity—not doctrine.

Is my data secure?

Prayers save on your device by default. We never see them unless you sign in and sync. Privacy

Why charge for Battle Pro?

Core features are free. Pro adds offline PDFs, Wins Report, premium devotionals. Same model as study Bibles.
