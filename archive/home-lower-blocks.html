Chain of Hope

When one hard week blends into the next, keep moving through a quiet chain instead of starting from scratch: fear to trust, grief to hope, then steadier praise—and when the sun comes out again, stay on the porch with thanks.

Fear → Faith When the what-ifs are loud and you need a first steady step. Worry → Trust When tomorrow keeps reaching into today. Grief → Hope When sorrow is real and you still need a place to stand. Hope → Praise When you are ready to end the week with thanks instead of noise. Praise & steady days When life feels kind and you want Scripture to hold the gladness—flexible length, same gentle pace.
