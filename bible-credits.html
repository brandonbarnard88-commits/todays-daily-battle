Bible credits

We want children and adults to learn the Word and draw near to Jesus — with clear labels and thankful credit.

Primary Scripture — King James Version (KJV)

Every main verse on Today’s Daily Battle is shown from the King James Version.

The KJV is in the public domain in the United States. We label it KJV so you always know what you are reading.

Credit: King James Version (public domain).

Optional simpler English — Bible in Basic English (BBE)

Where it helps children, new readers, or anyone who wants plainer words, we may offer a second line labeled Simpler English (BBE).

That text is from the Bible in Basic English, a whole-Bible translation that uses a limited, everyday vocabulary. It is offered as a bridge to understanding — not a replacement for the KJV, and never labeled as KJV.

The BBE was printed in 1965 by Cambridge University Press in England without a copyright notice and distributed in America; it is widely treated as public domain in the United States. We are thankful for that gift to the church.

Credit: Bible in Basic English (BBE) — public domain. Originally associated with work overseen by S. H. Hooke and published for plain-English readers; 1965 Cambridge printing treated as public domain in the U.S. We do not claim ownership of the BBE text.

Simple layman terms (our porch teaching)

Simple layman terms on this site are short teaching lines written for Today’s Daily Battle — plain meaning and gentle application, not a full alternate Bible translation. They sit under the KJV so the Word stays first.

How we use these together

  1. KJV first — the verse you can trust and share as Scripture here.
  2. Layman terms — a short unpacking for real life.
  3. Optional BBE — open only if you want simpler Bible wording (public domain).

Our hope is simple: that kids and adults understand the Word, meet Jesus, and take one faithful step — without confusion about sources, and with thanks to those who made free plain-English Scripture available.
