What's weighing on you?

Or type freely: lonely and scared

"Lord, I will both lay me down in peace, and sleep: for thou, LORD, only makest me dwell in safety."

Psalm 4:8 (KJV)

Real talk: The day's battles are done. Whatever weighed on you — anxiety, grief, anger, loneliness — hand it over. God's got the night watch. Let your mind rest in His promise, not your worries.
Quick step: Whisper or pray it aloud once. Write one thing you're thankful for today. Close your eyes and breathe: "Thou, LORD, makest me dwell in safety."

Heavenly Father, thank You for carrying me through this day. I lay down my cares at Your feet. Guard my sleep, quiet my thoughts, and let Psalm 4:8 be true in my heart tonight. Protect my family, heal what hurts, and wake me with Your mercy in the morning. In Jesus' name, Amen.
