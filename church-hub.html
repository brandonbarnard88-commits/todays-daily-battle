For pastors, small churches, and families

Pastor & Church Tools

Hey Pastor — you and your people are already welcome here. This is a calm porch of free KJV tools designed as a supplement, never a replacement, for the local church. One verse. One small step. Tools that work offline. No login required. No pressure to adopt everything.

Practical resources that help families stay in the Word between Sundays and give you ready material without adding to your load.

Biggest wins for churches

Here are the most practical on-ramps pastors and leaders tell us they use first. Take what serves your congregation — nothing is required.

Church Sharing Kit Copy-paste bulletin blurbs, VBS packs, small group outlines, QR codes, printable verse cards, and seasonal bundles. Ready in minutes. Open the Kit → Sermon Builder OIA workflow (Observation, Interpretation, Application) with live preview, supporting context from the Bible Tool, and PDF/handout export. Drafts stay on your device. Start Sermon Workspace → Embeddable Verse Widgets Drop a daily KJV verse + calm breakdown directly on your church website with one script tag. Customizable and privacy-first. Get Embed Code → Battle Plans for Groups Short (7–40 day) KJV rhythms for fear, grief, parenting, anxiety, and more. Perfect for midweek groups or one-on-one counseling. No streaks, just honest steps. Browse Plans → Family & Kids Tools Gentle family rhythms, Kids Corner stories, coloring, memory aids, and little-ones pages. Helps parents lead at the table without adding guilt. Family Hub →

Supplement, never replacement

We exist to help your people stay in the Word between Sundays and to give you practical, low-prep resources. The local church is the primary place of discipleship. This porch is simply a quiet side porch — free, private, offline-first, and built with reverence for the KJV. Take what serves. Ignore the rest. You and your congregation are already welcome here.
