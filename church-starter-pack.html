First hour workflow

When the week feels thin, run this order and stop there. It is enough for a faithful Sunday.

  1. Choose a text and draft in Sermon Builder; use One-Tap Export when ready.
  2. Pull church-ready blurbs, QR paths, and social copy from Church Sharing Kit.
  3. Assign next steps in Team Toolkit and keep volunteers aligned.
  4. Send families to one printable from Printables before Sunday closes.

Pastor reset + ready copy

Three practical helpers you can use this week without new setup: monthly reset, thin-week guide, and copy-ready church templates.

Monthly Pastor Reset (print-ready)

Use this once a month to keep preaching, people care, and rest from drifting. Calm rhythm, no complexity.

  • One sentence: what God is pressing on your heart this month.
  • Three sermon anchors (text + next faithful action).
  • Two people to encourage personally this week.
  • One non-negotiable rest block to guard.
  • One family follow-through habit after Sunday.

Thin Week Survival Guide (one page)

For weeks where you feel under water before you begin. Keep the minimum faithful path visible.

  1. Preach one clear burden, not five ideas competing.
  2. Call or text one weary member before polishing slides.
  3. Delegate one task that does not require your voice.
  4. Choose one family follow-through item and repeat it.
  5. Guard one rest block and treat it as ministry stewardship.
