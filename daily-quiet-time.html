Daily quiet time

A single doorway for the first minutes with God—no checklist pressure. Open what fits; skip what does not. Everything stays on your device until you choose to share or print.

A gentle order (not a rule)

  1. Verse of the Day — one steady anchor.
  2. Calm — when you need breath before you read anything else.
  3. 7-Day Peace — one plan day if you want rhythm (or Armor of God when the fight feels spiritual, Battle Distraction when the scroll wins).
  4. My Study — a line in your own words; optional print later.
  5. Prayer & message board — leave a request or read one for someone else. On Home, the prayer wall lives in the same spirit.

When you have a little more time

  • Chapter reader — stay in one Psalm or chapter.
  • Psalms hub — comfort, praise, and family Psalms plans.
  • All Battle Plans — pick a longer track when the morning grows.
