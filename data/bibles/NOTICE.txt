Today's Daily Battle — locale Bible NOTICE
==========================================

English on this site is the King James Version (public domain in the United States).
Language homes look up the same official UTC reference from the files below.
We flattened each text to a {"Book C:V": "text"} map for lookup. That is an
adaptation, not a new translation.
