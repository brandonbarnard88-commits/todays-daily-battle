# Another Dimension — master plan (site-wide)

**One goal:** everyone who lands here feels **welcomed**—rest, permission, grace first; KJV honored; handmade calm, not mass‑produced noise.

## Vision (short)

- Warm **porch** voice: friend at the door, second person, short breaths.
- **Grace** is the default air; not performance.
- **Alive** in small, optional delights; never busy.
- Two visual temperatures over time: **warm paper / sun‑wash** and **calm night study**—same porch, different hour.
