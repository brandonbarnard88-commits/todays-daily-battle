# Family & Kids Depth Principles

**Purpose:** Help the weary parent at the table find **one honest KJV moment with little ones** — fun-first for kids, rest-first for grown-ups — without turning the porch into a curriculum.

This lane deepens what already exists. It does not add performance pressure, new nav chrome, or “complete the journey” language.

These principles sit beside [PORCH-DISCOVERABILITY-PRINCIPLES.md](./PORCH-DISCOVERABILITY-PRINCIPLES.md), [PORCH-VISUAL-PRINCIPLES.md](./PORCH-VISUAL-PRINCIPLES.md), the four foundations ([NORTH-STAR-PRINCIPLES.md](./NORTH-STAR-PRINCIPLES.md)), and Color & Tell line-art rules ([COLOR-AND-TELL-HOUSE-STYLE.md](./COLOR-AND-TELL-HOUSE-STYLE.md)).

---

## North star (one sentence)

Little ones get **fun-first, short KJV**; parents get **one quiet door** when the day is long — same porch, same device, no account, no scoreboard.

---

## Decision filters (use these for every Family & Kids change)

### 1. Fun first for kids; rest first for parents

Kids copy stays bouncy and short (10-second loops, big eyes, tiny KJV ref). Parent bridges stay calm — never sermon tone on the Kids surface.

### 2. Scripture stays KJV-only and reverent

Full references where shown. No paraphrase. No AI-generated devotionals or stories.

### 3. Gentle Journey is a rhythm, not a checklist

`TDB_GENTLE_JOURNEY.CANONICAL_DISTINCT_STORY_GOAL` (365) is a **north star for distinct stories with the full calm package** — not a public counter, streak, or “days behind” message.

### 4. One primary door + optional `<details>`

Reuse `.tdb-porch-path-card` and existing Kids bands. Extras (Family Armor, `parentweary`, coloring) stay optional.

### 5. Feeling-first at the table

“Scared at bedtime?” or “Grown-up weary on the couch?” beats “Story Library index” or “Open curriculum.”

### 6. Offline-first after first visit

Read-alouds, coloring, and story cards must work when the signal drops — same as the rest of the site.

### 7. No performance language

No streak shame, no “you haven’t opened…”, no badge pressure on Kids home. Mercy-first progress stays local and private.

### 8. Mobile two-tap test (parent at the table)

From Kids Battle home or Family hub, can a tired parent reach **one read-aloud + one grown-up plan** in **two taps** without hunting?

### 9. Deepen; do not consolidate entry points

Kids Battle, Bible Story Library, Bible Loop Library, Family Armor, and porch read-alouds serve **different manners of access** — relocate or bridge; never remove.

---

## What we do not do in this lane

- Public “X of 365 complete” counters or journey dashboards
- New top-nav items or forced onboarding tours
- AI story or coloring generation as default
- Paraphrased or multi-translation kids content
- Consolidating “redundant” Kids tools
- Gamified parent dashboards that grade faithfulness

## Measure success by the table, not the catalog

Ask: *Did a weary parent and a little heart share one calm KJV moment tonight without feeling behind?*

Not: *Did they browse every Kids tool?*
