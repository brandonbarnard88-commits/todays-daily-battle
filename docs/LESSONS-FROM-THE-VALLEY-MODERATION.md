# Lessons from the Valley — moderation guidance (builder reference)

**North star:** Quiet markers from the valley — what the Lord showed someone there, anchored in His Word, for the day their turn comes. Not advice to skip suffering. Not life hacks.

## Default posture

- **Brandon-seeded markers** stay the heart for a long season. Add only when a valley has been walked and the Lord has shown something clear.
- **Reader submissions** are optional, slow, and off by default until explicitly opened.

## If reader submissions open someday

### What we accept

- One honest battle (one sentence)
- One KJV verse or short passage the Lord brought (full KJV text, correct reference)
- What He showed there (short, humble — no formulas, no “five steps”)
- One small way you walk differently now (pressure-free)
- A quiet prayer (KJV-toned, not performative)
- Optional link to an existing porch room (plan, deep lesson, Möbius station)

### What we decline (kindly)

- Prosperity, politics, debate, or culture-war framing
- “How to avoid” or “you should have known” tone
- AI-generated or generic devotional language
- Promotion, fundraising, or other-site links
- Advice that replaces Scripture or sounds like clinical/medical/legal counsel
- Long testimonies, trauma details that identify others, or shaming third parties

### Review checklist

1. **Scripture-first** — Is the KJV anchor accurate and central?
2. **Humble voice** — Does it sound like “the Lord showed me,” not “here’s how to fix it”?
3. **Porch tone** — Calm, honest, no hype, no streaks, no pressure
4. **Safety** — No identifying minors, no calls to harm, no harassment
5. **Privacy** — First name or anonymous only; edit identifying details
6. **Fit** — Pairs naturally with existing plans/lessons when cross-linked

### Publishing shape

- Same card template as seeded entries on `lessons-from-the-valley.html`
- Light edit for clarity and tone only — preserve the writer’s voice
- No dates, ranks, or “most popular”
- Group by battle theme, not chronology

### Relationship to other surfaces

| Surface | Role |
|--------|------|
| **Battle Stories** (`stories.html`) | Verse met me in a battle — moderated, anonymous |
| **Reader Stories** (`testimonials.html`) | Short notes about tools — curated |
| **What God Has Done** | Private journal — not public |
| **Valley markers** | What the Lord showed in the valley — Scripture-anchored companions |

Do not merge these into one feed. Each stays its own quiet room.

## Operational notes

- Use existing safety contact: `/contact.html?topic=safety`
- Never publish raw submission text without review
- When in doubt, wait — the porch does not hurry
