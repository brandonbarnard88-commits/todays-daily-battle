# Porch Discoverability Principles

**Purpose:** Keep this porch a quiet place, not a map of tools.

We do not help people “find every feature.” We help the weary heart that already landed here quietly find **one honest next door**.

These principles sit beside [PORCH-VISUAL-PRINCIPLES.md](./PORCH-VISUAL-PRINCIPLES.md) and the four foundations ([NORTH-STAR-PRINCIPLES.md](./NORTH-STAR-PRINCIPLES.md)). They add paths, not volume.

---

## Decision filters (use these for every signpost)

### 1. Scripture first, always

Signposts point to KJV truth or a calm path through it — never to tools for their own sake.

### 2. One honest line + one primary link

Never a wall of choices. One gentle invitation is enough.

### 3. Feeling-first language

“Anxious tonight?” or “Little ones at the table?” beats “Battle Plans” or “Kids Corner index.”

### 4. Every signpost answers one question

*If today feels like X, try Y.* Meet the real weight; offer one next gentle step.

### 5. Primary path visible; secondary in `<details>` only

Breathing room stays. No pressure to explore everything.

### 6. No performance language

No badges, no “you haven’t tried…”, no “complete this path.” The porch never grades the visitor.

### 7. Mobile-first, two-tap test

From Home, can a tired parent reach a relevant plan or Kids read-aloud in **two taps** without scrolling past today’s verse?

### 8. Offline-safe, private, pressure-free

All new paths work fully offline after first load. No analytics on signpost taps. No forced modals. Same restful spirit as the local restfulness note.

---

## What we do not do in this lane

- Global search redesign (site search stays; this lane is **path**, not **index**)
- New top-nav items or hamburger expansion
- AI “where should I go?” widgets
- Gamified tours, progress bars, or completion language on Explore
- Consolidating intentional duplicate entry points (variety by context stays)

---

## Measure success by finding the door, not browsing the house

Ask: *Did someone already on the porch find one honest next step without feeling managed?*

Not: *Did they discover every tool?*
