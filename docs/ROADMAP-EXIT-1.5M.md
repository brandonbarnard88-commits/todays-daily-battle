# $1.5M Exit Roadmap — 24–36 Months

No fluff, no hype. Where you are now: **~$55k** (three months in, zero MRR). Goal: **$1.5M exit** in 24–36 months.

**See also:** [ROADMAP.md](ROADMAP.md) for product phases, [ROADMAP-NEXT.md](docs/ROADMAP-NEXT.md) for immediate priorities.

## Phase 1: $100k–$300k (Months 4–9, end of 2026)

**Users:** 2k–5k MAU  
**Revenue:** $1k–$5k MRR  

**How:**

- **Launch free email** — Daily verse + streak nudge → 3k subs.
- **Push Battle Pro promo hard** — 50–100 subs at $9.99 = **$1k MRR**.
- **Viral:** "Share your streak" auto-post to X/TikTok ("Day 30—join me!").
- **Add "7-day streak badge" unlock** — Retention jumps ~15%.
- **Church outreach** — Email 100 pastors: "Free Team trial—assign verses to youth." One win = 200 users.

**Value at end of Phase 1:** $150k–$300k (buyers see early revenue + growth).

## Phase 2: $500k–$800k (Months 10–18, 2027)

**Users:** 10k–20k MAU  
**Revenue:** $10k–$20k MRR  

**How:**

- **Double down on Pro + Church** — 500–1.5k Pro subs, 20–50 church/team accounts. Bundle annual (save 33%) to lock in LTV.
- **Wins Report + share loop** — Shareable streak graphic → "Join my battle" link. Track referral signups; 5–10% of MAU from shares.
- **SEO + topical funnels** — "Daily verse for anxiety," "verse for grief," "pastor sermon prep." 5–10 landing pages; capture long-tail.
- **Paid acquisition (test)** — $500–2k/mo on Meta or Google; target church leaders, small groups. CAC < $20, LTV > $80.
- **Church/Team tier proof** — 3–5 case studies: "Youth group of 40 uses Battle for weekly verses." Sales deck + demo call.
- **Retention levers** — Streak repair (1 free/month), Armor series, offline PDFs. Reduce churn to <5% monthly.

**Value at end of Phase 2:** $500k–$800k (recurring revenue + proven playbook).

## Phase 3: $1M–$1.5M (Months 19–36, 2027–2028)

**Users:** 25k–50k+ MAU  
**Revenue:** $25k–$50k+ MRR (or equivalent ARR trajectory)  

**How:**

- **Scale what works** — More churches, more paid spend (CAC payback < 12 months). Affiliate or partner rev-share with ministries.
- **Enterprise / network** — "Diocese bundle," "denomination deal." One contract = 50–200 churches, $500–2k/mo.
- **Product expansion** — Premium devotionals, kids curriculum, Spanish (or second language). Higher ARPU, new segments.
- **Acquisition interest** — At $15k–30k MRR, buyers (ministry platforms, Bible apps, private equity) pay 3–5× ARR. $1.5M = ~$30k MRR at 4× or $375k ARR at 4×.
- **Clean books, clean story** — MRR dashboard, churn by cohort, one-pager: "3 years, bootstrapped, X MAU, Y MRR, Z churches."

**Value at exit:** $1M–$1.5M (proven niche, recurring revenue, clear growth path).
