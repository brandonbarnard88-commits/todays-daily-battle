# Site announcement draft — churches & homeschool groups

Use or trim as needed for bulletins, newsletters, or group email. Tone: calm, Scripture-first, no hype.

---

**Short (bulletin blurb)**

Today’s Daily Battle is a quiet, KJV-first place to search by how you feel, read today’s verse, follow gentle battle plans, and keep memory verses at home or in class—without ads chasing your family. Everything stays gentle: optional saves on your device, printable church and homeschool helpers, and a year-round rhythm you can print when you want it.

---

**Medium (email / newsletter)**

We wanted to share **Today’s Daily Battle** (todaysdailybattle.com) as a steady companion for hard days and ordinary ones. It is built to be **calm and Scripture-first**: KJV text, search by what you are carrying, daily verse, reading plans for adults and kids, and tools for study without noisy clutter.

**For families and classrooms:** There is a **year-round memory rhythm**, printable planners, a **church sharing kit** with VBS and children’s ministry ideas, and **gentle memory verse teaching methods**—no quizzes required, no performance pressure.

**Privacy posture:** The site does not need an account for the core experience; saves you choose stay oriented to **this device** unless you opt into sync. We are grateful when groups point people to something steady and true.

---

**One line (social / caption)**

A quiet KJV site for real days—verse, plans, memory helps, and a church kit you can print.
