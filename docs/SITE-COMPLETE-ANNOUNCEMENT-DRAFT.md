We’re glad to point you to **Today’s Daily Battle** (**todaysdailybattle.com**)—built as a **calm, KJV-first** place for hard days and ordinary ones. You can search by what you’re carrying, read today’s verse, walk short reading plans (adults and kids), and study without noisy clutter.

**For families, homeschool, and private-school Bible class:** the site offers a clear **year-round rhythm** (monthly memory grid, seasonal doors, summer seeds), **printable planners**, a one-page **year at a glance**, and a **church sharing kit** with volunteer-friendly ideas—including **gentle memory verse teaching methods** that respect opt-outs and skip quizzes.

**Privacy posture:** the core experience does not require an account. Verses, plan progress, and notes you save are oriented to **this device** unless you sign in to sync. We’re grateful when churches and co-ops share something steady and true.
