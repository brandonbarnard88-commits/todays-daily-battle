    Parent tools

    Short ideas—pick one when you are tired.

    • Read the verse aloud once, then ask: “What word stuck with you?” Silence is fine.
    • When correction lands hard, pause and pray one sentence before the next line.
    • Let littles draw while you talk; they are still in the room with God’s Word.
    • Hard week? Do one day from Psalms of Comfort (Family Edition) instead of skipping altogether.

    When parenting feels heavy — seven days in the Parenting in Faith plan (KJV).
