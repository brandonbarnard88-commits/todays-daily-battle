    How to use wins gently

    If you use the kids streaks, badges, or parent view, let them stay mercy-first.

    • Name what you saw: “You opened God’s Word today.” Save bigger praise for faithfulness, not performance.
    • Use the Family quiet view as a conversation helper, not a scoreboard.
    • Missed days do not need a speech. Pick up the next story and thank God for new mercy.
    • If a child shares a doodle or favorite story, ask one question before offering your own lesson.

    How to use Plans with your kids

    Short days, real KJV on screen, no quiz pressure. Pick one trail and let it fit your table.

    1. Start with one kids plan—Lord’s Prayer for Kids, Beatitudes for Kids, or Parable of the Sower for Kids—and stay with it until the rhythm feels familiar.
    2. Read the verse aloud once. The plain paragraph underneath is there if you want simpler words; let them ask one honest question before you turn the page.
    3. Try the small step together, or whisper it as a prayer. If the day is loud, skip gently and come back tomorrow—grace beats streaks.
    4. Progress saves on this device; no account needed. For take-home ideas or lobby cards, see the Church sharing kit (copy blocks and printable QR images).

    Homeschooling or co-op days? The same one-day rhythm fits morning basket and independent Bible time—see the ages 5–18 map, plus the homeschool guide and printable week tracker on the Church sharing kit.

    A simple family devotion rhythm

    About ten to fifteen minutes, centered on one kids plan day. No quiz; the site keeps your place on this device.

    1. Gather in one spot (table, couch, or bedtime).
    2. Open Lord’s Prayer for Kids, Beatitudes for Kids, or Sower for Kids on one device.
    3. Read the KJV verse and reflection together.
    4. Do the step as a family—draw, whisper, a small kindness, or pray.
    5. Pray the day’s prayer; anyone who wants may add one short sentence.
    6. Close with: “The site will remember where we are for next time.”

    Weekly shape: Monday–Friday, one day from the same study. Saturday, revisit a favorite prayer or step. Sunday, glance at progress on this page. Ages: littles lean on drawing and repeating the phrase; older kids can hear one line from the adult plan beside you if it helps.

    Leaders and churches: printable wording and parent handouts live in the Church sharing kit — family devotion rhythm.
