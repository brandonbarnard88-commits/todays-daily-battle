FAQ

Why do people use this instead of social media?

Same two minutes—but here you leave filled up, not drained. One verse, one moment, every day. The streak and daily battle give you a habit that actually grounds you. We're built to be the Bible companion you reach for first.

What are Elite tools?

Elite tools are the full set of features we offer. Free includes search, daily verse, 7-day plan, chapter reader, notes, message board, kids activities, verse cards (share image), memory verse mode, Battle of the day, prayer list, and share & listen. Supporter adds custom reading plans, verse image generator, choose email time, bulk export, recurring reminders, and private notes. Church adds assign reading, group progress, church verse of the day, church-branded exports, and shared prayer list. See Pricing for the full breakdown.

Is this free?

Yes. Search, Verse of the Day, 7-day plan, chapter reader, notes, message board, kids activities, verse cards, memory verse mode, Battle of the day, and prayer list are always free. Optional Supporter and Church plans add more elite tools like sermon/lesson builders, custom plans, and sync.

What do paid plans include?

Supporter ($5/mo or $50/yr) adds Daily Battle emails, Sermon Builder, Lesson Builder, shareable links, PDF/email export, curriculum builder, Church Center access, custom reading plans, verse image generator, choose email time, bulk export, recurring verse reminders, and private notes. Battle Pro ($10/mo or $100/yr) adds Wins Report, offline PDFs, and premium devotionals. Church/Team ($10/mo or $100/yr beta; reg $20/$200) adds team logins, shared lesson library, church hub, assign reading to groups, group progress, church verse of the day, verse + announcement, church-branded exports, and shared prayer list. See Pricing.

Which Bible version is included?

KJV is available now. NIV and ESV are planned after we secure licensing.

What is Battle of the day?

One suggested topic, one verse, and one short reflection question to focus your day—free for everyone. Topic of the day rotates so you always have a fresh starting point.

How do streaks work?

Your streak is the number of days in a row you've done today's battle (or verse). It saves locally; when you sign in, it can sync across devices. If you miss a day, share your invite link—when a friend signs up with it, you both get one streak repair.

Is my prayer public?

Quick-pray (♥) is anonymous. Only the count and anonymous dots on the map are shown—no names, no personal data. We never display who prayed what.

What are verse cards?

Create a shareable image of any verse (text + reference) for social media or your lock screen. Free for all users.

Can I use this for sermons and lessons?

Absolutely. The Sermon Builder and Study Tools are built for pastors and teachers.

How do I save my work?

When logged in, your notes and drafts can save across devices. If you’re not logged in, they save locally on your device.

Can free accounts post on the message board?

Yes. Free members can read and post encouragements.

How do I reset my password?

Click “Forgot password?” in the login bar and follow the email link.

Why didn’t my confirmation email work?

If the link shows an error, please request a new signup email and try again. If needed, use the password reset link to regain access.

How do I cancel my subscription?

When subscriptions are live, you can cancel anytime from your account or by contacting us. You'll keep access until the end of your billing period.

What's your refund policy?

If you're not satisfied, contact us within 14 days of a charge and we'll work with you—full refund for the current period when appropriate.

Can I export or delete my data?

Yes. You can request a copy of your data or full account deletion by contacting us at support@todaysdailybattle.com. We'll respond within a reasonable time.

My notes aren't syncing or I'm stuck logging in—what do I do?

Try "Forgot password?" to reset. If you still can't get in, clear the site data for todaysdailybattle.com in your browser and sign in again. If sync or login issues persist, contact us and we'll help troubleshoot.

How do I get help?

Use the Contact page or the contact link in the footer and we’ll get back to you.
