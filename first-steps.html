First steps in faith

You’re already welcome here—brand-new faith, a fresh start, or honest curiosity. Nothing on this page rushes you or scores your pace.

Whether you are brand-new or starting again, this page gathers the same KJV Plans the site already uses—one verse a day, plain words, a small step, a short prayer. Progress stays on your device; use Listen on any day if you want it read aloud.

If you are wondering how to come to Christ

You are welcome here—no test, no performance, no church résumé. If you are new, coming back, or only curious, stay. The Bible says God loves you and that life is in His Son, Jesus.

John 3:16 (KJV)

For God so loved the world, that he gave his only begotten Son, that whosoever believeth in him should not perish, but have everlasting life.

In simpler words (BBE)

Same verse—plainer English. The KJV above is still the main Scripture. How we use the Bible

Romans 10:9 (KJV) — That if thou shalt confess with thy mouth the Lord Jesus, and shalt believe in thine heart that God hath raised him from the dead, thou shalt be saved.

Romans 10:13 (KJV) — For whosoever shall call upon the name of the Lord shall be saved.

You can talk to Him now, in your own words. He hears you.

Lord Jesus, I need You. I believe You died for me and rose again. I turn to You—forgive me, save me, and make me Yours. Amen.

If you prayed that, tell a trusted believer, open a quiet plan below, and keep reading His Word. We are not a church membership desk—we are a porch with an open Bible. A local church that loves Jesus can walk with you next.

The Road to Salvation is five quiet days in the KJV—our need, God's love, the cross, faith, and new life. No test at the end. When you only want the Lord Jesus's own words, Only His Voice (red letters only) is five quiet days of listening.
