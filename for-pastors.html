For pastors & mobile ministers

Everything here is already on the site—gathered in one calm page so you can grab what you need on a travel day or a thin week. English tools use KJV on screen; localized “mood door” pages name their own public-domain text. No funnel, no signup wall for the tools themselves.

Print & share in the room

  • Verse cards gallery — printable KJV cards.
  • Verse image generator — quiet graphics for slides or texts.
  • Verse of the Day — copy or share the daily line.

Teach & prep

  • Sermon Builder — outline-friendly flow.
  • Pastor Builder and Pastor Library — study and shelf.
  • Pastor Toolkit and Pastor Resources.
  • Bible Tool — search and read (KJV in tool).

Care where people hurt

English hubs: Anxiety, Hope, Loneliness, Grief, Guilt, Overwhelmed — and the full list on Explore. Localized mood doors (many languages) live under Languages on Explore — good targets for a QR code on a handout.

Calm — one verse and breath. Prayer wall — anonymous intents, no performance.

Rhythm & groups

  • Battle Plans — multi-day tracks, offline-friendly.
  • 7-Day Plan — short anchor.
  • Team Toolkit and Church Join Hub — when you are equipping others.

Voices & contact

Real notes from readers: Reader stories. Questions or a permission to reprint: Contact or support@todaysdailybattle.com.

All tools are private by design. Your data stays on your device unless you sign in to sync.
