KJV verses for anxiety before tomorrow

If tomorrow is loud in your head, start with one steady line instead of ten spirals.

Philippians 4:6-7 (KJV): "Be careful for nothing; but in every thing by prayer and supplication with thanksgiving let your requests be made known unto God. And the peace of God, which passeth all understanding, shall keep your hearts and minds through Christ Jesus."

Psalm 56:3 (KJV): "What time I am afraid, I will trust in thee."

Matthew 6:34 (KJV): "Take therefore no thought for the morrow: for the morrow shall take thought for the things of itself."

One next step: Write one sentence prayer, then set the phone down for two minutes in silence.
