KJV verses for loneliness at night

Night can magnify everything. Scripture gives us a voice when words are low.

Psalm 34:18 (KJV): "The LORD is nigh unto them that are of a broken heart; and saveth such as be of a contrite spirit."

Hebrews 13:5 (KJV): "I will never leave thee, nor forsake thee."

Psalm 147:3 (KJV): "He healeth the broken in heart, and bindeth up their wounds."

One next step: Read one verse out loud, then place your hand over your chest and breathe slowly for sixty seconds.
