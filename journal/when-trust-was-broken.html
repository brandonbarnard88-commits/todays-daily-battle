KJV verses when trust was broken

For survivors, parents, and anyone carrying old harm. Read one verse. Sit with it. Trauma-informed therapy stays primary; Scripture is a companion.

Psalm 34:18 (KJV): “The LORD is nigh unto them that are of a broken heart; and saveth such as be of a contrite spirit.”

Mark 10:14 (KJV): “Suffer the little children to come unto me, and forbid them not: for of such is the kingdom of God.”

Isaiah 43:1 (KJV): “Fear not: for I have redeemed thee, I have called thee by thy name; thou art mine.”

One next step: Read one verse slowly. If you are a parent, say once: “It was not your fault. I love you. I believe you.” No sermon required.
