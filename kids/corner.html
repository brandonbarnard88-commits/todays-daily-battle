Story #2 · Comfort in the storm

Noah and the Ark

A long time ago, the world was full of sadness. But there was one man named Noah who loved God and tried to do right.

God told Noah, “I am going to send a great flood, but I will keep you safe. Build a big boat called an ark.” Noah obeyed and worked hard for many years. He brought his wife, his three sons, and their wives inside the ark. He also brought two of every kind of animal.

Then the rain began to fall. It rained and rained for forty days and forty nights. The water covered the whole earth. But inside the ark, Noah and his family and all the animals were safe and dry.

After many days the rain stopped. Noah sent out a dove, and it came back with an olive leaf in its mouth. The water was going down. Soon the ground was dry again.

God opened the door of the ark, and Noah’s family stepped out onto the dry land. God put a beautiful rainbow in the sky and said, “I promise I will never flood the whole earth like this again. I will always keep My word.”

Exact KJV reference: Genesis 6:5–9:17

Color the ark and the rainbow while someone reads it out loud. Then listen to the gentle loop if you want.
