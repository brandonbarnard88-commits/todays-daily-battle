Pick a path

Same Jesus—friendly doors. Loops for a quick replay, full stories when you have a few minutes, coloring when little hands want to make something, and calm prayer habits when you want screen-light time with God.

Need the family map? For the Little Ones is the calm all-in-one first stop, Family hub is the parent doorway, Bible Story Library is for read-aloud cards, Bible Loop Library is for quick replays, and Color & Tell is for hands-busy retelling.

For very young kids, Color & Tell and one comfort loop are the gentlest first doors. Save the longer story cards for when they want a few more minutes.

Best for ages 5-8 🎬 Quick Bible loops Short, bouncy cartoons—replay-safe when little hearts need calm. Best for ages 7-11 📚 Full Bible stories Read-along words, comic panels, and gentle quizzes live in the Library. Some stories add a short YouTube preview. Best for ages 5-10 🖍️ Color & create Tap a big picture, paint on the page, print for the fridge—KJV line on each sheet. Best for any age with a grown-up 🙏 Calm prayer habits Five-finger prayer, thank-you slips, paper chains—screen-light, ties to today’s verse. Printable sheet inside.
