Read-aloud

Sometimes missing someone aches in your throat and in your tummy. God does not rush you to “be okay.” He bends low.

The LORD is nigh unto them that are of a broken heart; and saveth such as be of a contrite spirit. Psalm 34:18 (KJV)

And Jesus promised the Holy Spirit as a Comforter who stays—like a friend who does not leave when the room is quiet.

I will not leave you comfortless: I will come to you. John 14:18 (KJV)

Gentle question

What is one kind memory you want God to hold with you tonight—even if tears come while you tell Him?

Short prayer together

Lord, we miss someone we love. Thank You that You are near when words are small. Carry our hearts. Amen.

Coloring idea

Picture a quiet porch at dusk: a rocking chair, a small lantern, empty seat beside someone who loves you. Use Verse Image with Quiet porch (T17), KJV only on the card.
