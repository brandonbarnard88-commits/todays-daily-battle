Words about God at the real table

A tiny telling that fits Family Worship in the Trenches—no perfect house required.

Read-aloud

God never said His words only belong in shiny buildings. He told families to speak of Him in the middle of real days.

And these words, which I command thee this day, shall be in thine heart: And thou shalt teach them diligently unto thy children, and shalt talk of them when thou sittest in thine house, and when thou walkest by the way, and when thou liest down, and when thou risest up. Deuteronomy 6:6–7 (KJV)

That can sound big—but today it might mean one true sentence about Jesus while shoes are still untied.

Gentle question

Where did we sit or walk today where we could tuck in one sentence about God—without making it a speech?

Short prayer together

Lord, make our house a place where Your words feel at home—in the mess and the tired and the love. Amen.
