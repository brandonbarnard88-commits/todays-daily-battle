Treat others the way you want to be treated

Jesus said it plain. Grown-ups can sit longer in the Golden Rule Life Lesson.

Read-aloud

Sometimes a brother grabs your toy. Sometimes you want to grab back. Jesus gives a different kind of strong—the kind that asks, “What would I want someone to do to me?”

Therefore all things whatsoever ye would that men should do to you, do ye even so to them: for this is the law and the prophets. Matthew 7:12 (KJV)

That is the Golden Rule. It is not easy. It is good. God helps us try again.

Gentle question

If someone did to you what you just did (or wanted to do), would it feel kind or sharp?

Short prayer together

Jesus, help us treat others the way we want to be treated. When we forget, pull us back to Your words. Amen.
