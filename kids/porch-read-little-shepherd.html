When mercy feels hard

Jesus blesses the merciful. Grown-ups can sit longer in Blessed Are the Merciful.

Someone hurt your feelings. Your tummy wants to stay mad. Jesus says people who show mercy are blessed—not because it is easy, but because God is kind first.

Blessed are the merciful: for they shall obtain mercy. Matthew 5:7 (KJV)

Mercy can be a small cup of water, a quiet sorry, or asking God to help your heart soften. You do not have to pretend nothing happened. You can ask Jesus for help to try.

Gentle question

What is one small kind thing you could do today, even if your heart still feels sore?

Short prayer together

Lord, You were merciful to us. Help us be merciful too. Amen.
