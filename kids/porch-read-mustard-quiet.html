When faith feels small

A quiet read beside the mustard seed—different from summer seeds (slow growth). This is trust when faith feels tiny in your hand.

Read-aloud

Some days faith feels as small as a seed in your palm—not loud, not sure, just honest. You do not need giant faith tonight. Jesus told His friends that even faith that small is real in God’s hands. He does not ask us to be giants. He asks us to trust Him.

And Jesus said unto them, Because of your unbelief: for verily I say unto you, If ye have faith as a grain of mustard seed, ye shall say unto this mountain, Remove hence to yonder place; and it shall remove; and nothing shall be impossible unto you. Matthew 17:20 (KJV)

That is not hype about being brave. It is rest: small faith held out to a great God.

Gentle question

What is one small true thing we can trust God with tonight?

Short prayer together

Lord, our faith feels small—but You are great. Help us trust You with what we cannot carry. Amen.
