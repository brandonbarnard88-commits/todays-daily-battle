Peace in the storm

Jesus was in the boat when the wind roared. Grown-ups can sit longer in Peace in the Storm or trace the Möbius ribbon.

One day Jesus and His friends were in a boat. Wind and waves got big. The boat rocked. Jesus was resting—He was not afraid. His friends woke Him because they were scared. Jesus stood up and spoke to the storm.

And he arose, and rebuked the wind, and said unto the sea, Peace, be still. And the wind ceased, and there was a great calm. Mark 4:39 (KJV)

Jesus is with you in hard things too. You can whisper His words when you feel afraid.

Gentle question

What felt stormy today—loud, scary, or too much? Can you tell Jesus in one short sentence?

Short prayer together

Jesus, You are in the boat with me. Peace, be still. Amen.
