After voices were loud

For the table when everyone needs a gentler tone—pairs with After a hard conversation.

Read-aloud

Sometimes words come out sharp—even people who love each other. You do not have to pretend nothing happened. God’s Word shows a quiet way back toward peace.

A soft answer turneth away wrath: but grievous words stir up anger. Proverbs 15:1 (KJV)

Soft does not mean fake. It means we ask Jesus for help before we speak again—one honest sentence at a time.

Gentle question

What is one kind thing we could say or do next—even if we still feel upset inside?

Short prayer together

Lord, when our voices were loud, meet us with Your quiet strength. Help us speak soft and true. Amen.
