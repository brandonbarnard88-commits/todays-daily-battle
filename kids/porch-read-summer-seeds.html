A tiny seed, a big shade

Summer-light telling beside Summer Seeds for Kids—one anchor day at a time.

Read-aloud

Jesus told a story about something smaller than your thumb—and God made it grow until birds could rest in its branches.

It is like a grain of mustard seed, which, when it is sown in the earth, is less than all the seeds that be in the earth: But when it is sown, it groweth up, and becometh greater than all herbs, and shooteth out great branches; so that the fowls of the air may lodge under the shadow of it. Mark 4:31–32 (KJV)

Your summer might feel ordinary. God still grows quiet faith when we water it with His Word.

Gentle question

What is one “small seed” habit we could keep this week—a verse at breakfast, a thank-you at bedtime?

Short prayer together

Lord, grow faith in our home this summer—not loud, just true. Amen.

Coloring idea

A path through trees, dappled light, a bird on a branch—rest under something God grew. Verse Image → Woodland path (T18).
