When friendship feels hard to keep

A quiet read beside David and Jonathan—not about perfect friends. Jonathan loved David when it was dangerous to love him.

Read-aloud

Sometimes friends bump hard. Sometimes staying loyal costs something—you tell the truth when it is easier to stay quiet, or you say goodbye when someone you love has to run. Jonathan and David made a promise before God. When they parted, Jonathan did not pretend everything was easy. He sent David in peace.

And Jonathan said to David, Go in peace, forasmuch as we have sworn both of us in the name of the LORD, saying, The LORD be between me and thee, and between my seed and thy seed for ever. 1 Samuel 20:42 (KJV)

God sees covenant love—the kind that tells the truth and lets the other person go in peace.

Gentle question

How can we show a friend we will tell the truth kindly and let them be safe?

Short prayer together

Lord, help us love friends loyally. Be between us and the people we love. Amen.
