# Story Package: Abraham and Isaac (Genesis 22)

**Story Key**: `abrahamIsaac`

**Position in 365 Roadmap**: Phase 1, Story #3 — A powerful story of faith and God’s provision.

---

## Reverent Read-Aloud Retelling (for ages 3–8)

God had given Abraham a wonderful promise: he would have a son, and through that son God would bless many people. When Abraham was very old, God kept His promise and gave him a son named Isaac.

One day God tested Abraham’s faith. He said, “Take your son Isaac, whom you love, and go to the mountain. Offer him there as a sacrifice to Me.”

Abraham trusted God. Early the next morning he got up, took Isaac and two servants, and went to the place God had told him. When they reached the mountain, Abraham built an altar and placed the wood on it. Then he laid Isaac on the altar.

Just as Abraham lifted his hand to obey, the angel of the Lord called from heaven: “Abraham! Abraham! Do not lay your hand on the boy. Now I know that you fear God, because you have not withheld your only son from Me.”

Abraham looked up and saw a ram caught in a thicket by its horns. He took the ram and offered it instead of his son.

Then God said, “Because you have obeyed Me, I will surely bless you. Your descendants will be as many as the stars in the sky and the sand on the seashore.”

Abraham and Isaac went home together, knowing that God always provides.

## Quiz — 4 Questions (with helpful scaffolding)

**1. What did God ask Abraham to do with Isaac?**
- A) Take him on a trip
- B) Offer him as a sacrifice on the mountain
- C) Leave him in the wilderness
- D) Give him to the neighbors

*Correct: B*  
Wrong answers scaffolding:
- If A: “It was much more than a trip. God asked Abraham to offer Isaac as a sacrifice to test his faith.”
- If C: “God didn’t ask Abraham to leave Isaac alone. He asked for something very hard — to offer him.”
- If D: “God was testing Abraham’s trust by asking him to offer Isaac on the altar.”

**2. What did Abraham do when God told him to stop?**
- A) He kept going anyway
- B) He got angry at God
- C) He immediately obeyed and stopped
- D) He ran away

*Correct: C*  
Wrong answers scaffolding:
- If A: “Abraham trusted God so much that when the angel told him to stop, he obeyed right away.”
- If B: “Abraham didn’t get angry. He was willing to obey, and God provided another way.”
- If D: “Abraham stayed and listened. He was ready to obey God completely.”

**3. What did God provide instead of Isaac?**
- A) A lamb
- B) A ram caught in a thicket
- C) A dove
- D) Nothing — Abraham didn’t need to sacrifice anything

*Correct: B*  
Wrong answers scaffolding:
- If A: “It wasn’t a lamb. God provided a ram that was caught in the bushes by its horns.”
- If C: “It was a ram, not a dove. The ram was caught in the thicket.”
- If D: “God did provide a sacrifice — a ram instead of Isaac.”

**4. What did God promise Abraham because he obeyed?**
- A) He would never have to move again
- B) His descendants would be as many as the stars and the sand
- C) Isaac would live forever
- D) He would become very rich

*Correct: B*  
Wrong answers scaffolding:
- If A: “Abraham still had to move and live by faith. The big promise was about his many descendants.”
- If C: “Isaac would grow up, but the promise was that Abraham’s family would become huge — like the stars and sand.”
- If D: “God did bless Abraham, but the main promise was about his descendants being countless.”
