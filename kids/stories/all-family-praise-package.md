# All Family Praise (Psalm 150:6)

## Emotional Focus
The joy of a family that praises God together.

## Key KJV
“Let every thing that hath breath praise the LORD. Praise ye the LORD.” (Psalm 150:6)

## Gentle Retelling
When families sing, pray, and thank God together, the whole house feels full of joy. Everything that has breath can praise the Lord — including us at home.

## Coloring Prompt
A family sitting together with arms raised happily praising God in their cozy home.

## Read-Along Flow + Response
“The happiest homes are the ones that praise God together.”
