# Ananias Goes to Saul (Acts 9:15)

## Emotional Focus
Obeying God even when it feels scary.

## Key KJV
“But the Lord said unto him, Go thy way: for he is a chosen vessel unto me.” (Acts 9:15)

## Gentle Retelling
God told Ananias to go help Saul, who had been mean to Christians. Ananias obeyed, prayed for Saul, and Saul’s eyes were opened to Jesus.

## Coloring Prompt
Ananias gently placing hands on Saul’s eyes with soft healing light.

## Read-Along Flow + Response
“God can use even our scary yes to do big things.”
