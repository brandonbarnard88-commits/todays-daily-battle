# Anna’s Prayer in the Temple (Luke 2:38)

## Emotional Focus
Praying for many years and seeing God answer.

## Key KJV
“And she coming in that instant gave thanks likewise unto the Lord.” (Luke 2:38)

## Gentle Retelling
Anna prayed in the temple every day for many years. When baby Jesus was brought in, she knew God had answered and praised Him with joy.

## Coloring Prompt
Old Anna smiling and giving thanks as she sees baby Jesus in the temple.

## Read-Along Flow + Response
“God hears prayers even when we wait a long time.”
