# Baby Moses in the Basket (Exodus 2:10)

## Emotional Focus
God watching over babies and families.

## Key KJV
“And the child grew, and she brought him unto Pharaoh’s daughter, and he became her son.” (Exodus 2:10)

## Gentle Retelling
Baby Moses was placed in a little basket on the river, but God kept him safe and gave him a new home with a princess who loved him.

## Coloring Prompt
Tiny Moses smiling in his basket while the princess gently reaches in.

## Read-Along Flow + Response
“God watches over every baby and every family.”
