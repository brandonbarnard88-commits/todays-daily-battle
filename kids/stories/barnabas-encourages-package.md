# Barnabas the Encourager (Acts 11:23)

## Emotional Focus
Being a good friend who cheers others on.

## Key KJV
“And the son of consolation… Barnabas… exhorted them all.” (Acts 11:23)

## Gentle Retelling
When the new believers were scared, Barnabas came alongside them and said kind words that helped them keep trusting Jesus. He was called the Son of Encouragement.

## Coloring Prompt
Barnabas standing with new friends, smiling and patting a shoulder; warm light.

## Read-Along Flow + Response
“You can be someone’s encourager just like Barnabas.”
