# Cornelius Listens (Acts 10:4)

## Emotional Focus
Listening when God sends someone to help us.

## Key KJV
“What is it, Lord?” (Acts 10:4)

## Gentle Retelling
Cornelius prayed and an angel told him to send for Peter. Cornelius obeyed and learned about Jesus. His whole house believed.

## Coloring Prompt
Cornelius kneeling and listening to the angel with a peaceful face.

## Read-Along Flow + Response
“God sends help when we listen and obey.”
