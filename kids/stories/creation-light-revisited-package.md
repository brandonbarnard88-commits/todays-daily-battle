# Creation Light (Genesis 1:3)

## Emotional Focus
Thankfulness for light that chases away dark.

## Key KJV
“And God said, Let there be light: and there was light.” (Genesis 1:3)

## Gentle Retelling
In the very beginning it was dark. God spoke and light filled everything. He made the sun for day and stars for night so we would never be alone in the dark.

## Coloring Prompt
Bright sun rising over a peaceful world with soft rays touching everything.

## Read-Along Flow + Response
“Thank You, God, for light and for being with me in the dark too.”
