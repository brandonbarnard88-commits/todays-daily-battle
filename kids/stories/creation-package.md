## Reverent Read-Aloud Retelling (for ages 3–8)

In the beginning God made the heaven and the earth. Everything was dark and empty, but God said, “Let there be light,” and there was light. He made the sky, the dry land, and the seas. He filled the earth with green grass, bright flowers, tall trees, and every kind of fruit. He placed the sun to shine by day and the moon and stars to shine by night. He made the fish to swim in the seas, the birds to fly in the air, and every animal to walk on the land. Then God made man in His own image, and He gave him a garden home filled with good things. On the seventh day God rested and blessed that day and made it holy.

Everything God made was very good.

## Quiz — 4 Questions (with helpful scaffolding)

**1. What did God make first?**
- A) The sun and moon
- B) Light
- C) Animals
- D) People

*Correct: B*  
Wrong answers scaffolding:
- If A: “The sun and moon came later. On the very first day, God spoke and made light appear.”
- If C: “Animals came on day five and six. The very first thing God created was light.”
- If D: “People were made on the sixth day. The first thing God made was light.”

**2. At the end of each day, what did God say about what He made?**
- A) “It was okay.”
- B) “It was very good.”
- C) “It was finished.”
- D) “It needed more work.”

*Correct: B*  
Wrong answers scaffolding:
- If A: “God didn’t just say it was okay. He looked at everything and called it very good.”
- If C: “God did say it was finished on the seventh day, but every day He said something even better — that it was very good.”
- If D: “God was happy with everything He made. He called it very good.”

**3. On which day did God rest?**
- A) The first day
- B) The third day
- C) The seventh day
- D) The fifth day

*Correct: C*  
Wrong answers scaffolding:
- If A: “On the first day God made light. He rested after all His work was done.”
- If B: “On the third day God made land and plants. He rested when everything was complete.”
- If D: “On the fifth day God made birds and fish. He rested on the seventh day.”

**4. Why did God make the world?**
- A) Because He was lonely
- B) Because it was very good and He wanted to share it
- C) Because He needed help
- D) By accident

*Correct: B*  
Wrong answers scaffolding:
- If A: “God wasn’t lonely. He made everything out of love and called it very good.”
- If C: “God doesn’t need help. He made the world because it pleased Him and it was good.”
- If D: “God carefully made everything on purpose. He said it was very good.”
