# Story Package: Daniel in the Lions’ Den (Daniel 6)

**Story Key**: `danielLions`

**Position in 365 Roadmap**: Phase 4, Story #13 — God protects those who pray and trust Him even when it is dangerous.

---

## Reverent Read-Aloud Retelling (for ages 3–8)

Daniel loved God and prayed to Him every day. The king made a new law that said no one could pray to anyone except the king for thirty days. Anyone who broke the law would be thrown into a den of lions.

Daniel knew the law, but he still went to his room, opened his window toward Jerusalem, and prayed to God three times a day, just like he always did.

Some jealous men saw Daniel praying and told the king. The king was very sad because he liked Daniel, but he had to obey his own law. So Daniel was thrown into the lions’ den.

That night the king could not sleep. Early in the morning he ran to the den and called out, “Daniel! Has your God been able to save you from the lions?”

Daniel answered, “My God sent His angel and shut the lions’ mouths. They have not hurt me!”

The king was overjoyed. He had Daniel brought out of the den. Not even a scratch was on him because he had trusted in his God.

The king made a new law that everyone in his kingdom should honor the God of Daniel.

Simple black-and-white line art for ages 3–8. A peaceful man (Daniel) praying on his knees with hands together. In the background, several lions with closed mouths looking calm. A stone den with a large stone rolled in front. A king looking surprised and happy at the opening. Gentle, brave, and miraculous feeling. Clean bold lines, large open spaces.

## Read-Along Flow

Color Daniel praying and the quiet lions → listen to the story of courage and God’s protection → remember that God is with us when we choose to pray and trust Him, even when it’s scary.

## Quiz — 4 Questions (with helpful scaffolding)

**1. What did Daniel do even after the king made the new law?**
- A) He stopped praying for thirty days
- B) He kept praying to God three times every day
- C) He prayed only to the king
- D) He hid his prayers so no one would see

*Correct: B*  
Wrong answers scaffolding:
- If A: “Daniel loved God too much to stop. He kept praying just like he always had.”
- If C: “Daniel only prayed to the one true God. He would not pray to the king.”
- If D: “Daniel did not hide. He prayed with his window open, just as he always did.”

**2. Why was the king sad when Daniel had to go into the lions’ den?**
- A) Because he didn’t like lions
- B) Because he cared about Daniel and didn’t want him to get hurt
- C) Because he wanted to go into the den himself
- D) Because the law was his favorite law

*Correct: B*  
Wrong answers scaffolding:
- If A: “The king was sad because he liked Daniel and knew the lions could hurt him.”
- If C: “The king didn’t want to go in. He was worried about his friend Daniel.”
- If D: “The king had made the law, but he was sorry it had to be used on Daniel.”

**3. What happened inside the lions’ den during the night?**
- A) The lions roared and tried to hurt Daniel
- B) God sent an angel who shut the lions’ mouths so they did not hurt Daniel
- C) Daniel fought the lions with a sword
- D) The lions fell asleep and snored loudly

*Correct: B*  
Wrong answers scaffolding:
- If A: “The lions were quiet because God protected Daniel. They did not hurt him at all.”
- If C: “Daniel didn’t need to fight. God sent an angel to keep the lions calm.”
- If D: “The lions stayed awake but gentle. God’s angel made sure they left Daniel alone.”

**4. What did the king do after Daniel was saved?**
- A) He made everyone in the kingdom honor the God of Daniel
- B) He threw the jealous men into the den instead
- C) He told Daniel never to pray again
- D) He forgot all about it

*Correct: A*  
Wrong answers scaffolding:
- If B: “The king did punish the men who tricked him, but the important thing was that he made a new law to honor God.”
- If C: “The king saw God’s power and wanted everyone to know about the true God.”
- If D: “The king was so amazed that he wrote a new law so the whole kingdom would respect Daniel’s God.”
