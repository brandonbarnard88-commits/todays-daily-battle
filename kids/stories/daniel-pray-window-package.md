# Daniel Prays at His Window (Daniel 6:10)

## Emotional Focus
Praying even when it’s hard or scary to do so.

## Key KJV
“Now when Daniel knew that the writing was signed, he went into his house… and prayed.” (Daniel 6:10)

## Gentle Retelling
Daniel kept praying to God three times a day even when the king made it against the law. God shut the lions’ mouths and kept him safe.

## Coloring Prompt
Daniel praying at an open window with calm lions lying nearby.

## Read-Along Flow + Response
“You can pray to God anytime — He is always listening.”
