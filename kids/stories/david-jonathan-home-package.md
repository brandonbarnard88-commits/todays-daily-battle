# David and Jonathan Like Family (1 Samuel 18:1)

## Emotional Focus
Best friends who feel like family.

## Key KJV
“The soul of Jonathan was knit with the soul of David, and Jonathan loved him as his own soul.” (1 Samuel 18:1)

## Gentle Retelling
Jonathan and David became best friends and loved each other like brothers. Even when life was hard, their friendship felt like home.

## Coloring Prompt
David and Jonathan standing together as friends, one giving the other his coat.

## Read-Along Flow + Response
“God gives us friends who feel like family.”
