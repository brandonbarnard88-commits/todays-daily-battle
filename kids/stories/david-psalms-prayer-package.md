# David’s Prayers in the Psalms (Psalm 9:1)

## Emotional Focus
Talking to God with honest words and songs.

## Key KJV
“I will praise thee, O LORD, with my whole heart.” (Psalm 9:1)

## Gentle Retelling
David talked to God in songs and honest words — when he was happy, scared, or sorry. God loved every prayer David prayed.

## Coloring Prompt
Young David playing his harp and singing to God on green hills.

## Read-Along Flow + Response
“You can talk to God with songs and honest words anytime.”
