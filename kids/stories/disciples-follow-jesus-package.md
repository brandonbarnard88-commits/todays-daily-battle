# The Disciples Follow Jesus (Matthew 4:19)

## Emotional Focus
Leaving what we know to follow Jesus.

## Key KJV
“Follow me, and I will make you fishers of men.” (Matthew 4:19)

## Gentle Retelling
Jesus called ordinary fishermen by the sea and said, “Follow Me.” They left their nets right away and walked with Jesus every day.

## Coloring Prompt
Fishermen leaving their boats and nets to walk with Jesus along the shore.

## Read-Along Flow + Response
“Following Jesus is the best adventure.”
