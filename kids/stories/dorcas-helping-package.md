# Dorcas Helping the Widows (Acts 9:36)

## Emotional Focus
Using your hands to show kindness to others.

## Key KJV
“This woman was full of good works and almsdeeds which she did.” (Acts 9:36)

## Gentle Retelling
Dorcas made clothes for widows and helped anyone who needed it. When she died, her friends asked Peter to come because they missed her kind heart.

## Coloring Prompt
Dorcas sitting happily sewing colorful clothes for smiling widows.

## Read-Along Flow + Response
“God can use your hands to show love every day.”
