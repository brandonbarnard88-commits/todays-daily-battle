# Elijah and the Fire from Heaven (1 Kings 18:38)

## Emotional Focus
Trusting God’s power when things look impossible.

## Key KJV
“Then the fire of the LORD fell, and consumed the burnt sacrifice.” (1 Kings 18:38)

## Gentle Retelling
Elijah prayed on the mountain and fire came down from heaven and burned up the sacrifice. Everyone saw that the Lord is the true God.

## Coloring Prompt
Elijah kneeling at the altar while fire comes down from heaven onto the sacrifice.

## Read-Along Flow + Response
“God’s power is real and He hears our prayers.”
