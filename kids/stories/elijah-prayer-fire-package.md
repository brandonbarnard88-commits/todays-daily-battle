# Elijah Prays for Fire (1 Kings 18:37)

## Emotional Focus
Praying when you want others to know God is real.

## Key KJV
“Hear me, O LORD, hear me, that this people may know that thou art the LORD God.” (1 Kings 18:37)

## Gentle Retelling
Elijah prayed on the mountain and fire came down from heaven. Everyone saw that the Lord is the true God.

## Coloring Prompt
Elijah kneeling at the altar while fire comes down from heaven.

## Read-Along Flow + Response
“God hears our prayers and shows Himself real.”
