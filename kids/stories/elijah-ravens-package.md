# Story Package: Elijah and the Ravens (1 Kings 17)

**Story Key**: `elijahRavens`

**Position in 365 Roadmap**: Phase 3, Story #12 — God provides in surprising ways when we trust Him.

---

## Reverent Read-Aloud Retelling (for ages 3–8)

There was a wicked king named Ahab who led the people away from God. God sent a prophet named Elijah to speak to the king.

Elijah told the king, “There will be no rain for a long time because you have turned away from the Lord.”

Then God told Elijah, “Go hide by the brook Cherith. I have commanded the ravens to bring you food there.”

Elijah obeyed and went to the brook. Every morning and every evening the ravens brought him bread and meat. He drank water from the brook.

When the brook dried up because there was no rain, God sent Elijah to a widow’s house in another town. Even though she had almost no food left, God made her flour and oil last for many days so she, her son, and Elijah had enough to eat.

God took care of Elijah in wonderful ways — first with birds, and then with a poor widow — because Elijah trusted and obeyed Him.

---

## Coloring Page Prompt

Simple black-and-white line art for ages 3–8. A prophet sitting by a small stream. Large black birds (ravens) flying with bread in their beaks. A kind woman at a doorway with a jar of flour and jug of oil. Peaceful wilderness scene. Clean bold lines, gentle and miraculous feeling.

---

## Read-Along Flow

Color the ravens bringing food → listen to how God provides exactly what we need → remember that God can use surprising ways (even birds!) to take care of His people.

## Quiz — 4 Questions (with helpful scaffolding)

**1. Why did God tell Elijah to hide by the brook?**
- A) Because it was a nice place for a vacation
- B) Because the king was angry and God wanted to protect Elijah
- C) Because Elijah was tired of talking
- D) Because there were no people there at all

*Correct: B*  
Wrong answers scaffolding:
- If A: “It wasn’t a vacation. The king was very angry at Elijah’s message, so God sent him somewhere safe.”
- If C: “Elijah was still serving God. He went where God told him for protection.”
- If D: “There were no people nearby, but God used the ravens to bring him food every day.”

**2. Who brought food to Elijah by the brook?**
- A) His friends from the city
- B) Ravens that God sent every morning and evening
- C) Angels with baskets
- D) The king’s servants

*Correct: B*  
Wrong answers scaffolding:
- If A: “No friends came. God used wild birds — ravens — to bring bread and meat to Elijah.”
- If C: “It was ravens, not angels. God can use any of His creatures to help His people.”
- If D: “The king was the one who was angry. God used the ravens instead.”

**3. What happened when the brook dried up?**
- A) Elijah had to go hungry
- B) God sent him to a widow’s house where the flour and oil never ran out
- C) He went back to the king and said sorry
- D) He found another brook nearby

*Correct: B*  
Wrong answers scaffolding:
- If A: “God didn’t let him stay hungry. He sent Elijah to a new place with a new way to provide food.”
- If C: “Elijah kept obeying God. The widow’s flour and oil lasted because of God’s miracle.”
- If D: “The brook was dry everywhere because there was no rain. God had a different plan.”

**4. What does this story show us about God?**
- A) That He only helps people who are rich
- B) That He can provide for us in surprising ways when we trust and obey Him
- C) That we should never ask for help
- D) That birds are the only way God sends food

*Correct: B*  
Wrong answers scaffolding:
- If A: “God helped Elijah and a poor widow. He cares for people whether they have a lot or a little.”
- If C: “Elijah trusted God and went where he was told. God met his needs in wonderful ways.”
- If D: “God used ravens first, then a widow. He can use many different ways to take care of us.”
