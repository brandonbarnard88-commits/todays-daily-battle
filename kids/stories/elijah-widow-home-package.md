# Elijah and the Widow’s Home (1 Kings 17:16)

## Emotional Focus
When the family feels like there isn’t enough.

## Key KJV
“And the barrel of meal wasted not, neither did the cruse of oil fail.” (1 Kings 17:16)

## Gentle Retelling
A widow and her son had almost nothing, but Elijah stayed with them. God made their little bit of flour and oil last and last. Their home was cared for.

## Coloring Prompt
The widow smiling as she pours endless flour and oil while her son watches happily.

## Read-Along Flow + Response
“God takes care of families even when it feels like not enough.”
