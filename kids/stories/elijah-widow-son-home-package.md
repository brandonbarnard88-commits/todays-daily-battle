# Elijah and the Widow’s Son (1 Kings 17:16)

## Emotional Focus
God providing for a single-parent home.

## Key KJV
“And the barrel of meal wasted not, neither did the cruse of oil fail, according to the word of the LORD.” (1 Kings 17:16)

## Gentle Retelling
A widow and her son had almost nothing, but God sent Elijah and made their food last. Their little home was full of God’s care.

## Coloring Prompt
The widow and her son smiling as they share bread at a simple table.

## Read-Along Flow + Response
“God takes special care of families who feel alone.”
