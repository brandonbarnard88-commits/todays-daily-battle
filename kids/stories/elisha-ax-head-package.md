# Elisha and the Floating Axe Head (2 Kings 6:6)

## Emotional Focus
When something small is lost and you need help.

## Key KJV
“And the man of God said, Where fell it? And he shewed him the place. And he cut down a stick, and cast it in thither; and the iron did swim.” (2 Kings 6:6)

## Gentle Retelling
A borrowed axe head fell into the river. Elisha threw a stick in the water and the iron axe head floated up so it could be saved.

## Coloring Prompt
Elisha throwing a stick into a river while an iron axe head floats to the surface.

## Read-Along Flow + Response
“Nothing is too small for God’s power to fix.”
