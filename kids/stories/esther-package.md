# Story Package: Esther (Book of Esther)

**Story Key**: `esther`

**Position in 365 Roadmap**: Phase 4, Story #15 — God’s quiet providence and courageous obedience.

---

## Reverent Read-Aloud Retelling (for ages 3–8)

A young Jewish woman named Esther became queen in a faraway land. Her cousin Mordecai had raised her. He loved her like a daughter and taught her to honor God.

A wicked man named Haman hated the Jewish people and tricked the king into making a law that said all the Jews should be destroyed on a certain day.

Mordecai asked Esther to go to the king and beg for her people’s lives. But no one was allowed to go to the king unless he first invited them. If Esther went without being called, she could be put to death.

Esther was afraid, but she decided to be brave. She asked all the Jewish people to fast and pray with her for three days. Then she put on her royal robes and went to the king.

When the king saw her, he held out his golden scepter, which meant she was welcome. Esther asked the king to save her people.

God helped Esther be very wise. In the end, the wicked Haman was punished, and the Jewish people were saved. God had placed Esther in the palace “for such a time as this.”

In the background, a kind older man (Mordecai) and a crowd of Jewish people praying. Calm, brave, and hopeful feeling. Clean bold lines with good open space.

## Quiz — 4 Questions (with helpful scaffolding)

**1. Why was Esther afraid to go to the king?**
- A) Because she didn’t like the king
- B) Because no one was allowed to go to the king unless he first called for them
- C) Because she had never seen the king before
- D) Because the king was her enemy

*Correct: B*  
Wrong answers scaffolding:
- If A: “Esther was the queen, but there was a strict law. Going without being invited could mean death.”
- If C: “She was already the queen. The problem was the law about approaching the king.”
- If D: “The king actually loved Esther. The danger was breaking the palace rule.”

**2. What did Esther ask the Jewish people to do before she went to the king?**
- A) Throw a big party
- B) Fast and pray with her for three days
- C) Run and hide
- D) Write letters to the king

*Correct: B*  
Wrong answers scaffolding:
- If A: “They didn’t celebrate. They prayed and went without food to ask God for help.”
- If C: “They stayed together and prayed. Esther knew they needed God’s help.”
- If D: “Writing letters wouldn’t have been enough. They needed God to change the king’s heart.”

**3. What happened when Esther went to see the king without being called?**
- A) The king was angry and sent her away
- B) The king held out his golden scepter, which meant she was safe and welcome
- C) The guards arrested her
- D) The king didn’t notice her

*Correct: B*  
Wrong answers scaffolding:
- If A: “The king was pleased to see her. He held out the scepter as a sign of favor.”
- If C: “God softened the king’s heart. Esther was allowed to speak.”
- If D: “The king saw her right away and was glad she had come.”

**4. How did God use Esther to help her people?**
- A) She became a soldier and fought the enemy
- B) She bravely spoke to the king and the Jewish people were saved from being destroyed
- C) She ran away with all the Jewish people
- D) She became the new queen of a different country

*Correct: B*  
Wrong answers scaffolding:
- If A: “Esther didn’t fight with weapons. She used courage and wisdom with the king.”
- If C: “She stayed and spoke up. God gave her favor with the king.”
- If D: “She remained queen and used her position to protect her people.”
