# Eutychus Falls and Lives (Acts 20:10)

## Emotional Focus
Friends who stay and help when someone gets hurt.

## Key KJV
“And Paul went down, and fell on him, and embracing him said, Trouble not yourselves; for his life is in him.” (Acts 20:10)

## Gentle Retelling
Young Eutychus fell asleep and fell from a window. Paul hugged him and God brought him back. Friends stayed close and helped.

## Coloring Prompt
Paul gently holding Eutychus after he fell; worried friends looking on with hope.

## Read-Along Flow + Response
“Friends stay close when you need help.”
