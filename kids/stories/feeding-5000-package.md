# Story Package: Feeding the 5,000 (Matthew 14, Mark 6, Luke 9, John 6)

**Story Key**: `feeding5000`

**Position in 365 Roadmap**: Phase 5, Story #20 — Jesus cares about our everyday needs and can do more than we can imagine with what we give Him.

---

## Reverent Read-Aloud Retelling (for ages 3–8)

A huge crowd had followed Jesus out into the countryside to hear Him teach and to see Him heal the sick. As the day grew late, the disciples came to Jesus and said, “This is a lonely place, and the people are hungry. Send them away so they can buy food in the villages.”

Jesus answered, “You give them something to eat.”

The disciples looked at each other. “We only have five small loaves of bread and two fish — that’s not nearly enough for five thousand men, plus women and children!”

Jesus said, “Bring them here to Me.”

He told the people to sit down on the green grass. Then He took the five loaves and two fish, looked up to heaven, gave thanks, and broke the bread. He gave it to the disciples, and the disciples gave it to the people.

Everyone ate until they were full. When they gathered up the leftovers, there were twelve baskets full! Jesus had taken a little boy’s small lunch and fed thousands of people with plenty left over.

The people were amazed and said, “Truly this is the Prophet who is to come into the world.”

Extra-thick outlines, very large simple shapes, almost no internal lines, maximum white space, preschool coloring page. Minimal internal detail — no tiny patterns, no cross-hatching, no texture. Pure black lines only on pure white background. Flat 2D, no perspective tricks. Friendly, warm, peaceful, respectful (never cartoonish, never scary, never irreverent). Centered composition with room for a short KJV verse at the bottom if needed. Avoid: shading, shadows, gradients, gray, grayscale, color, texture, crosshatching, stippling, noise, watermark, text, signature, photorealistic, 3d, realistic, scary, violent, detailed background, small details, thin lines, broken outlines

## Read-Along Flow

Color the little lunch and the huge crowd being fed → listen to how Jesus takes what we offer (even if it feels small) and multiplies it → remember that nothing is too small for Jesus to use when we give it to Him with trust.

## Quiz — 4 Questions (with helpful scaffolding)

**1. How many loaves and fish did the disciples have at the beginning?**
- A) Twelve baskets full
- B) Five small loaves and two fish
- C) Enough food for everyone already
- D) Nothing at all

*Correct: B*  
Wrong answers scaffolding:
- If A: “That was what was left over after everyone ate! At the start they only had a tiny lunch.”
- If C: “They had almost nothing. That’s why the miracle was so surprising.”
- If D: “A little boy had offered his lunch. It wasn’t much, but it was all he had.”

**2. What did Jesus do before He broke the bread?**
- A) He told everyone to go home
- B) He looked up to heaven and gave thanks
- C) He sent the disciples to buy more food
- D) He hid the food so only a few could eat

*Correct: B*  
Wrong answers scaffolding:
- If A: “Jesus wanted to feed them right there. He didn’t send them away.”
- If C: “He didn’t need to buy more. He took what they had and made it enough.”
- If D: “Jesus shared generously. Everyone had more than enough.”

**3. How much food was left over after everyone had eaten?**
- A) None — they ran out
- B) Twelve baskets full
- C) Just a few crumbs
- D) One extra loaf

*Correct: B*  
Wrong answers scaffolding:
- If A: “There was so much left that they filled twelve baskets! Jesus always gives more than we need.”
- If C: “There were huge amounts left over. God’s blessing was overflowing.”
- If D: “It wasn’t just one loaf. There was more than enough for everyone to take home.”

**4. What does this story help us remember?**
- A) That we should never share our lunch
- B) That Jesus can take what feels small and do something wonderful with it when we give it to Him
- C) That crowds are always easy to feed
- D) That the disciples had plenty of money

*Correct: B*  
Wrong answers scaffolding:
- If A: “The little boy shared what he had, and Jesus used it to bless thousands.”
- If C: “It was a huge crowd in a lonely place. Only Jesus could have fed them all.”
- If D: “They had almost nothing at first. Jesus made it more than enough.”
