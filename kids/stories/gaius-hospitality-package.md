# Gaius Shows Hospitality (3 John 1:5)

## Emotional Focus
Being a friend who welcomes travelers.

## Key KJV
“Beloved, thou doest faithfully whatsoever thou doest to the brethren.” (3 John 1:5)

## Gentle Retelling
Gaius opened his home and helped traveling preachers. John said his kindness was faithful and pleasing to God.

## Coloring Prompt
Gaius standing at his door welcoming tired travelers with food and smiles.

## Read-Along Flow + Response
“Welcoming others is a beautiful way to show God’s love.”
