# Story Package: Gideon (Judges 6–7)

**Story Key**: `gideon`

**Position in 365 Roadmap**: Phase 2, Story #9 — God uses the weak and afraid to do great things when they trust Him.

---

## Reverent Read-Aloud Retelling (for ages 3–8)

The Israelite people were in trouble again. The Midianites were mean to them and stole their food. The people hid in caves and cried out to God for help.

One day an angel of the Lord appeared to a young man named Gideon. Gideon was hiding and threshing wheat in a winepress because he was afraid of the enemy.

The angel said, “The Lord is with you, mighty warrior.”

Gideon was surprised. “If the Lord is with us, why are we in so much trouble?”

God told Gideon He was sending him to save Israel. Gideon felt very small and afraid. He said, “My family is the weakest, and I am the least important.”

God promised, “I will be with you.”

To help Gideon believe, God gave him signs. First with a fleece of wool that was wet when the ground was dry, then dry when the ground was wet.

Then God told Gideon to send most of his army home. Only three hundred men stayed. God wanted everyone to know that the victory would come from Him, not from a big army.

That night, Gideon and his small army went to the enemy camp. Each man carried a trumpet, a jar, and a torch inside the jar. At the right moment they broke the jars, blew the trumpets, and shouted, “A sword for the Lord and for Gideon!”

The enemy soldiers were so confused and scared that they ran away and fought each other. God gave Gideon and his tiny army a great victory.

Gideon learned that when we are weak, God can be strong through us.

---

## Coloring Page Prompt

Simple black-and-white line art for ages 3–8. A young man (Gideon) with a small group of men holding trumpets and torches hidden in jars. In the background, a large enemy camp looking confused. Nighttime scene with moon. Clean bold outlines, peaceful and brave feeling. Large open spaces.

---

## Read-Along Flow

Color Gideon with the torch and trumpet → listen to how God uses small, scared people → remember that God’s strength is made perfect in our weakness. Great for when children feel small or afraid.

## Quiz — 5 Questions (with helpful scaffolding)

**1. Why was Gideon hiding when the angel found him?**
- A) He was playing a game
- B) He was afraid of the mean Midianite enemies who were stealing food
- C) He was taking a nap
- D) He was looking for sheep

*Correct: B*  
Wrong answers scaffolding:
- If A: “Gideon wasn’t playing. The people were scared and had to hide their food from the enemy.”
- If C: “He was working hard but in a hidden place because he was afraid.”
- If D: “It wasn’t about sheep. The Midianites were taking all the food, so Gideon was hiding while he worked.”

**2. What did the angel call Gideon?**
- A) “You are very strong and brave already”
- B) “The Lord is with you, mighty warrior”
- C) “You are too weak to help”
- D) “Go away and hide”

*Correct: B*  
Wrong answers scaffolding:
- If A: “Gideon didn’t feel strong or brave at all. The angel called him a mighty warrior because God would make him one.”
- If C: “God saw what Gideon could become with His help, not how weak he felt right then.”
- If D: “God had a big job for Gideon. He called him to be brave with God’s help.”

**3. What signs did God give Gideon with the fleece?**
- A) The fleece was always wet
- B) First the fleece was wet when the ground was dry, then dry when the ground was wet
- C) The fleece turned into gold
- D) The fleece disappeared

*Correct: B*  
Wrong answers scaffolding:
- If A: “God made the fleece wet one night and dry the next to show Gideon He was really speaking.”
- If C: “It wasn’t about gold. The wet and dry fleece helped Gideon believe God’s promise.”
- If D: “The fleece stayed, but it was dry or wet at different times to prove God’s word.”

**4. Why did God tell Gideon to send most of the army home?**
- A) Because the army was too tired
- B) So everyone would know the victory came from God, not from a big army
- C) Because the enemy was already running away
- D) Because Gideon asked for fewer soldiers

*Correct: B*  
Wrong answers scaffolding:
- If A: “The army wasn’t tired. God wanted the people to see that He was the One who won the battle.”
- If C: “The enemy was still there. God reduced the army so the victory would clearly be His doing.”
- If D: “Gideon didn’t ask for this. God chose only 300 men so everyone would trust in Him.”

**5. What did Gideon’s small army do that made the enemy run away?**
- A) They attacked with swords and bows
- B) They broke jars, blew trumpets, held up torches, and shouted “A sword for the Lord and for Gideon!”
- C) They hid and waited quietly
- D) They sent a letter asking for peace

*Correct: B*  
Wrong answers scaffolding:
- If A: “They didn’t fight with weapons. The noise and lights from the jars and trumpets confused the enemy so much they ran and fought each other.”
- If C: “They made a lot of noise on purpose! The surprise and the shout showed God’s power.”
- If D: “There was no letter. God used the unusual plan of breaking jars and blowing trumpets.”
