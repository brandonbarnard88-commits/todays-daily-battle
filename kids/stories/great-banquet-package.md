# The Great Banquet (Luke 14:23)

## Emotional Focus
God invites everyone to His party.

## Key KJV
“Go out into the highways and hedges, and compel them to come in, that my house may be filled.” (Luke 14:23)

## Gentle Retelling
A king invited important people to a big feast. They said no. So he invited the poor, the sick, and everyone else. His house was full!

## Coloring Prompt
Happy people from all walks of life coming to a big feast table.

## Read-Along Flow + Response
“God invites you to His party.”
