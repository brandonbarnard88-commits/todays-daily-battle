# Hezekiah’s Prayer (2 Kings 19:15)

## Emotional Focus
Bringing big worries straight to God.

## Key KJV
“O LORD God of Israel… thou art the God, even thou alone.” (2 Kings 19:15)

## Gentle Retelling
King Hezekiah received a scary letter from enemies. He spread it out before the Lord and prayed. God heard and sent help that very night.

## Coloring Prompt
Hezekiah kneeling in the temple spreading a letter before the altar.

## Read-Along Flow + Response
“You can bring every worry to God — He hears and helps.”
