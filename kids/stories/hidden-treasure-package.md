# The Hidden Treasure (Matthew 13:44)

## Emotional Focus
Finding Jesus and being so happy you give up anything for Him.

## Key KJV
“The kingdom of heaven is like unto treasure hid in a field; the which when a man hath found, he hideth, and for joy thereof goeth and selleth all that he hath, and buyeth that field.” (Matthew 13:44)

## Gentle Retelling
A man found treasure hidden in a field. He was so happy he sold everything he owned to buy the field and keep the treasure.

## Coloring Prompt
A man digging in a field and discovering a chest of shining treasure.

## Read-Along Flow + Response
“Jesus is the greatest treasure.”
