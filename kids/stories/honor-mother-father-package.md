# Honor Your Father and Mother (Exodus 20:12)

## Emotional Focus
Learning to love and respect the people God gave us as family.

## Key KJV
“Honour thy father and thy mother: that thy days may be long upon the land which the LORD thy God giveth thee.” (Exodus 20:12)

## Gentle Retelling
God tells us to honor our father and mother. When we listen to them, help them, and speak kindly, our home feels peaceful and God blesses us.

## Coloring Prompt
A child happily helping mom or dad with simple chores at home; warm smiles and soft light.

## Read-Along Flow + Response
“Honoring our family is one way we show love to God.”
