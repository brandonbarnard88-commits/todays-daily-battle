# God Calls Young Jeremiah (Jeremiah 1:5,7)

## Emotional Focus
God can use you even when you feel too young or small.

## Key KJV
“Before I formed thee in the belly I knew thee… say not, I am a child: for thou shalt go to all that I shall send thee.” (Jeremiah 1:5,7)

## Gentle Retelling (3–8)
God called young Jeremiah to speak for Him. Jeremiah felt too young, but God said, “I knew you before you were born — go, and I will be with you.”
