# Abide in Me (John 15:4)

## Emotional Focus
Staying close to Jesus every day.

## Key KJV
“Abide in me, and I in you.” (John 15:4)

## Gentle Retelling
Jesus said if we stay close to Him like a branch stays on the vine, we will have joy and bear good fruit.

## Coloring Prompt
A strong vine with healthy green branches and sweet grapes.

## Read-along flow + response
“Stay close to Jesus every day — He will stay close to you.”
