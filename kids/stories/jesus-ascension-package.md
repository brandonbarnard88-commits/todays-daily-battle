# The Ascension (Matthew 28:20)

## Emotional Focus
Jesus is in heaven but He is still with us.

## Key KJV
“Lo, I am with you alway, even unto the end of the world.” (Matthew 28:20)

## Gentle Retelling
Jesus rose up into heaven while His friends watched. He promised to be with us always, even to the end.

## Coloring Prompt
Jesus ascending with open arms and soft clouds; disciples looking up in wonder.

## Read-along flow + response
“Jesus is with you every single day.”
