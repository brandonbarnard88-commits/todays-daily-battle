# Jesus’ Baptism (Matthew 3:17)

## Emotional Focus
Jesus did what was right and pleased His Father.

## Key KJV
“This is my beloved Son, in whom I am well pleased.” (Matthew 3:17)

## Gentle Retelling
Jesus went to John to be baptized. As He came up from the water the Holy Spirit came like a dove and God said, “This is my beloved Son.”

## Coloring Prompt
Jesus being baptized in the river with the dove descending and soft light from heaven.

## Read-Along Flow + Response
“God was so pleased with Jesus.”
