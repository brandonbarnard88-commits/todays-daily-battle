# Jesus as a Boy in the Temple (Luke 2:49)

## Emotional Focus
Jesus was once a boy who loved His Father’s house.

## Key KJV
“How is it that ye sought me? wist ye not that I must be about my Father’s business?” (Luke 2:49)

## Gentle Retelling
When Jesus was twelve He stayed in the temple talking about God with the teachers. Mary and Joseph found Him there. Jesus wanted to be in His Father’s house.

## Coloring Prompt
Young Jesus sitting with teachers in the temple, listening and asking questions peacefully.

## Read-Along Flow + Response
“Jesus loved talking about God even when He was a boy.”
