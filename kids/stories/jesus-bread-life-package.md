# The Bread of Life (John 6:35)

## Emotional Focus
Jesus gives us what our hearts really need.

## Key KJV
“I am the bread of life: he that cometh to me shall never hunger.” (John 6:35)

## Gentle Retelling
Jesus said He is the bread of life. When we come to Him our hearts never have to be hungry again.

## Coloring Prompt
Jesus breaking bread with a happy crowd sitting on the grass.

## Read-along flow + response
“Jesus fills the hungry places in our hearts.”
