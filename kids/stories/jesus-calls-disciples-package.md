# Jesus Calls His Disciples (Matthew 4:19)

## Emotional Focus
Being chosen just as you are.

## Key KJV
“Follow me, and I will make you fishers of men.” (Matthew 4:19)

## Gentle Retelling
Jesus walked by the sea and called ordinary fishermen to follow Him. He said, “I will make you…” — He loved them and had a plan for them.

## Coloring Prompt
Jesus calling to fishermen in their boats by the sea; nets and gentle waves.

## Read-Along Flow + Response
“Jesus calls you by name and has good plans for you.”
