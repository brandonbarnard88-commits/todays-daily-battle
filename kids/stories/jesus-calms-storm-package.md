# Jesus Calms the Storm (Mark 4:39)

## Emotional Focus
When life feels stormy and scary.

## Key KJV
“And he arose, and rebuked the wind, and said unto the sea, Peace, be still. And the wind ceased, and there was a great calm.” (Mark 4:39)

## Gentle Retelling
A big storm rocked the boat and the disciples were afraid. Jesus woke up, spoke to the wind and waves, and everything became peaceful.

## Coloring Prompt
Jesus standing in the boat with arms raised while wind and waves become calm and still.

## Read-Along Flow + Response
“Jesus speaks peace to every storm. You can call on Him.”
