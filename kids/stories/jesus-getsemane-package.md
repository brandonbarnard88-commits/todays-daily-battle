# Jesus in Gethsemane (Luke 22:42)

## Emotional Focus
Obeying God even when it’s hard.

## Key KJV
“Not my will, but thine, be done.” (Luke 22:42)

## Gentle Retelling
In the garden Jesus prayed, “Father, if it’s possible, take this away… but not what I want — what You want.” He obeyed and went to the cross for us.

## Coloring Prompt
Jesus kneeling and praying under olive trees in the quiet garden at night.

## Read-Along Flow + Response
“Jesus obeyed for us. We can ask Him to help us obey too.”
