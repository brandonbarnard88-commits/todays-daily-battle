# The Good Shepherd (John 10:14)

## Emotional Focus
Jesus knows you and takes care of you.

## Key KJV
“I am the good shepherd, and know my sheep.” (John 10:14)

## Gentle Retelling
Jesus said He is the good shepherd who knows every lamb by name. He leads us, protects us, and lays down His life for us.

## Coloring Prompt
Smiling shepherd carrying a little lamb on his shoulders across green hills.

## Read-along flow + response
“Jesus is your good shepherd. You are never alone.”
