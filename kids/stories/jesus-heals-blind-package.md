# Jesus Heals a Blind Man (Luke 18:42)

## Emotional Focus
When it feels dark or confusing and you need Jesus to help you see.

## Key KJV
“And Jesus said unto him, Receive thy sight: thy faith hath saved thee.” (Luke 18:42)

## Gentle Retelling
A blind man sat by the road calling out to Jesus. Jesus stopped, touched his eyes, and the man could see! He followed Jesus with a glad heart.

## Coloring Prompt
Blind man kneeling before Jesus with hands gently on his eyes; bright light and joyful smile after healing.

## Read-Along Flow + Response
“Jesus can bring light into any dark place. Call on Him and trust.”
