# Jesus Heals a Deaf Man (Mark 7:34)

## Emotional Focus
When it feels hard to hear or understand and you need Jesus’ touch.

## Key KJV
“Ephphatha, that is, Be opened.” (Mark 7:34)

## Gentle Retelling
A man could not hear or speak clearly. Jesus put His fingers in the man’s ears, looked to heaven, and said, “Be opened.” The man could hear and speak perfectly.

## Coloring Prompt
Jesus gently touching a man’s ears while the man’s face lights up with understanding.

## Read-Along Flow + Response
“Jesus opens ears and hearts. He helps us hear His voice.”
