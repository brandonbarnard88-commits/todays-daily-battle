# Jesus Heals a Leper (Matthew 8:3)

## Emotional Focus
When you feel “different” or unclean and need Jesus to make you new.

## Key KJV
“And Jesus put forth his hand, and touched him, saying, I will; be thou clean.” (Matthew 8:3)

## Gentle Retelling
A man with a skin disease came to Jesus and said, “Lord, if You will, You can make me clean.” Jesus reached out, touched him, and said, “I will; be clean.” Right away the man was healed.

## Coloring Prompt
Jesus gently touching a man who is kneeling with a joyful, clean face; soft light around them.

## Read-Along Flow + Response
“Jesus is willing to make us clean and whole. Come to Him just as you are.”
