# Jesus Heals a Man Born Blind (John 9:7)

## Emotional Focus
When something has been hard since you were little, Jesus can still heal.

## Key KJV
“Go, wash in the pool of Siloam… He went his way therefore, and washed, and came seeing.” (John 9:7)

## Gentle Retelling
A man born blind listened to Jesus. Jesus made mud, put it on his eyes, and told him to wash. When he did, he could see for the first time!

## Coloring Prompt
The man washing his eyes at a pool while bright light and joy fill his face.

## Read-Along Flow + Response
“Jesus can bring sight and new beginnings no matter how long it’s been.”
