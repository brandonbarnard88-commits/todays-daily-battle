# Love Your Enemies (Matthew 5:44)

## Emotional Focus
Loving even when someone is unkind.

## Key KJV
“Love your enemies, bless them that curse you, do good to them that hate you.” (Matthew 5:44)

## Gentle Retelling
Jesus said even when people are mean we can still pray for them and be kind. That is how we show we belong to Him.

## Coloring Prompt
A child offering a flower to someone who looks angry while Jesus smiles gently.

## Read-along flow + response
“Jesus helps us love even when it’s hard.”
