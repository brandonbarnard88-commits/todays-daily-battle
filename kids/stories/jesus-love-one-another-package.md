# Love One Another (John 13:34)

## Emotional Focus
The new commandment Jesus gave us.

## Key KJV
“A new commandment I give unto you, That ye love one another; as I have loved you.” (John 13:34)

## Gentle Retelling
Jesus said the best way to show we love Him is to love each other the same way He loves us.

## Coloring Prompt
Children and friends holding hands in a circle with Jesus in the center smiling.

## Read-along flow + response
“Love one another just like Jesus loves you.”
