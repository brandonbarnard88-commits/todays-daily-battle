# Jesus Raises Lazarus (John 11:25)

## Emotional Focus
When something feels too late or too big for anyone else.

## Key KJV
“Jesus said unto her, I am the resurrection, and the life.” (John 11:25)

## Gentle Retelling
Lazarus had died and been in the tomb four days. Jesus called out, “Lazarus, come forth!” and his friend walked out alive. Jesus showed He is stronger than death.

## Coloring Prompt
Lazarus walking out of the tomb wrapped in cloths while Jesus stands with open arms; Mary and Martha watching in joy.

## Read-Along Flow + Response
“Jesus has power even over death. He brings life and hope.”
