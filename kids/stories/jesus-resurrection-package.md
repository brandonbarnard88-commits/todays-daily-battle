# The Resurrection (Matthew 28:6)

## Emotional Focus
The happiest news — Jesus is alive!

## Key KJV
“He is not here: for he is risen, as he said.” (Matthew 28:6)

## Gentle Retelling
On the third day the tomb was empty. Jesus is alive! He met His friends and showed them He had conquered death.

## Coloring Prompt
Empty tomb with the stone rolled away and soft sunrise light.

## Read-along flow + response
“Jesus is alive and He loves you forever.”
