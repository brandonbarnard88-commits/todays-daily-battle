# Jesus’ Sermon on the Mount (Matthew 5:3)

## Emotional Focus
Jesus teaching us how to live with happy hearts.

## Key KJV
“Blessed are the poor in spirit: for theirs is the kingdom of heaven.” (Matthew 5:3)

## Gentle Retelling
On a hillside Jesus sat with the people and taught them how to live. He said the people who feel small and need God are the ones who are truly happy in His kingdom.

## Coloring Prompt
Jesus sitting on a grassy hill teaching a crowd of happy people.

## Read-Along Flow + Response
“Jesus’ words make our hearts happy when we listen.”
