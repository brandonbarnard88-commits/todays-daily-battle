# Jesus’ Temptation (Luke 4:8)

## Emotional Focus
Jesus understands when we feel pulled in the wrong direction.

## Key KJV
“Get thee behind me, Satan.” (Luke 4:8)

## Gentle Retelling
After Jesus was baptized the enemy tried to trick Him three times. Each time Jesus answered with God’s own words and sent the tempter away. Jesus understands when we feel pulled in the wrong direction.

## Coloring Prompt
Jesus standing strong in a quiet wilderness with open Bible; calm and peaceful.

## Read-Along Flow + Response
“Jesus helps us choose what is right. You can ask Him anytime.”
