# The Transfiguration (Matthew 17:2)

## Emotional Focus
Seeing how bright and wonderful Jesus really is.

## Key KJV
“And his face did shine as the sun, and his raiment was white as the light.” (Matthew 17:2)

## Gentle Retelling
On a mountain Jesus showed Peter, James, and John how bright and wonderful He is. His face shone like the sun.

## Coloring Prompt
Jesus shining with bright light on the mountain while the disciples watch in awe.

## Read-along flow + response
“Jesus is more wonderful than we can imagine.”
