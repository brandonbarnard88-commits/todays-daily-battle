# The Triumphal Entry (Matthew 21:9)

## Emotional Focus
Praising Jesus like the children did.

## Key KJV
“Hosanna to the son of David.” (Matthew 21:9)

## Gentle Retelling
Children and people waved palm branches and shouted “Hosanna!” as Jesus rode into Jerusalem. They were so happy to see Him.

## Coloring Prompt
Children waving palm branches and laying coats on the road while Jesus rides a donkey.

## Read-along flow + response
“We can praise Jesus with happy hearts too.”
