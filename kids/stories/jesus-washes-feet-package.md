# Jesus Washes His Disciples' Feet (John 13:14)

## Emotional Focus
Learning that true friends serve each other with love.

## Key KJV
“If I then, your Lord and Master, have washed your feet; ye also ought to wash one another’s feet.” (John 13:14)

## Gentle Retelling
On the night before He died, Jesus washed His disciples’ dusty feet. He showed them that the greatest love is serving one another like a friend.

## Coloring Prompt
Jesus kneeling with a towel and basin washing a disciple’s feet; soft, humble smiles.

## Read-Along Flow + Response
“Jesus shows us how to be kind friends by helping each other.”
