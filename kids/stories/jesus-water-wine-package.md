# Jesus Turns Water into Wine (John 2:11)

## Emotional Focus
Wonder that Jesus can turn ordinary things into something beautiful and good.

## Key KJV
“This beginning of miracles did Jesus in Cana of Galilee, and manifested forth his glory.” (John 2:11)

## Gentle Retelling
At a wedding the wine ran out. Jesus told the servants to fill big jars with water. When they poured it out, it had become the very best wine. Jesus showed His friends His power and care.

## Coloring Prompt
Jesus at a wedding table with servants pouring water from jars that turns into wine; happy faces and colorful jars.

## Read-Along Flow + Response
“Jesus can take what we have and make it more than enough. He is powerful and kind.”
