# Love One Another (1 John 4:7)

## Emotional Focus
The simple command to be kind and love like Jesus.

## Key KJV
“Beloved, let us love one another: for love is of God.” (1 John 4:7)

## Gentle Retelling
John wrote to his friends and said the best way to show we know Jesus is to love one another. Kindness is how we show God’s love.

## Coloring Prompt
Children and friends holding hands in a circle with Jesus in the center smiling.

## Read-Along Flow + Response
“Let’s love one another just like Jesus loves us.”
