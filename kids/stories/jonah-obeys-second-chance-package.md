# Jonah Obeys the Second Time (Jonah 3:1)

## Emotional Focus
Coming back to obey after running away.

## Key KJV
“And the word of the LORD came unto Jonah the second time.” (Jonah 3:1)

## Gentle Retelling
Jonah tried to run from God, but after the big fish God gave him a second chance. This time Jonah obeyed and went to the city God said.

## Coloring Prompt
Jonah standing on the shore looking thankful after being let go by the fish.

## Read-Along Flow + Response
“God gives second chances when we come back to obey.”
