# Jonah’s Prayer Inside the Fish (Jonah 2:2)

## Emotional Focus
Praying even when we’ve made a mistake.

## Key KJV
“I called by reason of mine affliction unto the LORD, and he heard me.” (Jonah 2:2)

## Gentle Retelling
Inside the big fish Jonah prayed and told God he was sorry. God heard him and gave him a second chance.

## Coloring Prompt
Jonah inside the big fish (gentle and calm) praying with hope.

## Read-Along Flow + Response
“God hears us even when we’ve run the wrong way.”
