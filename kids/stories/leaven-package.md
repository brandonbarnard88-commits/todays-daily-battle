# The Leaven (Matthew 13:33)

## Emotional Focus
How a little bit of God’s kingdom grows big in our hearts.

## Key KJV
“The kingdom of heaven is like unto leaven, which a woman took, and hid in three measures of meal, till the whole was leavened.” (Matthew 13:33)

## Gentle Retelling
A woman mixed a little yeast into flour. It worked quietly until the whole loaf was light and fluffy. God’s kingdom grows like that in our hearts.

## Coloring Prompt
A woman mixing dough while it rises big and fluffy.

## Read-Along Flow + Response
“A little of Jesus in your heart grows into something wonderful.”
