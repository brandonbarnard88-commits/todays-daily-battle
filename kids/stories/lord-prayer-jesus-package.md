# The Lord’s Prayer (Matthew 6:9)

## Emotional Focus
Learning the simple, loving way Jesus taught us to talk to God.

## Key KJV
“After this manner therefore pray ye: Our Father which art in heaven, Hallowed be thy name.” (Matthew 6:9)

## Gentle Retelling
Jesus sat with His friends and taught them a beautiful prayer. He said we can call God “Our Father” and talk to Him about everything — our needs, forgiveness, and keeping us safe.

## Coloring Prompt
Jesus sitting peacefully with His disciples, hands folded in prayer under a soft sky.

## Read-Along Flow + Response
“You can talk to God like Jesus taught — He is your loving Father.”
