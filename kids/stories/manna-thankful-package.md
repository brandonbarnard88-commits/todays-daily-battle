# Manna from Heaven (Exodus 16:15)

## Emotional Focus
Learning to say thank You for daily blessings.

## Key KJV
“This is the bread which the LORD hath given you to eat.” (Exodus 16:15)

## Gentle Retelling
Every morning God sent fresh manna for His people. They gathered just what they needed for that day and said thank You. God still gives us what we need each day.

## Coloring Prompt
People gathering white manna flakes on the desert ground with happy, thankful faces.

## Read-Along Flow + Response
“Thank You, God, for today’s blessings.”
