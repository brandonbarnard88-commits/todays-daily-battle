# Mary Anoints Jesus (Mark 14:8)

## Emotional Focus
Showing love and kindness in a special way.

## Key KJV
“She hath done what she could.” (Mark 14:8)

## Gentle Retelling
Mary poured expensive perfume on Jesus’ feet because she loved Him so much. Jesus said her kind act would always be remembered.

## Coloring Prompt
Mary gently pouring perfume from a jar onto Jesus’ feet; peaceful and loving scene.

## Read-Along Flow + Response
“Small acts of kindness from the heart please Jesus.”
