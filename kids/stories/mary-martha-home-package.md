# Mary and Martha at Home (Luke 10:41-42)

## Emotional Focus
When the house feels busy and hearts feel worried.

## Key KJV
“Martha, Martha, thou art careful and troubled about many things: but one thing is needful.” (Luke 10:41-42)

## Gentle Retelling
Martha was rushing to make everything perfect for Jesus. Mary sat quietly listening to Him. Jesus said being with Him is the most important thing in the home.

## Coloring Prompt
Mary sitting peacefully at Jesus’ feet while Martha works gently in the background.

## Read-Along Flow + Response
“It’s okay to stop and just be with Jesus at home.”
