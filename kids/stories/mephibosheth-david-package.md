# Mephibosheth and King David (2 Samuel 9:7)

## Emotional Focus
Showing unexpected kindness to someone who feels forgotten.

## Key KJV
“I will surely shew thee kindness for Jonathan thy father’s sake.” (2 Samuel 9:7)

## Gentle Retelling
Mephibosheth was scared because he was from the old king’s family, but King David invited him to eat at the palace table forever because of his friend Jonathan.

## Coloring Prompt
Mephibosheth sitting at a royal table with David smiling kindly.

## Read-Along Flow + Response
“God’s kindness can surprise us when we feel left out.”
