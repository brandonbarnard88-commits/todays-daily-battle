# Moses and the Red Sea (Exodus 14:21)

## Emotional Focus
Obeying God when there seems to be no way.

## Key KJV
“And Moses stretched out his hand over the sea.” (Exodus 14:21)

## Gentle Retelling
God told Moses to hold out his staff over the Red Sea. Moses obeyed and God opened a dry path through the water for His people.

## Coloring Prompt
Moses holding his staff while tall walls of water stand on both sides of a dry path.

## Read-Along Flow + Response
“Obey God and watch Him make a way.”
