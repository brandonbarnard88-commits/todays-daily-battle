# God Opens the Red Sea (Exodus 14:21-22)

## Emotional Focus
Trusting God when there seems to be no way out.

## Key KJV
“And the LORD caused the sea to go back by a strong east wind all that night, and made the sea dry land, and the waters were a wall unto them on their right hand, and on their left.” (Exodus 14:21-22)

## Gentle Retelling (3–8)
God’s people were trapped between the sea and Pharaoh’s army. Moses told them not to be afraid. God made a dry path through the sea, and they walked safely between walls of water. God saved them.
