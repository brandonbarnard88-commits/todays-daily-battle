# Naaman Dips in the Jordan (2 Kings 5:10)

## Emotional Focus
Doing what God says even when it seems too simple.

## Key KJV
“Go and wash in Jordan seven times… and thou shalt be clean.” (2 Kings 5:10)

## Gentle Retelling
Naaman had a bad skin disease. Elisha said to dip in the Jordan River seven times. Naaman obeyed and came out completely clean and happy.

## Coloring Prompt
Naaman coming out of the river with clean, happy skin while family cheers.

## Read-Along Flow + Response
“Simple obedience brings big blessings from God.”
