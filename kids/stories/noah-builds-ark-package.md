# Noah Builds the Ark (Genesis 6:22)

## Emotional Focus
Obeying God even when others don’t understand.

## Key KJV
“Thus did Noah; according to all that God commanded him, so did he.” (Genesis 6:22)

## Gentle Retelling
God told Noah to build a big ark because a flood was coming. Noah obeyed even though it seemed strange. He and his family were safe inside when the rain came.

## Coloring Prompt
Noah and his family happily building the big wooden ark with animals watching nearby.

## Read-Along Flow + Response
“Obeying God keeps us safe and close to Him.”
