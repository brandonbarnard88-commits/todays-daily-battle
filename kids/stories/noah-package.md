## Reverent Read-Aloud Retelling (for ages 3–8)

God saw that the people on the earth were doing many wrong things. It made His heart sad. But one man, Noah, loved God and walked with Him. God told Noah to build a very big boat — an ark — because He was going to send a great flood to cover the earth.

Noah obeyed. He and his family worked for many years building the ark. They brought two of every kind of animal — the lions, the birds, the elephants, even the little bugs — and plenty of food for everyone.

Then the rain began to fall. It rained for forty days and forty nights. The water covered the highest mountains. But the ark floated safely on the water with Noah, his family, and all the animals inside.

After many months the rain stopped and the water went down. Noah sent out a dove. The dove came back with a fresh olive leaf in its beak — a sign that the land was growing green again.

At last the ark came to rest on a mountain. Noah and his family and all the animals came out. God put a beautiful rainbow in the sky and made a promise: “Never again will I send a flood to cover the whole earth.” The rainbow would always be the sign of that promise.

Noah built an altar and thanked God. God blessed Noah and his family and told them to fill the earth again with life.

## Quiz — 4 Questions (with helpful scaffolding)

**1. Why did God tell Noah to build the ark?**
- A) Because it was going to rain a little
- B) Because the people were doing many wrong things and God was sending a flood
- C) Because Noah wanted a big boat
- D) Because the animals needed a new home

*Correct: B*  
Wrong answers scaffolding:
- If A: “It wasn’t just a little rain. God was sending a great flood because of the wrong things people were doing.”
- If C: “Noah didn’t ask for a boat. God told him to build it to save his family and the animals.”
- If D: “The animals needed saving too, but the main reason was the flood because of the people’s sin.”

**2. How many days and nights did it rain?**
- A) Seven days and nights
- B) Twenty days and nights
- C) Forty days and nights
- D) One hundred days and nights

*Correct: C*  
Wrong answers scaffolding:
- If A: “It rained much longer than a week. The Bible says it rained for forty days and forty nights.”
- If B: “It was longer than twenty days. It rained for forty days and forty nights.”
- If D: “It was forty days and nights — a long time, but not one hundred.”

**3. What did the dove bring back to Noah that showed the land was growing again?**
- A) A leaf from an olive tree
- B) A flower
- C) A piece of wood
- D) Some grass

*Correct: A*  
Wrong answers scaffolding:
- If B: “It wasn’t a flower. The dove brought back a fresh olive leaf — a sign of new life.”
- If C: “It wasn’t wood. The olive leaf showed that trees were growing again.”
- If D: “It was a specific sign — a fresh olive leaf from a living tree.”

**4. What did God put in the sky as a promise never to flood the whole earth again?**
- A) A bright star
- B) A rainbow
- C) A cloud shaped like a dove
- D) The sun

*Correct: B*  
Wrong answers scaffolding:
- If A: “God gave a rainbow, not just a star. The rainbow is the special sign of His promise.”
- If C: “It was a rainbow — the beautiful sign we still see after rain.”
- If D: “The sun was already there. God gave the rainbow as His promise sign.”
