# The Pearl of Great Price (Matthew 13:45-46)

## Emotional Focus
Knowing Jesus is worth everything.

## Key KJV
“Again, the kingdom of heaven is like unto a merchant man, seeking goodly pearls: Who, when he had found one pearl of great price, went and sold all that he had, and bought it.” (Matthew 13:45-46)

## Gentle Retelling
A man found one beautiful pearl that was worth more than everything else. He sold all he had just to get it. Jesus is like that pearl — worth everything.

## Coloring Prompt
A happy merchant holding one shining pearl while selling his other treasures.

## Read-Along Flow + Response
“Jesus is the best treasure of all.”
