# Peter Obeys with the Net (Luke 5:5)

## Emotional Focus
Trying again when Jesus says to.

## Key KJV
“Nevertheless at thy word I will let down the net.” (Luke 5:5)

## Gentle Retelling
Peter had fished all night with no fish. Jesus said, “Let down the net.” Peter obeyed and caught so many fish the boat almost sank.

## Coloring Prompt
Peter pulling a net full of fish into the boat while Jesus watches kindly.

## Read-Along Flow + Response
“When Jesus says try again, obey — He has good things ready.”
