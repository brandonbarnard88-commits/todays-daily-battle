# The Church Prays for Peter (Acts 12:5)

## Emotional Focus
When the whole church prays for someone.

## Key KJV
“Prayer was made without ceasing of the church unto God for him.” (Acts 12:5)

## Gentle Retelling
The church prayed for Peter while he was in prison. God sent an angel and set him free. Prayer really works.

## Coloring Prompt
People praying together in a house while an angel helps Peter walk out of prison.

## Read-Along Flow + Response
“When we pray together, God does wonderful things.”
