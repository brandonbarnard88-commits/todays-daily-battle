# Philemon and Onesimus (Philemon 1:16)

## Emotional Focus
Forgiving a friend who made a mistake and welcoming them home.

## Key KJV
“Receive him… not now as a servant, but above a servant, a brother beloved.” (Philemon 1:16)

## Gentle Retelling
Onesimus ran away from his master Philemon, but Paul sent him back with a letter asking Philemon to forgive and treat him like a brother. Philemon chose kindness.

## Coloring Prompt
Onesimus returning home while Philemon opens his arms to welcome him; happy reunion.

## Read-Along Flow + Response
“Forgiveness makes room for friendship again.”
