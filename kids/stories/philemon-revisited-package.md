# Philemon Forgives and Welcomes (Philemon 1:16)

## Emotional Focus
Choosing forgiveness and making a friend part of the family.

## Key KJV
“Receive him… as a brother beloved.” (Philemon 1:16)

## Gentle Retelling
Philemon forgave Onesimus and welcomed him home like family. Forgiveness turned a servant into a brother.
