# The Father Welcomes His Son Home (Luke 15:24)

## Emotional Focus
When someone in the family comes home after being away.

## Key KJV
“For this my son was dead, and is alive again; he was lost, and is found.” (Luke 15:24)

## Gentle Retelling
A son left home but came back sorry. His father ran to meet him, hugged him tight, and celebrated. That’s how God welcomes us back too.

## Coloring Prompt
A joyful father running with open arms to hug his returning son on the road.

## Read-Along Flow + Response
“Home is always waiting with love when we come back.”
