# God Builds the House (Psalm 127:1)

## Emotional Focus
Knowing God builds the home.

## Key KJV
“Except the LORD build the house, they labour in vain that build it.” (Psalm 127:1)

## Gentle Retelling
The psalm says unless the Lord builds the house, the work is empty. When we ask God to be part of our home, everything feels stronger and happier.

## Coloring Prompt
A simple cozy house with a family inside and soft light shining from the windows.

## Read-Along Flow + Response
“God is the One who makes a house a real home.”
