# Psalm 133 – Family Living in Unity (Psalm 133:1)

## Emotional Focus
When brothers and sisters get along, home feels like heaven.

## Key KJV
“Behold, how good and how pleasant it is for brethren to dwell together in unity!” (Psalm 133:1)

## Gentle Retelling
The psalm says it is good and pleasant when family lives together in peace. It feels like oil on the head or dew on the mountains — refreshing and beautiful.

## Coloring Prompt
Brothers and sisters playing and laughing together in a sunny yard.

## Read-Along Flow + Response
“When we get along, our home feels like God’s blessing.”
