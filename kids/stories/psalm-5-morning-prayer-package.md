# Morning Prayer (Psalm 5:3)

## Emotional Focus
Starting the day by talking to God.

## Key KJV
“My voice shalt thou hear in the morning, O LORD; in the morning will I direct my prayer unto thee.” (Psalm 5:3)

## Gentle Retelling
The psalm says we can talk to God first thing in the morning. He listens and helps us all day long.

## Coloring Prompt
A child kneeling by the window in soft morning light, talking to God.

## Read-Along Flow + Response
“Talk to God in the morning — He is ready to listen.”
