# Cast Your Burden (Psalm 55:22)

## Emotional Focus
Giving our burdens to God at the end of the day.

## Key KJV
“Cast thy burden upon the LORD, and he shall sustain thee.” (Psalm 55:22)

## Gentle Retelling
The psalm says we can give every heavy thing to God. He will hold us up and give us rest.

## Coloring Prompt
A child giving a small bundle to Jesus with peaceful relief.

## Read-Along Flow + Response
“Give every worry to God — He will carry it for you.”
