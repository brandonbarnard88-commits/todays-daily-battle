# Safe in God’s Care (Psalm 91:1-2)

## Emotional Focus
Trusting God to keep us safe when we feel scared.

## Key KJV
“He that dwelleth in the secret place of the most High shall abide under the shadow of the Almighty.” (Psalm 91:1)

## Gentle Retelling (3–8)
When we stay close to God, we live under His shadow like a little bird under a mother’s wing. He is our refuge and fortress. We can call on Him, and He answers.
