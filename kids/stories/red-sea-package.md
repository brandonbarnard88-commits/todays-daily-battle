# Story Package: The Red Sea (Exodus 14)

**Story Key**: `redSea`

**Position in 365 Roadmap**: Phase 2, Story #7 — God makes a way when there seems to be no way.

---

## Reverent Read-Aloud Retelling (for ages 3–8)

After God sent the ten plagues, Pharaoh finally let the Israelite people leave Egypt. They walked and walked until they came to the edge of the Red Sea. Suddenly they heard a terrible sound — Pharaoh had changed his mind! His army with horses and chariots was chasing after them.

The people were very afraid. In front of them was a huge sea. Behind them were the angry soldiers. There was nowhere to go.

But Moses told the people, “Do not be afraid. Stand firm and you will see the Lord save you today.”

Then God told Moses to lift his staff over the sea. A strong wind began to blow all night. In the morning the water divided into two great walls, and a dry path opened right through the middle of the sea!

The Israelite people walked safely between the walls of water. When the Egyptian army tried to follow them, the waters came crashing back together. God had saved His people in an amazing way. They sang and praised the Lord for His great power and love.

---

## Coloring Page Prompt

Dramatic but calm black-and-white line art for ages 3–8. A huge path of dry ground between two tall walls of water. Israelite families walking safely with their animals. In the distance, the Egyptian army. Moses standing with his staff raised. Hopeful and miraculous feeling. Clean lines, good open space.

---

## Read-Along Flow

Color the walls of water and the dry path → listen to how God makes a way when there is no way → remember that nothing is too hard for God.

## Quiz — 4 Questions (with helpful scaffolding)

**1. Why were the Israelite people afraid when they reached the Red Sea?**
- A) They were tired from walking
- B) The sea was in front of them and Pharaoh’s army was chasing them from behind
- C) They didn’t have enough food
- D) It was too hot

*Correct: B*  
Wrong answers scaffolding:
- If A: “They were more than tired. They were trapped between the sea and the angry soldiers.”
- If C: “Food was not the biggest problem right then. They had nowhere to run.”
- If D: “The real danger was the army coming after them with no escape.”

**2. What did God do to make a path for His people?**
- A) He dried up the whole sea forever
- B) He sent a strong wind that made the water stand up like two walls with dry ground in between
- C) He made a bridge appear
- D) He turned the water into sand

*Correct: B*  
Wrong answers scaffolding:
- If A: “The sea didn’t disappear forever. God made a special path just for that moment.”
- If C: “It wasn’t a bridge. The water itself stood up on both sides like giant walls.”
- If D: “The ground stayed dry in the middle while the water stood tall on either side.”

**3. What did Moses tell the people when they were scared?**
- A) “Run as fast as you can!”
- B) “Do not be afraid. Stand firm and you will see the Lord save you today.”
- C) “We should go back to Egypt.”
- D) “Hide in the rocks.”

*Correct: B*  
Wrong answers scaffolding:
- If A: “There was nowhere to run. Moses told them to trust God and watch what He would do.”
- If C: “Going back was not God’s plan. Moses called them to stand still and trust.”
- If D: “Hiding wouldn’t have helped. God was about to do something they had never seen before.”

**4. What did the people do after God saved them from the sea?**
- A) They complained again
- B) They sang songs of praise and thanked the Lord for His great power
- C) They went back to fight Pharaoh
- D) They forgot all about it

*Correct: B*  
Wrong answers scaffolding:
- If A: “This time they were full of joy. They sang a beautiful song of thanks to God.”
- If C: “They didn’t need to fight. God had already won the victory for them.”
- If D: “They remembered this day for a long time and praised God for saving them.”
