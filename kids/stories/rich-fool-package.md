# The Rich Fool (Luke 12:20)

## Emotional Focus
Remembering what really matters.

## Key KJV
“Thou fool, this night thy soul shall be required of thee.” (Luke 12:20)

## Gentle Retelling
A rich man had so much he tore down his barns to build bigger ones. God called him a fool because he forgot to be rich toward God.

## Coloring Prompt
A man looking at huge full barns with a surprised face.

## Read-Along Flow + Response
“Be rich toward God, not just things.”
