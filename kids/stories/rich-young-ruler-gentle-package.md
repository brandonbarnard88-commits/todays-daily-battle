# The Rich Young Ruler (Matthew 19:21)

## Emotional Focus
Listening when Jesus asks us to let go of something.

## Key KJV
“If thou wilt be perfect, go and sell that thou hast, and give to the poor.” (Matthew 19:21)

## Gentle Retelling
A young man asked Jesus what to do. Jesus said to sell what he had and follow Him. The man went away sad because he loved his things more.

## Coloring Prompt
Young man walking away sadly while Jesus looks on with kind, open arms.

## Read-Along Flow + Response
“Jesus knows what is best. We can ask Him to help us listen.”
