# Story Package: Ruth and Boaz (Ruth 1–4)

**Story Key**: `ruthBoaz`

**Position in 365 Roadmap**: Phase 3, Story #10 — God’s kindness and redemption through loyal love.

---

## Reverent Read-Aloud Retelling (for ages 3–8)

There was a famine in the land, so Naomi and her husband and two sons moved to another country. While they were there, Naomi’s husband died, and later both of her sons died too. Naomi was very sad and decided to go back home to Bethlehem.

Her daughter-in-law Ruth loved Naomi very much. Ruth said, “Where you go I will go, and where you stay I will stay. Your people will be my people and your God my God.”

The two women walked a long way back to Bethlehem. They were poor and had no food. Ruth went out to the fields to pick up the grain that the workers left behind.

The owner of the field was a kind man named Boaz. He noticed Ruth and was very kind to her. He told his workers to leave extra grain for her and to make sure she was safe.

Boaz was a relative of Naomi’s family. In those days there was a special law that a close relative could marry a widow to take care of her. Boaz chose to do this for Ruth.

Boaz and Ruth got married and had a baby boy named Obed. Naomi was so happy to hold her grandson. God had turned their sadness into joy.

Many years later, Jesus would be born into the family line that came from Ruth and Boaz. God used Ruth’s loyal love to be part of His wonderful plan.

---

## Coloring Page Prompt

Warm black-and-white line art for ages 3–8. Two women walking together on a path (Naomi and Ruth). Ruth gathering grain in a field. A kind older man (Boaz) watching over her. A happy grandmother holding a baby. Gentle, loving, and hopeful feeling. Clean lines with plenty of open space.

---

## Read-Along Flow

Color the journey and the kind man in the field → listen to the story of loyal love and God’s kindness → remember that God sees and cares for people who love and help each other.

## Quiz — 4 Questions (with helpful scaffolding)

**1. What did Ruth say to Naomi when Naomi wanted to go home alone?**
- A) “Go ahead, I will stay here”
- B) “Where you go I will go, and your God will be my God”
- C) “I am too tired to walk”
- D) “I will find a new family”

*Correct: B*  
Wrong answers scaffolding:
- If A: “Ruth didn’t want to stay. She loved Naomi and chose to go with her and follow her God.”
- If C: “Ruth was willing to walk the long road. She promised to stay with Naomi no matter what.”
- If D: “Ruth chose Naomi’s family and Naomi’s God. She showed beautiful loyal love.”

**2. Why was Ruth out in the field gathering grain?**
- A) She was playing a game
- B) She and Naomi were poor and needed food
- C) She liked being outside
- D) The king asked her to

*Correct: B*  
Wrong answers scaffolding:
- If A: “It wasn’t a game. They had no money for food, so Ruth worked hard picking up leftover grain.”
- If C: “She was working to take care of Naomi. They were very poor after their husbands died.”
- If D: “No one asked her. She chose to work so she and Naomi would have something to eat.”

**3. How did Boaz show kindness to Ruth?**
- A) He told her to leave his field
- B) He made sure she had plenty of grain, water, and safety while she worked
- C) He gave her money and sent her away
- D) He pretended not to see her

*Correct: B*  
Wrong answers scaffolding:
- If A: “Boaz welcomed her. He was kind and protective toward Ruth.”
- If C: “He didn’t just give money and send her away. He made sure she had what she needed and was safe.”
- If D: “Boaz noticed her and went out of his way to be generous and kind.”

**4. What wonderful thing happened because Ruth stayed loyal to Naomi?**
- A) They became very rich with lots of gold
- B) Ruth married Boaz, they had a baby, and Jesus was born many years later into their family line
- C) They moved to a big city
- D) The king gave them a palace

*Correct: B*  
Wrong answers scaffolding:
- If A: “They didn’t get rich with gold. God gave them something much better — a family and a place in the story of Jesus.”
- If C: “They stayed in Bethlehem. God blessed them with love and a baby boy named Obed.”
- If D: “God’s blessing was a loving family and being part of the family Jesus would come from.”
