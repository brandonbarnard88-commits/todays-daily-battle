# Ruth and Naomi Find a New Home (Ruth 1:16)

## Emotional Focus
Family that sticks together even when life changes.

## Key KJV
“Whither thou goest, I will go… thy people shall be my people.” (Ruth 1:16)

## Gentle Retelling
Ruth chose to stay with her mother-in-law Naomi and go to a new home. God gave them both a safe place and a new family.

## Coloring Prompt
Ruth walking beside Naomi on a path to a new home, carrying a small bundle.

## Read-Along Flow + Response
“God can make a new home when we stay together.”
