# The Salt of the Earth (Matthew 5:13)

## Emotional Focus
Being salty — making the world better like Jesus.

## Key KJV
“Ye are the salt of the earth.” (Matthew 5:13)

## Gentle Retelling
Jesus said His friends are like salt. Salt makes food taste good and keeps it from spoiling. We can help make the world better by loving like Jesus.

## Coloring Prompt
A child sprinkling salt on food while smiling happily.

## Read-Along Flow + Response
“You can be like salt and make things better.”
