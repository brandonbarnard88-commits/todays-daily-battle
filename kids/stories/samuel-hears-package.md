# Story Package: Samuel Hears God (1 Samuel 3)

**Story Key**: `samuelHears`

**Position in 365 Roadmap**: Phase 3, Story #11 — God speaks to children who are ready to listen.

---

## Reverent Read-Aloud Retelling (for ages 3–8)

When Samuel was a little boy, his mother Hannah brought him to live at the temple with the priest Eli. Samuel helped Eli by taking care of the lamps and doing other jobs in God’s house.

One night while Samuel was sleeping, he heard someone call his name: “Samuel! Samuel!”

Samuel ran to Eli and said, “Here I am. You called me.”

Eli said, “I did not call you. Go back to bed.”

This happened three times. Each time Samuel ran to Eli.

Finally Eli understood. He told Samuel, “If you hear the voice again, say, ‘Speak, Lord, for your servant is listening.’”

The fourth time the Lord called, Samuel answered just as Eli had taught him: “Speak, Lord, for your servant is listening.”

God spoke to Samuel and gave him an important message for the people. From that time on, Samuel became a prophet who listened to God and told the people what God said.

Even as a child, Samuel learned to listen carefully to God’s voice.

## Coloring Page Prompt

Gentle black-and-white line art for ages 3–8. A young boy lying on a mat in the temple at night. An older priest (Eli) sitting nearby. A soft lamp glowing. The boy looking attentive as if hearing a voice. Peaceful nighttime scene with stars outside. Clean lines, calm and holy feeling.

---

## Read-Along Flow

Color the sleeping boy and the temple → listen to the story of a child who learned to hear God → encourage children that God can speak to them too when they listen with open hearts.

## Quiz — 4 Questions (with helpful scaffolding)

**1. Where did young Samuel live and work?**
- A) In a big palace with the king
- B) In the temple helping the priest Eli
- C) On a farm with his family
- D) In a cave in the mountains

*Correct: B*  
Wrong answers scaffolding:
- If A: “Samuel didn’t live in a palace. His mother brought him to God’s house to serve the priest Eli.”
- If C: “He was no longer on the farm. He lived at the temple to help with God’s work.”
- If D: “The temple was where Samuel learned to serve God from the time he was very young.”

**2. What did Samuel do the first three times he heard his name at night?**
- A) He stayed in bed and went back to sleep
- B) He ran to Eli and said, “Here I am. You called me.”
- C) He hid under his blanket
- D) He shouted for his mother

*Correct: B*  
Wrong answers scaffolding:
- If A: “Samuel thought Eli was calling him, so he got up right away to see what Eli needed.”
- If C: “He wasn’t scared. He got up quickly because he wanted to help.”
- If D: “His mother wasn’t there. Eli was the one taking care of him at the temple.”

**3. What did Eli finally tell Samuel to say the next time he heard the voice?**
- A) “Go away, I’m sleeping!”
- B) “Speak, Lord, for your servant is listening.”
- C) “Who are you?”
- D) “I’m busy right now.”

*Correct: B*  
Wrong answers scaffolding:
- If A: “Samuel was respectful. Eli taught him to answer God with an open and willing heart.”
- If C: “He already knew it was God after the third time. Eli helped him know how to reply.”
- If D: “Samuel was ready to listen. Eli showed him the right way to answer God’s call.”

**4. What happened after Samuel answered God the right way?**
- A) He went back to sleep and forgot everything
- B) God spoke to him and gave him an important message to share with the people
- C) Nothing happened and he was disappointed
- D) He became the king

*Correct: B*  
Wrong answers scaffolding:
- If A: “Samuel listened carefully. God gave him a message that he then shared with the people.”
- If C: “God did speak! Samuel became a prophet because he was willing to listen.”
- If D: “Samuel didn’t become king. He became a prophet who helped the people hear from God.”
