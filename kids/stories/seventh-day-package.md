# The Seventh Day (Genesis 2:1-3)

## Bible Story Retelling

God made the heaven and the earth in six days. On the seventh day He ended His work and rested. He blessed the seventh day and made it holy, because on it He rested from all the work He had created and made. Everything God made was very good, and He gave us a gentle day to rest with Him.

## Coloring Page Prompt

Create one simple black-and-white line-art coloring page for ages 3–8. Clean bold outlines, large open spaces. Peaceful scene: a gentle sunset over the finished creation with tall trees, flowers, a quiet stream, and a smiling child sitting peacefully beside a lamb. Focus on the heart moment of God resting and blessing the day. Calm, joyful, reverent.

## Read-Along Flow

Child colors the page. Tap “I’m finished coloring” → gentle transition to the read-aloud retelling with natural pauses. Ends with peaceful loop or “Next Story in the Gentle Journey.”
