# Story Package: The Parable of the Sower (Matthew 13, Mark 4, Luke 8)

**Story Key**: `parableSower`

**Position in 365 Roadmap**: Phase 5, Story #16 — How our hearts receive God’s Word.

---

## Reverent Read-Aloud Retelling (for ages 3–8)

Jesus told this story: A farmer went out to plant seeds. As he scattered the seed, some fell on the hard path. Birds came and ate it up right away.

Some seed fell on rocky ground where there was not much soil. The plants sprang up quickly, but when the sun got hot, they withered because they had no deep roots.

Other seed fell among thorns. The thorns grew up and choked the plants so they could not grow well.

But some seed fell on good soil. It grew and produced a wonderful harvest — some thirty times, some sixty, some a hundred times what was planted.

Later, Jesus explained the story to His disciples: The seed is God’s Word. The different soils are the different ways people’s hearts receive it.

The hard path is a heart that hears God’s Word but does not understand it, so the enemy quickly takes it away.

The rocky ground is a heart that hears the Word and gets excited at first, but when trouble comes, it gives up because the Word never went deep.

The thorny ground is a heart that hears the Word, but worries and love for other things choke it out.

The good soil is a heart that hears the Word, understands it, and lets it grow and produce good fruit in their life.

Simple black-and-white line art for ages 3–8. A farmer scattering seeds with his hand. Four clear sections: a hard path with birds eating seeds, rocky ground with small plants drying up, thorny ground with plants being choked, and rich soil with tall healthy plants full of grain. Clean, peaceful teaching scene. Bold outlines, large open spaces.

## Read-Along Flow

Color the four kinds of soil → listen to Jesus explain how our hearts receive God’s Word → gentle invitation to ask God to make our hearts “good soil” that listens and grows.

## Quiz — 4 Questions (with helpful scaffolding)

**1. In the story, what does the seed stand for?**
- A) The farmer’s tools
- B) God’s Word
- C) The birds
- D) The sun

*Correct: B*  
Wrong answers scaffolding:
- If A: “The seed is not the tools. Jesus said the seed is God’s Word being planted in hearts.”
- If C: “The birds are the enemy who tries to steal the Word. The seed itself is God’s Word.”
- If D: “The sun represents trouble or hard times. The seed is God’s Word.”

**2. What happened to the seed that fell on the hard path?**
- A) It grew into tall plants
- B) Birds came and ate it up right away
- C) It was choked by thorns
- D) It grew but had no grain

*Correct: B*  
Wrong answers scaffolding:
- If A: “The hard path had no soil, so nothing could grow. The birds took the seed immediately.”
- If C: “The thorns were in a different part of the field. The path seed was eaten by birds.”
- If D: “Nothing grew on the path because the seed never even started.”

**3. Why did the plants in the rocky ground die?**
- A) The birds ate them
- B) They had no deep roots, so when the sun came they dried up
- C) The farmer forgot to water them
- D) They were planted too late

*Correct: B*  
Wrong answers scaffolding:
- If A: “The birds only took the seed on the path. The rocky plants grew a little but then died from the heat.”
- If C: “The problem was not water. The soil was too shallow for roots to grow deep.”
- If D: “The timing wasn’t the issue. The plants had no deep roots to hold them when trouble came.”

**4. What kind of heart does Jesus want us to have?**
- A) A heart like the hard path
- B) A heart like the good soil that hears God’s Word, understands it, and lets it grow and produce good fruit
- C) A heart full of thorns and worries
- D) A heart that gets excited for a little while then forgets

*Correct: B*  
Wrong answers scaffolding:
- If A: “The hard path heart doesn’t let the Word sink in. Jesus wants our hearts to be soft and ready.”
- If C: “Thorns are worries and other things that crowd out God’s Word. Jesus wants a clean, good heart.”
- If D: “That is the rocky heart. Jesus wants a heart where His Word goes deep and stays.”
