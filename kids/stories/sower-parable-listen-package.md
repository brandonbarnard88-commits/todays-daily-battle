# The Sower and Listening (Matthew 13:23)

## Emotional Focus
Listening to God’s Word with a soft heart.

## Key KJV
“But he that received seed into the good ground is he that heareth the word, and understandeth it.” (Matthew 13:23)

## Gentle Retelling
Jesus told about seed falling on different ground. The good ground listened to God’s Word and it grew strong and beautiful.

## Coloring Prompt
Soft soil with tall green plants growing happily under sunshine.

## Read-Along Flow + Response
“Let God’s Word grow in your heart by listening.”
