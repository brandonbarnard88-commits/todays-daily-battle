# Tabitha (Dorcas) and Her Friends (Acts 9:39)

## Emotional Focus
Friends who help when someone is sick or sad.

## Key KJV
“And all the widows stood by him weeping, and shewing the coats and garments which Dorcas made.” (Acts 9:39)

## Gentle Retelling
When Dorcas died, her friends showed Peter all the clothes she had sewn for them. Their love and Peter’s prayer brought her back.

## Coloring Prompt
Widows showing colorful coats to Peter while Dorcas lies peacefully.

## Read-Along Flow + Response
“Kindness remembered by friends is a beautiful thing.”
