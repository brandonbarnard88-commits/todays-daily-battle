# Timothy Learns to Obey (2 Timothy 3:15)

## Emotional Focus
Learning to obey from those who love God.

## Key KJV
“From a child thou hast known the holy scriptures.” (2 Timothy 3:15)

## Gentle Retelling
Young Timothy learned to love and obey God from his grandmother and mother. He listened to the Bible and grew up following Jesus.

## Coloring Prompt
Young Timothy sitting happily with his grandmother and mother reading from a scroll.

## Read-Along Flow + Response
“Listening to God’s Word helps us obey and grow.”
