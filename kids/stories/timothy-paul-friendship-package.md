# Timothy and Paul (Philippians 2:20)

## Emotional Focus
A younger friend learning from an older one.

## Key KJV
“For I have no man likeminded, who will naturally care for your state.” (Philippians 2:20)

## Gentle Retelling
Young Timothy traveled with Paul and learned to love people the way Jesus does. Paul called him a son and a true friend.

## Coloring Prompt
Young Timothy walking beside Paul on a dusty road, talking happily.

## Read-Along Flow + Response
“Friends help each other grow closer to Jesus.”
