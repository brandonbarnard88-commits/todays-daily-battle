# The Wise and Foolish Builders (Matthew 7:24)

## Emotional Focus
Building our lives on Jesus so we stand strong when storms come.

## Key KJV
“Therefore whosoever heareth these sayings of mine, and doeth them, I will liken him unto a wise man, which built his house upon a rock.” (Matthew 7:24)

## Gentle Retelling
Jesus said the wise man built his house on rock. When the rain and wind came, it stood strong. The foolish man built on sand and his house fell down. Listening to Jesus is like building on rock.

## Coloring Prompt
Two houses — one strong on rock during a storm, one falling on sand.

## Read-Along Flow + Response
“Build your life on Jesus and you will be strong.”
