# Story Package: The Woman at the Well (John 4)

**Story Key**: `womanAtWell`

**Position in 365 Roadmap**: Phase 5, Story #23 — Jesus offers living water that satisfies every thirsty heart.

---

## Reverent Read-Aloud Retelling (for ages 3–8)

Jesus and His disciples were traveling through Samaria. Jesus was tired from the long walk, so He sat down by a well while His friends went into town to buy food.

A Samaritan woman came to the well to draw water. Jesus said to her, “Will you give Me a drink?”

The woman was surprised because Jewish people and Samaritans did not usually talk to each other. Jesus said, “If you knew who I am, you would ask Me, and I would give you living water.”

The woman said, “Sir, You have nothing to draw water with, and the well is deep. Where can You get this living water?”

Jesus answered, “Everyone who drinks this water will be thirsty again, but whoever drinks the water I give will never thirst. It will become in them a spring of water welling up to eternal life.”

The woman said, “Sir, give me this water so I won’t get thirsty and have to keep coming here to draw water.”

Jesus gently showed her that He knew everything about her life, even the hard parts. Instead of turning away, she ran back to her town and told everyone, “Come and see a man who told me everything I ever did! Could this be the Messiah?”

Many people from the town came to meet Jesus because of her words. They said to the woman, “We no longer believe just because of what you said; now we have heard for ourselves, and we know that this man really is the Savior of the world.”

## Coloring Page Prompt

Simple, warm black-and-white line art for ages 3–8. Jesus sitting by a well talking kindly to a woman holding a water jar. A bucket and rope on the well. Soft hills and a few houses in the distance. Gentle, welcoming, and hopeful feeling. Clean bold lines with large open spaces for easy coloring.

---

## Read-Along Flow

Color the woman at the well with Jesus → listen to the story of the living water that satisfies every thirsty heart → remember that Jesus knows us completely and still offers us the water that gives life forever. Perfect for any day we feel empty or thirsty inside.

## Quiz — 4 Questions (with helpful scaffolding)

**1. Why was the woman surprised when Jesus asked her for a drink?**
- A) Because she didn’t have a cup
- B) Because Jewish people and Samaritans did not usually talk to each other
- C) Because it was too early in the morning
- D) Because she had never seen a well before

*Correct: B*  
Wrong answers scaffolding:
- If A: “She had a jar. The surprise was that Jesus, a Jewish man, was speaking kindly to her, a Samaritan woman.”
- If C: “The time of day wasn’t the issue. People from different groups usually stayed apart.”
- If D: “She knew the well well. The shock was the conversation across the usual divide.”

**2. What kind of water did Jesus say He could give?**
- A) Water from the deepest part of the well
- B) Living water that would satisfy her so she would never be thirsty again
- C) Water that would make her rich
- D) Water only for special people

*Correct: B*  
Wrong answers scaffolding:
- If A: “Jesus wasn’t talking about regular water from the well. He was offering something much better that lasts forever.”
- If C: “He wasn’t offering money or things. He was offering water for the heart that never runs dry.”
- If D: “Jesus offers this living water to anyone who asks — even people others might overlook.”

**3. What did Jesus show the woman when He talked to her?**
- A) That He only liked perfect people
- B) That He knew everything about her life, even the hard parts, and still offered her living water
- C) That He didn’t want to talk to her
- D) That only important people could come to Him

*Correct: B*  
Wrong answers scaffolding:
- If A: “Jesus knew the messy parts of her story and still spoke to her with kindness and offered her the best gift.”
- If C: “He stayed and had a long, loving conversation with her. He wanted her to know Him.”
- If D: “Jesus welcomed her even though she felt small and ashamed. He came for people just like her.”

**4. What did the woman do after talking with Jesus?**
- A) She kept the good news to herself
- B) She ran back to town and told everyone, “Come and see a man who told me everything I ever did!”
- C) She went home and forgot all about it
- D) She argued with Jesus

*Correct: B*  
Wrong answers scaffolding:
- If A: “She couldn’t keep it inside. She wanted everyone to meet the One who knew her and still loved her.”
- If C: “This meeting changed her so much that she immediately went and told her whole town.”
- If D: “She believed Him. She became one of the first people to share the good news about Jesus.”
