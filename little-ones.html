Gentle starting places by age

Tap one lane. The others stay here when you want them, but you do not have to hold the whole page at once.

Ages 3–4, with a grown-up

Keep it very short. One simple picture, one short line, one warm ending.

  • Start with Jesus & the children, Creation, or The Good Shepherd.
  • Name two things in the picture and one thing God made or gives.
  • Repeat one word from today’s verse, then stop while it still feels sweet.

Ages 5–7

They can hold a little more story now, but the best rhythm is still short, warm, and repeatable.

  • Open one comfort or brave story from the Bible Story Library.
  • Color one matching scene in Color & Tell.
  • Ask one open question and pray one sentence together.

Family together

Use the same Scripture thread with everyone in the room, but keep the pressure low.

  • Read one verse aloud from the daily card above.
  • Let little ones color while older kids answer one simple question.
  • Use Family hub or Family Armor when you want to keep going a little farther.
