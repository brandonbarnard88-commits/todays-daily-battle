Memorize

You’re already welcome here—memory is practice, not a contest.

Private — everything stays on this device

Quiet tools to hide God’s Word in your heart. No streaks, no scores, no competition—just gentle repetition that still works on hard days. Verses you keep in My Study have a Memorize link on each card.

Families & classrooms: the same gentle month-by-month KJV grid (mark learning or memorized on this device) lives on Year-round rhythm and Family hub. Year at a glance prints every month plus seasonal doors on one page. Calm practice ideas (no quizzes): Memory Verse Activities in the Church Sharing Kit · one-page print sheet.

One-tap verse cards

Flip between reference and full text. Optional “hide words” blanks out some words for gentle recall.

Use the same form as elsewhere on the site (book, chapter:verse).

Gentle review

Verses you add also live in My Study’s memorize queue. A light spaced rhythm stays on this device—longer gaps when you tap “Remembered well,” sooner when you tap “Say it again soon.” Never a score.

Hiding God’s Word in your heart — one gentle rhythm at a time.

    Nothing here yet.

    When a verse touches your heart, tap Add to your memory list above.

    The Lord meets you right where you are.

    When your list has verses, optional buttons appear beside each line: In 1d, In 3d, In 7d schedule the next gentle check-in about that many days out—still private, still no score.

    Typing practice

    Type the verse from memory. When you pause, first letters appear as a quiet hint—not a test.

    Pause about a second after typing to show first-letter hints for words you have not typed yet.

    Listen & repeat

    Uses your browser’s calm voice when available. Load a verse above. “Repeat after me” plays the line once, then softly highlights the card so you can speak it aloud—no microphone, nothing leaves your device.

    Speaking is not available in this browser—that is all right. You can still read aloud quietly.

    Printable cards

    Opens a quiet print sheet: reference, then text—one card per verse. Trim or fold if you like. Text appears when you have loaded that verse on this page before (same cache as typing practice).

    Export My Memory List

    Private backup from this device — reference plus verse text when you have loaded it here. Never a score.

    Optional reminders

    Browser reminders stay off by default. Nothing here nags you; open this page when you want a quiet round.
