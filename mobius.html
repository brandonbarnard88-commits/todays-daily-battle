Möbius Loop

One journey, two modes. Graph (Bible canon) or Enoch Echoes—ancient apocryphal texts.

The Watchers descended to earth... and took wives among the daughters of men.
They taught forbidden arts: swords, metals, enchantments, and the signs of the stars.
Giants were born... and the earth filled with violence because of them.
I, Enoch, was taken up... and beheld the great sea above the clouds.
In the second heaven, prisoners hung in darkness... awaiting boundless judgment.
I saw fiery troops, archangels, cherubim, seraphim... and thrones of light.
The Holy One made me a throne... like the throne of glory itself.
Metatron stands before the Throne... voice of the Lord, scribe of secrets.
The heavens remember... the loop closes, yet the judgment comes.
All shall quake... but the righteous shall inherit light and peace.

Ancient apocryphal texts (1–3 Enoch)—not Bible canon. Historical, reflective. Read prayerfully, test against Scripture. Not doctrine.
