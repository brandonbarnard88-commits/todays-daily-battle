Introduction

God created humanity in His image. From the very beginning we were meant to live in family with Him.

Scripture traces one main line of promise — from Adam through Abraham and David to Jesus Christ. Along the way, God welcomes outsiders into that same story.

This page is a quiet map of that line. Read slowly. Through Christ, God offers adoption into His household — you are not outside this tree. New to the gospel? The Road to Salvation is a five-day KJV path with no test at the end.
