Morning Quiet Time

Monday — Psalm 5:3

“In the morning, O Lord, Thou wilt hear my voice: in the morning will I direct my prayer unto Thee, and will look up.”

Tuesday — Lamentations 3:22-23

“It is of the Lord’s mercies that we are not consumed, because His compassions fail not. They are new every morning: great is Thy faithfulness.”

Wednesday — Psalm 118:24

“This is the day which the Lord hath made; we will rejoice and be glad in it.”

Thursday — Psalm 143:8

“Cause me to hear Thy lovingkindness in the morning; for in Thee do I trust: cause me to know the way wherein I should walk; for I lift up my soul unto Thee.”

Friday — Psalm 90:14

“O satisfy us early with Thy mercy; that we may rejoice and be glad all our days.”

Saturday — Psalm 46:10

“Be still, and know that I am God…”

Sunday — Psalm 19:14

“Let the words of my mouth, and the meditation of my heart, be acceptable in Thy sight, O Lord, my strength, and my redeemer.”
