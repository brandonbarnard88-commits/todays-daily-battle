When partnership makes sense

Free is complete for most daily use. Partnership becomes practical when you lead a group or want to help sustain the ministry for others.

  • Leading a small group or church? The Church Center provides opt-in shared prayer and daily verse rhythm for your people.
  • Preparing messages regularly? Supporter tools like sermon builder, PDF export, and verse images save time and help share cleanly.
  • The site has been useful in hard seasons? Partnership keeps it available, maintained, and growing for the next person who needs it.
