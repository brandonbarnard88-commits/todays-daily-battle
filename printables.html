How to Use These Printables

These sheets are simple companions for your quiet time with the Lord — nothing more, nothing less. Print what feels helpful, use as much or as little as the day allows, and let the Holy Spirit meet you right where you are. Some mornings you may only have five minutes. That’s enough. The Lord is patient and kind.

Daily Rhythm Companions

Gentle structures to help you begin the day with the Lord and end it in peace — flexible for individuals, parents, and whole families.

  • One Week of Quiet Rhythm — A calm seven-day rhythm with morning quiet time, evening reflection, and a simple family table prompt. Spacious and flexible for anyone wanting gentle structure in their hand.
  • One Week of Quiet Rhythm – Kids & Family Edition — The same gentle rhythm, made simpler and more joyful for children and families. Larger fonts, drawing space, and light-hearted prompts that invite everyone to notice God together without pressure.
  • Be Still Moments — Five short, independent 5-minute cards (one for each weekday) with a single KJV verse and a quiet prompt. Ideal for busy mornings, lunch breaks, or any moment you simply need to pause and remember the Lord is near.
