Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 2026

Today's Daily Battle is a free, faith-based daily devotional tool. This policy explains what data is collected and how it is used. Plain language — no legal fog.

What we collect

Nothing you don't choose to give. The site works fully without an account. No tracking pixels, no ad networks, no selling of data — ever.

If you create an optional account (via Supabase Auth), we store your email address and your saved content (notes, prayer entries, streak data) so it syncs across your devices. You can delete your account and all associated data at any time by emailing support@todaysdailybattle.com.

Local storage

Mood check-ins, streak counts, saved verses, and reading plan progress are stored locally on your device using localStorage. This data never leaves your device unless you sign in and sync.

Push notifications

If you allow daily verse reminders, your browser's push subscription token is sent to our server (Supabase). It is only used to deliver the daily verse notification. You can revoke permission in your browser settings at any time.

Analytics

We use Google Analytics 4 and Plausible for anonymous usage statistics — page views, feature usage, general location (country level). No personally identifiable information is included. No raw search queries or prayer content is ever sent to analytics.

Third-party services

The site uses Supabase (database and auth), Firebase (push notifications), Cloudflare (CDN and security), Stripe (optional supporter payments), and Google Fonts. Each has its own privacy policy. We do not share your data with any of these services beyond what is necessary for the feature to work.

Children

The Kids Corner section of this site is designed for children with parental involvement. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Contact

Questions? support@todaysdailybattle.com
