Privacy Policy

User safety is the key. This is a safe place—we keep your data simple and respectful.

Anonymous prayers only—no names on the map. Your search is never stored or tied to you. We don't sell your data. Account data is only used to deliver your streak, notes, and reminders.

Your search is private

We never know who searched, and we never store what you type. Search is yours alone.

No tracking of who you are

We do not send your identity—no user ID, email, or IP—with search or analytics. You are not tracked.

No storage of what you type

What you enter in the search box is never sent to our analytics or any third party. We only use anonymous topic counts (e.g. “hope”, “anxiety”) to improve the site—never your words.

Safe place, even if something is breached

Our code and policy are built so that if any system is compromised, there is nothing that could identify who searched or what they typed. Your safety comes first.

How we protect your data

We treat your account and data like we would our own.

No selling data

We do not sell, rent, or trade your email or personal information.

Secure auth

Sign-in and passwords are handled by Supabase Auth (industry-standard). We never see or store your password.

Access control

Your notes, saved verses, and account data are tied to your account. Only you (and the site administrator for moderation) have access as needed.

HTTPS

The site runs over HTTPS so data in transit is encrypted.

Payments

Payments are processed by Stripe. We do not see or store your card number; Stripe handles it securely.

You stay in control

You can request a copy of your data or full account deletion anytime via the Contact page.

If a breach happens

We will notify affected users and take steps to secure data as quickly as possible.

What We Collect

We only collect what we need to provide the service.

Email

Used for account access and newsletter reminders if you opt in.

Local data

Notes, streaks, and settings are saved in your browser unless you sign in.

Anonymous usage

We only use anonymous counts (e.g. which topics are searched most). We never see or store what you type in search. User safety is the key.

How We Use It

We use your data to deliver features like reminders, saved notes, and account access. We do not sell personal data.

Third parties

We use only trusted providers necessary to run the service: Supabase (auth and database), Stripe (payments), and analytics (e.g. Google Analytics, Cloudflare) as described in this policy. We do not sell or share your personal information with third parties for their marketing. These providers have their own privacy and security obligations.

Data retention

We retain your data only as long as needed to provide the service and as required by law. You may request deletion of your account and data at any time via the Contact page; we will process such requests in a reasonable time.

Your Choices

You can unsubscribe from emails at any time or contact us to remove your account.

Terms of use

This page covers the basics. Use of the site implies acceptance of reasonable use—no abuse of the message board, no scraping at scale. We do not sell your personal data. For full terms see Terms of Service; for questions Contact.

Contact

If you have privacy questions or want to request data export/deletion, email support@todaysdailybattle.com or use the Contact page.
