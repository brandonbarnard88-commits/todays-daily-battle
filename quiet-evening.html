Quiet Evening — Entrust the day and rest in Him

You’re already welcome here—even heavy days can end with one honest breath; nothing here rushes you to peace.

Psalm 4:8 (KJV)
"I will both lay me down in peace, and sleep: for thou, LORD, only makest me dwell in safety."
What are you carrying as the day ends? One breath of gratitude is enough.
