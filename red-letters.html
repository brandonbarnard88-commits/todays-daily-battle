    When words fail

    When your own prayers feel empty, start with Only His Voice—only the Saviour’s speech—or walk Come Unto Me for five quiet days (KJV). Print Only His Voice or Come Unto Me when paper helps.

    When fear presses in

    When fear speaks louder than reason, walk When Fear Presses In—gentle Scripture for anxious days, His voice and sure promises. Print all five when paper helps. For pure red letters only, rest in Only His Voice. Pairs with Come Unto Me when weariness and fear share the same hour.

    Wondering how a sinner can be made right with God? The Road to Salvation walks five quiet KJV days—no test at the end, only an open invitation. Print all five when paper helps.
