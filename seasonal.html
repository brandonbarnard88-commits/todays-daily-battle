Seasonal paths

The site stays evergreen on purpose—but some weeks call for a named door. Each link opens the same Battle Plans you already use: one KJV verse a day, plain words, a step, a prayer, saved on your device.

Easter

He is risen

For Easter morning or Holy Week, He is risen offers short KJV readings—declarative, still quiet (available any time of year). For a full week of rhythm, keep Resurrection Hope (seven days, one verse a day).

When the week feels loud, the seven-day plan keeps the same gentle pace as everything else on the site—saved on your device, optional listen on each day.
