How to use this site

This page is a map, not a lesson. Scripture on the site is KJV unless a translated mood page says otherwise. Plan checkmarks, wall lines, and most notes stay on this device until you sign in or print on purpose. Nothing here is grading you.

Start on Home

  • Feel search — type how you are doing (or tap a topic chip). Verses line up with your words; what you type stays on your device.
  • Verse of the Day — a steady anchor when you do not want to search.
  • Quick topics — the row of chips (peace, fear, hope, and the rest) is the fastest way in.
Open Home Verse of the Day

One quiet minute

  • Calm — one verse, minimal chrome.
  • Daily quiet time — a gentle morning order: verse, calm, one plan day, prayer starters, optional chapter reading.
  • Möbius — a guided loop when you want a voice-led pause.
  • What God has done — private journal on this device; short dated entries, optional export. No posting.

Stay in the Word

  • Battle Plans — multi-day tracks: one verse, plain words, a step, a prayer; progress saves on your device.
  • Chapter reader — full chapters in order; good when you want context, not snippets.
  • Bible tool — look up passages and words; save lines to My Verses.
  • Psalms hub — comfort, praise, and family Psalms plans in one place.

Pray with quiet company

  • Prayer Wall on Home — short anonymous lines; tap the heart for a silent amen.
  • Prayer & message board — when you need a bit more room to write.

Family, kids, and paper

  • Family hub — verse, plans doorway, printables index.
  • Marriage, family & home — structured plans for the household.
  • Kids Corner — stories and activities for shorter attention spans.
  • Printables hub — sheets that print cleanly (packs, Easter readings, verse cards, and more).

New or returning to faith?

If you are asking about faith itself, not where to click, start with First steps in faith and Who God says you are. Those pages are a doorway, not a site map.
