The Story

You’re already welcome here—read at your own pace; no finish line.

Hi, I’m Brandon. Born in Dallas and raised in West Tennessee (Union City, Martin, and Memphis areas). A hospital season changed the pace of my life and made one thing painfully clear: when the day is heavy, most people do not need more noise. They need the Word of God near, plain, and steady.

So I built what I could not find. Not another feed. Not another performance tool. Not a place that turns pain into content. I built a calm KJV place for real battles: one verse, one prayer, one next step, and room to breathe.

Today’s Daily Battle is still solo-built, and I still use it every day myself. That matters to me. I do not want to hand people a product I would not reach for in my own hard moments.

If the site feels quiet, that is on purpose. If it feels human-scale, that is on purpose too. I want people to find help here without being tracked, crowded, or pressured. Just Scripture, honest tools, and a little steadiness for the next step in front of them.

I am not building for polished Christian performance. I am building for real days, real limits, and a real need for God’s Word.
