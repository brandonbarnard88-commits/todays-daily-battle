Quick start for team leads

Get your team going in three steps.

1. Set your church

Go to Church Center, find your church, and join. Then your team sees the same verse and prayer list.

2. Assign reading

Assign a passage and group name (e.g. "Youth," "Sunday Class"). Members see it under My assigned reading and can mark complete.

3. Share the link

Send your team the link to Church Center or Team Toolkit. They bookmark it and check in daily or weekly.
