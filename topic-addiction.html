Key Verses

1 Corinthians 10:13 (KJV)

KJV

There hath no temptation taken you but such as is common to man: but God is faithful, who will not suffer you to be tempted above that ye are able; but will with the temptation also make a way to escape, that ye may be able to bear it.

In simpler words

You have been put to no test but such as is common to man: and God is true, who will not let any test come on you which you are not able to undergo; but he will make with the test a way out of it, so that you may be able to go through it.

What was going on

Paul tells Corinth no temptation is unique to them; God is faithful, who will not suffer them to be tempted above what they are able, and will with the temptation make a way to escape.

What it means

No temptation has taken you but what is common to man. God is faithful: He will not let you be tempted above what you are able, and will make a way to escape so you can bear it.

The way of escape is promised with the temptation—not after you prove you are strong.

Romans 7:24 (KJV)

KJV

O wretched man that I am! who shall deliver me from the body of this death?

In simpler words

How unhappy am I! who will make me free from the body of this death?

What was going on

Paul cries out under the body of this death: O wretched man that I am! who shall deliver me?

What it means

O wretched man that I am! Who shall deliver me from the body of this death?

The cry is in the Bible. You do not have to tidy it before you pray it.

John 8:36 (KJV)

KJV

If the Son therefore shall make you free, ye shall be free indeed.

In simpler words

If then the son makes you free, you will be truly free.

What was going on

Jesus tells those in the temple that if the Son makes them free, they shall be free indeed.

What it means

If the Son therefore shall make you free, you shall be free indeed.

Freedom here is the Son’s doing—not a white-knuckle vow.

2 Corinthians 5:17 (KJV)

KJV

Therefore if any man be in Christ, he is a new creature: old things are passed away; behold, all things are become new.

In simpler words

So if any man is in Christ, he is in a new world: the old things have come to an end; they have truly become new.

What was going on

Paul tells Corinth that if anyone is in Christ, he is a new creature: old things are passed away; behold, all things are become new.

What it means

If anyone is in Christ, he is a new creature: old things are passed away; all things are become new.

The old things do not get to keep the title on you.

Psalm 40:2 (KJV)

KJV

He brought me up also out of an horrible pit, out of the miry clay, and set my feet upon a rock, and established my goings.

In simpler words

He took me up out of a deep waste place, out of the soft and sticky earth; he put my feet on a rock, and made my steps certain.

What was going on

David says the Lord brought him up out of a horrible pit and out of the miry clay, set his feet upon a rock, and established his goings.

What it means

He brought me up out of a horrible pit, out of the miry clay, set my feet on a rock, and established my goings.

A pit is named. So is the rock under the feet.

Galatians 5:1 (KJV)

KJV

Stand fast therefore in the liberty wherewith Christ hath made us free, and be not entangled again with the yoke of bondage.

In simpler words

Christ has truly made us free: then keep your free condition and let no man put a yoke on you again.

What was going on

Paul tells the churches of Galatia to stand fast in the liberty wherewith Christ has made them free, and not be entangled again with the yoke of bondage.

What it means

Stand fast in the liberty Christ has given, and do not be entangled again with the yoke of bondage.

Liberty is already given in Christ. The call is to stand in it, not earn it.

More on this topic

When it hits hardest

  • Before the old door when your feet already know the way—1 Corinthians 10:13 says the test is common, and God makes a way of escape.
  • After you fell again—Romans 7:24 is allowed to be a cry, not a speech.
  • When shame says you are the old man forever—2 Corinthians 5:17 names a new creature in Christ.

A Gentle Word

John 8:36. If the Son makes you free, you shall be free indeed. That is a Person, not a streak.
