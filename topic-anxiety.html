Key Verses

Philippians 4:6-7 (KJV)

KJV

Be careful for nothing; but in every thing by prayer and supplication with thanksgiving let your requests be made known unto God. And the peace of God, which passeth all understanding, shall keep your hearts and minds through Christ Jesus.

In simpler words

Have no cares; but in everything with prayer and praise put your requests before God.

What was going on

Paul writes from prison to Philippi: rejoice, do not be anxious, the peace of God guards hearts; contentment in every state through Christ.

What it means

Don't let worry take over—pray and thank God, and tell Him what you need.

Bring the whole worry to God in plain words—not a polished prayer first.

1 Peter 5:7 (KJV)

KJV

Casting all your care upon him; for he careth for you.

In simpler words

Putting all your troubles on him, for he takes care of you.

What was going on

Peter closes his letter: humble yourselves under God’s hand, cast all your care on Him, for He cares for you, and resist the devil.

What it means

Give God your worries—He cares about you.

All means the messy parts too—the ones you would not say out loud yet.

Isaiah 41:10 (KJV)

KJV

Fear thou not; for I am with thee: be not dismayed; for I am thy God: I will strengthen thee; yea, I will help thee; yea, I will uphold thee with the right hand of my righteousness.

In simpler words

Have no fear, for I am with you; do not be looking about in trouble, for I am your God; I will give you strength, yes, I will be your helper; yes, my true right hand will be your support.

What was going on

The Lord comforts fearful Israel in exile: “Fear thou not; for I am with thee” — strength and help from His right hand.

What it means

God tells us not to fear—He is with us, strengthens us, and holds us up.

When your body feels alone in the room, His nearness is the anchor.

Matthew 6:34 (KJV)

KJV

Take therefore no thought for the morrow: for the morrow shall take thought for the things of itself. Sufficient unto the day is the evil thereof.

In simpler words

Then have no care for tomorrow: tomorrow will take care of itself. Take the trouble of the day as it comes.

What was going on

Jesus said this to His disciples who worry about tomorrow: Do not be anxious.

What it means

Focus on today; don't borrow trouble from tomorrow.

One day’s weight is enough. Tomorrow can wait outside the door.

Psalm 56:3 (KJV)

KJV

What time I am afraid, I will trust in thee.

What was going on

David under Saul’s pursuit and Doeg’s betrayal; cries from caves and defeat; God still rules.

What it means

In the time of my fear, I will have faith in you.

Not “when fear is gone”—while fear is still loud.

Psalm 94:19 (KJV)

KJV

In the multitude of my thoughts within me thy comforts delight my soul.

What was going on

A cry against unjust thrones: the psalmist names oppression, then finds that when anxiety multiplies, the Lord’s comforts delight his soul.

What it means

When anxiety was great within me, Your consolation brought me joy—God meets us in the overwhelm.

When thoughts multiply faster than you can sort them.

2 Timothy 1:7 (KJV)

KJV

For God hath not given us the spirit of fear; but of power, and of love, and of a sound mind.

In simpler words

For God did not give us a spirit of fear, but of power and of love and of self-control.

What was going on

Paul said this to Timothy (and every timid heart): Spirit of power and love.

What it means

Fear is not from God. He gives power, love, and a clear mind — not panic.

Fear may feel huge; it is not the final word over you.

When it hits hardest

  • At night when your mind replays every conversation—read one verse aloud, slowly, before sleep.
  • With kids awake and your chest tight—one line from Psalm 56:3 is enough at the kitchen sink.
  • At work when you cannot fix the whole week—Matthew 6:34 holds one day, not the whole calendar.

A Gentle Word

1 Peter 5:7 — cast it all. Not the tidy pieces. All of it.
