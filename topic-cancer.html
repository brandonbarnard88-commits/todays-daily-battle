Key Verses

Psalm 73:26 (KJV)

KJV

My flesh and my heart faileth: but God is the strength of my heart, and my portion for ever.

In simpler words

My flesh and my heart are wasting away: but God is the Rock of my heart and my eternal heritage.

What was going on

Asaph’s flesh and heart fail; he confesses God as the strength of his heart and his portion for ever.

What it means

My flesh and my heart fail: but God is the strength of my heart, and my portion for ever.

The body can fail and still not be the last word over you.

Psalm 23:4 (KJV)

KJV

Yea, though I walk through the valley of the shadow of death, I will fear no evil: for thou art with me; thy rod and thy staff they comfort me.

In simpler words

Yes, though I go through the valley of deep shade, I will have no fear of evil; for you are with me, your rod and your support are my comfort.

What was going on

David walks through the valley of the shadow of death and says he will fear no evil, because the Shepherd is with him; rod and staff comfort him.

What it means

Even in the valley of the shadow of death, I will fear no evil: You are with me; Your rod and staff comfort me.

The valley is named. So is the Shepherd in it.

Isaiah 41:10 (KJV)

KJV

Fear thou not; for I am with thee: be not dismayed; for I am thy God: I will strengthen thee; yea, I will help thee; yea, I will uphold thee with the right hand of my righteousness.

In simpler words

Have no fear, for I am with you; do not be looking about in trouble, for I am your God; I will give you strength, yes, I will be your helper; yes, my true right hand will be your support.

What was going on

The Lord tells fearful Israel in exile: Fear thou not; for I am with thee — He will strengthen, help, and uphold with His right hand.

What it means

Fear not; I am with you. I am your God. I will strengthen you, help you, and uphold you.

Fear is answered with nearness, not with a demand to feel brave first.

2 Corinthians 4:16 (KJV)

KJV

For which cause we faint not; but though our outward man perish, yet the inward man is renewed day by day.

In simpler words

For which cause we do not give way to weariness; but though our outer man is getting feebler, our inner man is made new day by day.

What was going on

Paul says the outward man perishes, yet the inward man is renewed day by day — and for that cause they faint not.

What it means

We faint not: though the outward man perish, the inward man is renewed day by day.

The wasting is honest. So is the daily inward renewing.

Romans 8:38-39 (KJV)

KJV

For I am persuaded, that neither death, nor life, nor angels, nor principalities, nor powers, nor things present, nor things to come, Nor height, nor depth, nor any other creature, shall be able to separate us from the love of God, which is in Christ Jesus our Lord.

In simpler words

For I am certain that not death, or life, or angels, or rulers, or things present, or things to come, or powers, Or things on high, or things under the earth, or anything which is made, will be able to come between us and the love of God which is in Christ Jesus our Lord.

What was going on

Paul is persuaded that death, life, angels, powers, height, depth, or any creature cannot separate us from the love of God in Christ Jesus our Lord.

What it means

Nothing—death, life, angels, powers, height, depth, or any creature—can separate us from the love of God which is in Christ Jesus our Lord.

The love of God in Christ is not smaller than this diagnosis.

More on this topic

When it hits hardest

  • In the waiting room when names are called and yours is next—Isaiah 41:10 is one sentence you can hold.
  • When the body is weaker than yesterday—2 Corinthians 4:16 names the outward man without denying the inward one.
  • When family is trying to be strong for you—Psalm 23:4 is for the valley, not for a speech.

A Gentle Word

Psalm 73:26. Flesh and heart may fail. God remains the strength of the heart, and the portion for ever.
