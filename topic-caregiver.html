Key Verses

Galatians 6:2 (KJV)

KJV

Bear ye one another’s burdens, and so fulfil the law of Christ.

In simpler words

Take on yourselves one another's troubles, and so keep the law of Christ.

What was going on

Paul tells the churches to bear one another’s burdens, and so fulfil the law of Christ.

What it means

Bear one another’s burdens, and so fulfil the law of Christ.

The law of Christ here is shared weight—not you alone forever.

Matthew 11:28 (KJV)

KJV

Come unto me, all ye that labour and are heavy laden, and I will give you rest.

In simpler words

Come to me, all you who are troubled and weighted down with care, and I will give you rest.

What was going on

Jesus invites all who labour and are heavy laden to come unto Him, and He will give them rest.

What it means

Come to Me, all you who labour and are heavy laden, and I will give you rest.

The tired are invited as they are. Rest is His gift.

Isaiah 40:31 (KJV)

KJV

But they that wait upon the LORD shall renew their strength; they shall mount up with wings as eagles; they shall run, and not be weary; and they shall walk, and not faint.

In simpler words

But those who are waiting for the Lord will have new strength; they will get wings like eagles: running, they will not be tired, and walking, they will have no weariness.

What was going on

Isaiah tells weary Judah that they that wait upon the LORD shall renew their strength; they shall mount up with wings as eagles, run and not be weary, walk and not faint.

What it means

They that wait upon the LORD shall renew their strength: they shall mount up with wings as eagles; they shall run and not be weary; they shall walk and not faint.

Strength is renewed in waiting on the Lord—not in pretending you never tire.

Psalm 55:22 (KJV)

KJV

Cast thy burden upon the LORD, and he shall sustain thee: he shall never suffer the righteous to be moved.

In simpler words

Put your cares on the Lord, and he will be your support; he will not let the upright man be moved.

What was going on

David, crushed by a friend’s treachery, says cast thy burden upon the LORD, and He shall sustain thee: He shall never suffer the righteous to be moved.

What it means

Cast your burden upon the LORD, and He shall sustain you: He shall never suffer the righteous to be moved.

Sustain is the word—not “make the burden imaginary.”

Galatians 6:9 (KJV)

KJV

And let us not be weary in well doing: for in due season we shall reap, if we faint not.

In simpler words

And let us not get tired of well-doing; for at the right time we will get in the grain, if we do not give way to weariness.

What was going on

Paul tells the churches not to be weary in well doing: for in due season we shall reap, if we faint not.

What it means

Let us not be weary in well doing: in due season we shall reap, if we faint not.

Well doing can weary you. Due season is still promised.

2 Corinthians 1:4 (KJV)

KJV

Who comforteth us in all our tribulation, that we may be able to comfort them which are in any trouble, by the comfort wherewith we ourselves are comforted of God.

In simpler words

Who gives us comfort in all our troubles, so that we may be able to give comfort to others who are in trouble, through the comfort with which we ourselves are comforted by God.

What was going on

Paul says God comforts us in all our tribulation, that we may be able to comfort them which are in any trouble, by the comfort wherewith we ourselves are comforted of God.

What it means

God comforts us in all our tribulation so we can comfort others with the comfort we ourselves received from God.

You can pass comfort on. You also have to receive it first.

More on this topic

When it hits hardest

  • When everyone else sleeps and you are still on watch—Psalm 55:22 says cast thy burden upon the LORD; He shall sustain thee.
  • When well-doing has gone longer than your strength—Galatians 6:9 says do not be weary in well doing; due season is still ahead.
  • When you cannot put the burden down—Galatians 6:2 is for the church too: others may bear with you.

A Gentle Word

Matthew 11:28. Come unto Me, all ye that labour and are heavy laden. The invitation is to the tired one—you.
