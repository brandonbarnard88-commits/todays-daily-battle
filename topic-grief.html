Key Verses

Psalm 34:18 (KJV)

KJV

The LORD is nigh unto them that are of a broken heart; and saveth such as be of a contrite spirit.

What was going on

David, after escaping Abimelech, teaches: the Lord is near to the brokenhearted and saves those of a contrite spirit.

What it means

The Lord is close to the brokenhearted and saves those who are crushed in spirit.

Near means close—not waiting for you to compose yourself first.

Matthew 5:4 (KJV)

KJV

Blessed are they that mourn: for they shall be comforted.

In simpler words

Happy are those who are sad: for they will be comforted.

What was going on

Jesus teaches the Sermon on the Mount: heart righteousness, prayer, and the narrow way.

What it means

Those who mourn will be comforted by God.

Mourning is seen, not shamed, in the Sermon on the Mount.

Revelation 21:4 (KJV)

KJV

And God shall wipe away all tears from their eyes; and there shall be no more death, neither sorrow, nor crying, neither shall there be any more pain: for the former things are passed away.

In simpler words

And he will put an end to all their weeping; and there will be no more death, or sorrow, or crying, or pain; for the first things have come to an end.

What was going on

Christ returns; new heaven and new earth; the river of life; “Even so, come, Lord Jesus.”.

What it means

One day God will wipe away every tear; no more death, grief, or pain.

The ache is real now; this is the horizon Scripture holds out.

Psalm 147:3 (KJV)

KJV

He healeth the broken in heart, and bindeth up their wounds.

What was going on

Final Hallelujah chorus: trust not in princes; everything that has breath praise the Lord.

What it means

God heals the brokenhearted and binds up their wounds.

Binding takes time. Healing is His work, not a deadline for you.

John 14:18 (KJV)

KJV

I will not leave you comfortless: I will come to you.

In simpler words

I will not let you be without a friend: I am coming to you.

What was going on

Jesus said this to His disciples the night before the cross: Upper room comfort.

What it means

You are not abandoned. Jesus comes near when you feel friendless.

When the house feels empty after loss, His promise is presence.

Lamentations 3:22-23 (KJV)

KJV

It is of the LORD’s mercies that we are not consumed, because his compassions fail not. They are new every morning: great is thy faithfulness.

In simpler words

It is through the Lord's love that we have not come to destruction, because his mercies have no limit.

What was going on

Jerusalem has fallen; the poet weeps, yet hopes in mercies new every morning.

What it means

God's love never runs out; His compassion is new every morning.

Some mornings grief is the first thing you meet. Mercy meets you there too.

Romans 8:38-39 (KJV)

KJV

For I am persuaded, that neither death, nor life, nor angels, nor principalities, nor powers, nor things present, nor things to come, nor height, nor depth, nor any other creature, shall be able to separate us from the love of God, which is in Christ Jesus our Lord.

In simpler words

For I am certain that not death, or life, or angels, or rulers, or things present, or things to come, or powers,

What was going on

Paul teaches believers in Rome about life in the Spirit: no condemnation, adoption, and God working all things for good for those who love Him.

What it means

Nothing can separate us from God's love—not trouble, not death, nothing.

Death does not undo love. Separation is not the last word.

More on this topic

When it hits hardest

  • After goodbye when ordinary tasks feel impossible—Psalm 34:18 needs no performance from you.
  • Anniversaries and empty chairs—Lamentations 3:22-23 for the morning that hurts again.
  • With children watching you grieve—one quiet verse at the table is enough; see After grief for family-paced days.

A Gentle Word

Psalm 34:18. He doesn't ask you to pull it together. He just gets close.
