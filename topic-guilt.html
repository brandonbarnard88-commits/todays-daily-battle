Key Verses

1 John 1:9

KJV

If we confess our sins, he is faithful and just to forgive us our sins, and to cleanse us from all unrighteousness.

In simpler words

If we say openly that we have done wrong, he is upright and true to his word, giving us forgiveness of sins and making us clean from all evil.

What was going on

John writes about fellowship, light, and assurance against early antichrist lies.

What it means

If we confess our sins, he is faithful and just to forgive us our sins, and to cleanse us from all unrighteousness.

Romans 8:1

KJV

There is therefore now no condemnation to them which are in Christ Jesus.

In simpler words

For this cause those who are in Christ Jesus will not be judged as sinners.

What was going on

Paul teaches believers in Rome about life in the Spirit: no condemnation, adoption, and God working all things for good for those who love Him.

What it means

There is no condemnation for those who are in Christ Jesus.

Psalm 103:12

KJV

As far as the east is from the west, so far hath he removed our transgressions from us.

What was going on

David measures the Lord’s mercy: merciful and gracious, slow to anger; as far as the east is from the west He has removed our transgressions.

What it means

As far as the east is from the west, so far has he removed our transgressions from us.
