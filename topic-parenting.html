Key Verses

Isaiah 40:11 (KJV)

KJV

He shall feed his flock like a shepherd: he shall gather the lambs with his arm, and carry them in his bosom, and shall gently lead those that are with young.

In simpler words

He will give food to his flock like a keeper of sheep; with his arm he will get it together, and will take up the lambs on his breast, gently guiding those which are with young.

What was going on

Comfort ye my people: the Lord comes as a shepherd who feeds His flock, gathers the lambs, and gently leads those that are with young.

What it means

He shall feed his flock like a shepherd: he shall gather the lambs with his arm, and carry them in his bosom, and shall gently lead those that are with young.

Those with young are named gently—including you on the tired days.

Deuteronomy 6:6-7 (KJV)

KJV

And these words, which I command thee this day, shall be in thine heart: And thou shalt teach them diligently unto thy children, and shalt talk of them when thou sittest in thine house, and when thou walkest by the way, and when thou liest down, and when thou risest up.

In simpler words

Keep these words, which I say to you this day, deep in your hearts;

What was going on

Moses repeats the Ten Commandments and presses love for the Lord with the whole heart.

What it means

And these words, which I command you this day, shall be in yours heart: And you shall teach them diligently to your children, and shall talk of them when you sittest in yours house, and when you walkest by the way, and when you liest down, and when you risest up.

Ordinary rooms count—car line, bedtime, dishes.

Ephesians 6:4 (KJV)

KJV

And, ye fathers, provoke not your children to wrath: but bring them up in the nurture and admonition of the Lord.

In simpler words

And, you fathers, do not make your children angry: but give them training in the teaching and fear of the Lord.

What was going on

Paul turns to the household: children obey, fathers do not provoke their children to wrath, but bring them up in the nurture and admonition of the Lord.

What it means

And, you fathers, provoke not your children to wrath: but bring them up in the nurture and admonition of the Lord.

Nurture is the tone; admonition is truth held with care.

Proverbs 22:6 (KJV)

KJV

Train up a child in the way he should go: and when he is old, he will not depart from it.

In simpler words

If a child is trained up in the right way, even when he is old he will not be turned away from it.

What was going on

Short proverbs for daily life: work, words, friends, and the fear of the Lord.

What it means

Train up a child in the way he should go: and when he is old, he will not depart from it.

Faithfulness over years, not perfection in one afternoon.

Psalm 127:3 (KJV)

KJV

Lo, children are an heritage of the LORD: and the fruit of the womb is his reward.

What was going on

A song of ascents for Solomon: except the Lord build the house, they labour in vain that build it.

What it means

Lo, children are an heritage of the LORD: and the fruit of the womb is his reward.

Heritage holds on hard days too—when reward feels far away.

Colossians 3:21 (KJV)

KJV

Fathers, provoke not your children to anger, lest they be discouraged.

In simpler words

Fathers, do not be hard on your children, so that their spirit may not be broken.

What was going on

Paul addresses the household: fathers, provoke not your children to anger, lest they be discouraged.

What it means

Fathers, provoke not your children to anger, so that you do not they be discouraged.

Discouragement in a child’s eyes is worth pausing for.

Matthew 19:14 (KJV)

KJV

But Jesus said, Suffer little children, and forbid them not, to come unto me: for of such is the kingdom of heaven.

In simpler words

But Jesus said, Let the little ones come to me, and do not keep them away: for of such is the kingdom of heaven.

What was going on

Parents bring little children to Jesus; the disciples rebuke them; Jesus says, Suffer little children, and forbid them not, to come unto me.

What it means

But Jesus said, Suffer little children, and forbid them not, to come to me: for of such is the kingdom of heaven.

Jesus made room for littles in the middle of busy crowds.

More on this topic

When it hits hardest

  • After bedtime battles—Isaiah 40:11 for the parent still standing in the hallway.
  • When you snapped and guilt follows—Ephesians 6:4 and a quiet repair tomorrow; see Parent weary.
  • With littles underfoot all day—Deuteronomy 6:7 in one sentence at lunch counts as teaching.

A Gentle Word

Some days you don't know what you're doing. Neither did most parents in Scripture. God knows that — and still gives wisdom to those who ask.
