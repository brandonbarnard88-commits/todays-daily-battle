Who this is for

  • Survivors — child or adult, whether the harm was recent or decades ago.
  • Parents — carrying rage, guilt, grief, or exhaustion beside a hurting child.
  • Someone who loves them — looking for one true word to offer without preaching.

Read one verse slowly. Sit with it. No commentary required. Trauma-informed therapy stays primary; these passages are companions, not a treatment plan.

God saw—and was not indifferent

Mark 10:14 (KJV)

KJV

But when Jesus saw it, he was much displeased, and said unto them, Suffer the little children to come unto me, and forbid them not: for of such is the kingdom of God.

In simpler words

And when Jesus saw it, he was angry, and said to them, Let the little children come to me, and do not keep them away; for of such is the kingdom of God.

What was going on

Parents bring little children to Jesus; the disciples rebuke them; Jesus is much displeased and welcomes the children into His arms.

What it means

But when Jesus saw it, he was much displeased, and said to them, Suffer the little children to come to me, and forbid them not: for of such is the kingdom of God.

Jesus wanted children near Him. Harm to the vulnerable is not a small matter in His sight.

Matthew 18:10 (KJV)

KJV

Take heed that ye despise not one of these little ones; for I say unto you, That in heaven their angels do always behold the face of my Father which is in heaven.

In simpler words

Let it not seem to you that one of these little ones is of no value; for I say to you that in heaven their angels see at all times the face of my Father in heaven.

What was going on

In Capernaum Jesus teaches humility with a child in their midst and warns against despising little ones whose angels behold the Father’s face.

What it means

The little ones are not leftover to God. Heaven pays attention to them.

What was done was seen in heaven. The Father has not looked away.

Psalm 10:17-18 (KJV)

KJV

LORD, thou hast heard the desire of the humble: thou wilt prepare their heart, thou wilt cause thine ear to hear: To judge the fatherless and the oppressed, that the man of the earth may no more oppress.

What was going on

David sings of God’s justice against oppressors and of the fool who says there is no God.

What it means

Lord, you have given ear to the prayer of the poor: you will make strong their hearts, you will give them a hearing:.

The humble are heard. Justice for the oppressed belongs to the Lord.

When the heart has carried this a long time

Psalm 34:18 (KJV)

KJV

The LORD is nigh unto them that are of a broken heart; and saveth such as be of a contrite spirit.

What was going on

David, after escaping Abimelech, teaches: the Lord is near to the brokenhearted and saves those of a contrite spirit.

What it means

The Lord is close to the brokenhearted and saves those who are crushed in spirit.

Near means close—not waiting for you to compose yourself first.

Psalm 147:3 (KJV)

KJV

He healeth the broken in heart, and bindeth up their wounds.

What was going on

Final Hallelujah chorus: trust not in princes; everything that has breath praise the Lord.

What it means

God heals the brokenhearted and binds up their wounds.

Binding takes time. Healing is His work, not a deadline for you.

Psalm 56:8 (KJV)

KJV

Thou tellest my wanderings: put thou my tears into thy bottle: are they not in thy book?

What was going on

David under Saul’s pursuit and Doeg’s betrayal; cries from caves and defeat; God still rules.

What it means

You have seen my wanderings; put the drops from my eyes into your bottle; are they not in your record?

Every tear is counted—yours and theirs.

Lamentations 3:22-23 (KJV)

KJV

It is of the LORD’s mercies that we are not consumed, because his compassions fail not. They are new every morning: great is thy faithfulness.

In simpler words

It is through the Lord's love that we have not come to destruction, because his mercies have no limit.

What was going on

Jerusalem has fallen; the poet weeps, yet hopes in mercies new every morning.

What it means

God's love never runs out; His compassion is new every morning.

Some mornings the weight is the first thing you meet. Mercy meets you there too.

When shame whispers “it was my fault”

Isaiah 43:1 (KJV)

KJV

But now thus saith the LORD that created thee, O Jacob, and he that formed thee, O Israel, Fear not: for I have redeemed thee, I have called thee by thy name; thou art mine.

In simpler words

But now, says the Lord your Maker, O Jacob, and your life-giver, O Israel: have no fear, for I have taken up your cause; naming you by your name, I have made you mine.

What was going on

Comfort for exiles: God is incomparable; idols are nothing; a servant will bring justice.

What it means

God made you, named you, and claims you. Fear does not get the last word.

Abuse does not get the final say on who you are. Called. Named. His.

Romans 8:38-39 (KJV)

KJV

For I am persuaded, that neither death, nor life, nor angels, nor principalities, nor powers, nor things present, nor things to come, Nor height, nor depth, nor any other creature, shall be able to separate us from the love of God, which is in Christ Jesus our Lord.

In simpler words

For I am certain that not death, or life, or angels, or rulers, or things present, or things to come, or powers,

What was going on

Paul teaches believers in Rome about life in the Spirit: no condemnation, adoption, and God working all things for good for those who love Him.

What it means

Nothing can separate us from God's love—not trouble, not death, nothing.

Not what happened. Not shame. Not time. Love in Christ is not undone.

Zephaniah 3:17 (KJV)

KJV

The LORD thy God in the midst of thee is mighty; he will save, he will rejoice over thee with joy; he will rest in his love, he will joy over thee with singing.

In simpler words

The Lord your God is among you, as a strong saviour: he will be glad over you with joy, he will make his love new again, he will make a song of joy over you as in the time of a holy feast.

What was going on

The day of the Lord is near; a humble remnant will trust the Lord and rejoice.

What it means

God is not only near — He delights in you and quiets you with His love.

You do not have to feel glad yet. He rests in His love toward you anyway.

For parents and the ones who stayed

2 Corinthians 1:3-4 (KJV)

KJV

Blessed be God, even the Father of our Lord Jesus Christ, the Father of mercies, and the God of all comfort; Who comforteth us in all our tribulation, that we may be able to comfort them which are in any trouble, by the comfort wherewith we ourselves are comforted of God.

In simpler words

Praise be to the God and Father of our Lord Jesus Christ, the Father of mercies and the God of all comfort;

What was going on

Paul said this to the church at Corinth in affliction: God of all comfort.

What it means

God is the Father of compassion and the One who comforts us in our troubles.

Your long faithfulness beside them is real ministry. Comfort received is not wasted.

Isaiah 49:15-16 (KJV)

KJV

Can a woman forget her sucking child, that she should not have compassion on the son of her womb? yea, they may forget, yet will I not forget thee. Behold, I have graven thee upon the palms of my hands; thy walls are continually before me.

In simpler words

Will a woman give up the child at her breast, will she be without pity for the fruit of her body? yes, these may, but I will not let you go out of my memory.

What was going on

The Servant suffers for sins; free pardon is offered; the word of God stands forever.

What it means

Even if a mother could forget her child, God will not forget you.

He has not forgotten your child—even when the years feel endless.

Say plainly, as often as needed: It was not your fault. You are safe now. I love you. I believe you. Those words matter as much as any reference.

When hope feels far away

Isaiah 61:1-3 (KJV)

KJV

The Spirit of the Lord GOD is upon me; because the LORD hath anointed me to preach good tidings unto the meek; he hath sent me to bind up the brokenhearted, to proclaim liberty to the captives, and the opening of the prison to them that are bound… to comfort all that mourn; to appoint unto them that mourn in Zion, to give unto them beauty for ashes, the oil of joy for mourning, the garment of praise for the spirit of heaviness…

In simpler words

The spirit of the Lord is on me, because I am marked out by him to give good news to the poor; he has sent me to make the broken-hearted well, to say that the prisoners will be made free, and that those in chains will see the light again;

What was going on

Promises of a new heavens and new earth; a call to true fasting and hope for the nations.

What it means

The spirit of the Lord is on me, because I am marked out by him to give good news to the poor;.

Beauty for ashes is a long promise—not pressure to feel better by Friday.

Revelation 21:4 (KJV)

KJV

And God shall wipe away all tears from their eyes; and there shall be no more death, neither sorrow, nor crying, neither shall there be any more pain: for the former things are passed away.

In simpler words

And he will put an end to all their weeping; and there will be no more death, or sorrow, or crying, or pain; for the first things have come to an end.

What was going on

Christ returns; new heaven and new earth; the river of life; “Even so, come, Lord Jesus.”.

What it means

One day God will wipe away every tear; no more death, grief, or pain.

Today’s tears are real. This is the horizon Scripture holds out.

When it hits hardest

  • When memories surface again — Psalm 34:18 needs no performance; sit with one verse.
  • Milestones — dating, marriage, parenting your own children; Isaiah 43:1 for identity when the past whispers.
  • Nights that will not quiet — Journal: when trust was broken or the When it's hard.
  • When a friend asks how to help — share this page or one verse on a card; no sermon required.

Quiet cautions

  • Do not rush forgiveness of the abuser. That is the Lord’s work in His time, often with professional help.
  • Avoid Romans 8:28 here—survivors often hear it as “your pain served a purpose,” which can wound.
  • Healing verses are not deadlines. “Over it by now” is not Scripture’s voice.
  • If you or a child are in danger now, reach local child protective services, law enforcement, or a Child Advocacy Center—not only this page.

A Gentle Word

Psalm 34:18. He does not ask you to pull it together. He draws nigh. That is enough for today.
