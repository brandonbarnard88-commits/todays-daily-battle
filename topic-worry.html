Key Verses

Matthew 6:34

KJV

Take therefore no thought for the morrow: for the morrow shall take thought for the things of itself. Sufficient unto the day is the evil thereof.

In simpler words

Then have no care for tomorrow: tomorrow will take care of itself. Take the trouble of the day as it comes.

What was going on

Jesus said this to His disciples who worry about tomorrow: Do not be anxious.

What it means

Focus on today; don't borrow trouble from tomorrow.

Philippians 4:6

KJV

Be careful for nothing; but in every thing by prayer and supplication with thanksgiving let your requests be made known unto God.

In simpler words

Have no cares; but in everything with prayer and praise put your requests before God.

What was going on

Paul writes from prison to Philippi: rejoice, do not be anxious, the peace of God guards hearts; contentment in every state through Christ.

What it means

Don't let worry take over—pray and thank God, and tell Him what you need.

Psalm 55:22

KJV

Cast thy burden upon the Lord, and he shall sustain thee: he shall never suffer the righteous to be moved.

What was going on

David is crushed by a friend’s treachery and casts his burden on the Lord, who will sustain him.

What it means

Cast your cares on the Lord and He will sustain you.
