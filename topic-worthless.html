Key Verses

Genesis 1:27

KJV

So God created man in his own image.

In simpler words

And God made man in his image, in the image of God he made him: male and female he made them.

What was going on

God is creating the heavens and the earth from nothing — speaking light, order, and life into being.

What it means

God created mankind in His own image—you have dignity and worth.

Psalm 139:14

KJV

I am fearfully and wonderfully made.

What was going on

David marvels that God searches him, knows him fully, formed him in the womb, and leads him in the everlasting way.

What it means

You were formed on purpose. Your life is not an accident.

Isaiah 43:4

KJV

Since thou wast precious in my sight, thou hast been honourable.

In simpler words

Because of your value in my eyes, you have been honoured, and loved by me; so I will give men for you, and peoples for your life.

What was going on

Comfort for exiles: God is incomparable; idols are nothing; a servant will bring justice.

What it means

You are precious to God — not leftover, not extra. He names your worth.
