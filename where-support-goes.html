Where support goes

Support is optional. The goal stays the same: keep calm, Scripture-first help available for anyone who needs it.

Give securely How support works

At a glance

Built solo by Brandon. Human-curated. Privacy-first. KJV only.

100% of support stays with hosting, security, new plans, free printables for hospitals/prisons/new believers, and keeping the quiet place open. No salaries.

It is all boring but necessary work so the site remains calm, reliable, and available when someone needs one verse in a hard moment.

Current support priorities

  • Hosting and CDN so pages load quickly and stay online.
  • Security hardening, dependency maintenance, and abuse controls.
  • KJV data upkeep, search index refreshes, and offline reliability.
  • New Bible Study tracks, Battle Plans, and family printables reviewed by hand.
  • Feature maintenance for plans, prayer surfaces, and family tools.
  • Accessibility fixes and quality checks across mobile and desktop.

What support is not used for

  • No ad network spending.
  • No data brokerage.
  • No selling prayer text or private notes.
  • No paywall on the basic daily Scripture path.

Questions: support email or contact form (safety/moderation).
